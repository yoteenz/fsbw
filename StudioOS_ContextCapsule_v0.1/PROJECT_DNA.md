# Project DNA — Studio OS Philosophy

**Capsule:** StudioOS_ContextCapsule_v0.1 · v0.1.0  
**Scope:** The **why** — not implementation details.

---

## Why Studio OS exists

Commerce software treats businesses as accounts and features. Studio OS treats a business as a **place you inhabit** — a headquarters that grows districts, hires digital staff, and remembers every decision.

The founder is not configuring software. They are **building a civilization** where creative work has an address.

## Core beliefs

| Belief | Implication |
|--------|-------------|
| Place over menu | Navigation asks "Where would you like to go?" |
| Canon over convenience | Temporary hacks need explicit expiry |
| Forensic over retry | Failures must be visible and proven |
| Mobile over desktop | Real phone verification is default |
| Governance over generation | Material assets require authorization |
| Memory over repetition | Context must persist across AI sessions |

## What Studio OS is not

- Not a generic admin dashboard
- Not a feature subscription marketplace
- Not a chatbot bolted onto CRUD
- Not a single-brand website — it is a **platform** with organizations

## Relationship to Frontal Slayer

Frontal Slayer (Build-a-Wig) is the **host deployment** and first live organization. Studio OS wraps it — the storefront and the headquarters coexist.

## Relationship to Studio World

Studio World is the **spatial projection** of the OS — Atlas, Knowledge Graph, Civilization Layer. Users should feel they travel, not click.

## Relationship to Genesis

Genesis is the **constitutional DNA** — company genome, experience runtime, organizational memory. It is law, not lore.

## Relationship to Institute

Institute is learning woven through the city — not an isolated LMS. Professors live in places; lessons connect via Knowledge Graph.

## Long-horizon intent

Studio OS should still make sense in ten years — expansion via infill districts, not redesign. Every new capability answers: **Where does this live?**

## AI collaboration DNA

The founder should never manually re-explain the entire project to a new AI. The **AI Context Capsule™** is the portable operating system for that continuity — one file, one upload, immediate collaboration.
