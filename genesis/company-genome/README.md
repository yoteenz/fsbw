# Company Genome™ content home

**Canonical article:** [`../articles/COMPANY_GENOME.md`](../articles/COMPANY_GENOME.md)  
**Compiled guide:** [`../../docs/studio-os/genesis/COMPANY_GENOME.md`](../../docs/studio-os/genesis/COMPANY_GENOME.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional architecture proposal for Genesis review

Company Genome™ is the canonical living organizational model for a Studio OS
company.

It owns organizational meaning and learning through:

- Organizational Identity
- Business Architecture™
- Operating Model™
- Governance & Constraint Model™
- Organization Graph™
- Organizational Learning Ledger™

It does not replace specialized owners of doctrine, execution, reasoning,
brand strategy, experience expression, runtime state, evidence, or
transactions.

The implementation of this constitutional specification is blocked pending
Genesis review of its object relationships, versioning, and activation gates.
