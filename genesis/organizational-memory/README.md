**Canonical article:** [`../articles/ORGANIZATIONAL_MEMORY.md`](../articles/ORGANIZATIONAL_MEMORY.md)  
**Compiled guide:** [`../../docs/studio-os/genesis/ORGANIZATIONAL_MEMORY.md`](../../docs/studio-os/genesis/ORGANIZATIONAL_MEMORY.md)

Organizational Memory™ governs how Studio OS retains and recalls accumulated
organizational experience without introducing a second company truth store.

It is a capability of the Company Genome™ Organizational Learning Ledger™,
realized with Institute of Knowledge™ evidence, native outcomes, and Studio
Intelligence™ synthesis.

It distinguishes facts, evidence, knowledge, experience, patterns, forecasts,
and organizational wisdom; requires scope/provenance/revalidation; and forbids
historical learning from automatically changing doctrine, workflows, or
experience behavior.

No runtime implementation is authorized until the Ledger, evidence, recall,
retention, and consumer contracts complete Genesis review.
