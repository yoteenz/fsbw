This audit tests whether Studio OS has every durable constitutional
responsibility necessary for thirty-year universal organizational operation.

It rejects feature-level additions and finds Genesis v1 incomplete until it
closes responsibility for external obligations/entity governance, independent
assurance/safety/resilience, domain record stewardship, data/integration/time
integrity, and constitutional due process/continuity.

After those responsibilities are ratified, future domains must attach to the
defined slots; no new peer constitutional system may be created.
