# Narrative Intelligence™

**Project:** Studio OS  
**System:** Narrative Intelligence™ · Production Genome™  
**Status:** Canonical architecture draft for Genesis review  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Authority:** Genesis.md  
**Parent:** Studio Intelligence Layer™ · Experience Engine™ · Experience Runtime™  
**Depends on:** Studio Intelligence Layer™, Brand Discovery Engine™, Experience Runtime™, Experience Engine™, Company Genome™, Audience DNA™, Product DNA™, Creative Genome™, Taste Genome™, Canon Engine™, Orb™, Executive Headquarters™, Creative Direction Studio™

---

## 0. Prime directive

```text
Creative Direction Studio™ should not generate videos.
Creative Direction Studio™ should generate experiences.
Every piece of content must first become a narrative.
```

Narrative Intelligence™ is the layer that turns company truth, audience desire,
brand meaning, product promise, and founder taste into an experience arc before
any image, script, video, campaign, page, product scene, package, launch, or
asset is produced.

It is not a content generator.

It is the experience-design intelligence that defines:

- what the audience should feel
- what they should understand
- what proof they should encounter
- what sequence of moments should unfold
- what transformation the experience should create
- what production system should execute the experience

---

## 1. Why this layer exists

Studio Intelligence Layer™ understands the company.
Brand Discovery Engine™ understands the brand.
Experience Engine™ defines expressive DNA.
Experience Runtime™ executes assembled environments.

But none of those layers, by themselves, answer:

```text
What is the story this experience must make someone live through?
```

Narrative Intelligence™ answers that question.

It prevents Studio OS from treating creative work as output formats:

- video
- reel
- campaign
- email
- landing page
- product shoot
- launch asset
- social post
- package
- ad

Those are production containers.

The canonical object is the **Narrative Experience™**.

---

## 2. Anti-patterns

Narrative Intelligence™ must never become:

- a script generator
- a video generator
- a caption generator
- a content calendar
- a prompt wrapper
- a marketing copy tool
- a story template picker
- a moodboard with labels
- a campaign idea list
- a generic hero's journey machine
- a system that creates assets before understanding the experience

Production begins only after the narrative experience is resolved.

---

## 3. Core distinction

| Layer | Owns | Does not own |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Studio Intelligence Layer™ | executive reasoning across company, decisions, taste, audience, product, canon | experience sequence design |
| Brand Discovery Engine™ | strategic Brand DNA™ and brand intelligence | production sequencing |
| Experience Engine™ | brand/department/scene/component/motion/interaction DNA | narrative intent |
| Experience Runtime™ | runtime graph assembly and state continuity | story meaning |
| Narrative Intelligence™ | experiential narrative, emotional journey, proof sequence, production genome | rendering or media generation |
| Production systems | assets, edits, pages, video, packaging, campaigns | narrative authority |

---

## 4. Narrative Intelligence hierarchy

The permanent hierarchy is:

```text
Narrative Intelligence™
  -> Narrative Genome™
    -> Production Genome™
      -> Experience Arc™
        -> Moment Map™
          -> Proof System™
            -> Emotional Journey™
              -> Production Brief™
                -> Runtime / Production Execution™
```

### 4.1 Narrative Genome™

Narrative Genome™ is the reusable story identity for a company, product,
campaign, project, launch, or content world.

It includes:

- central truth
- audience transformation
- tension
- stakes
- desire
- belief shift
- emotional promise
- proof required
- world/setting logic
- recurring symbols
- transformation language
- founder point of view
- brand point of view
- product role
- customer role
- before/after state
- canonical anti-stories

### 4.2 Production Genome™

Production Genome™ is the execution genome for turning Narrative Genome™ into
experiential production without reducing it to a format.

It includes:

- format-agnostic production intent
- required moments
- scene roles
- pacing logic
- sensory direction
- proof captures
- product appearances
- talent/founder/customer roles
- environment requirements
- brand atmosphere
- motion rules
- camera posture
- edit rhythm
- sound posture
- copy posture
- asset derivation map
- channel adaptations
- quality gates

Production Genome™ is not a shot list.

It is the reusable production DNA that can create many shots, scenes, assets,
pages, packages, emails, ads, and spatial experiences from one narrative truth.

## 5. Experience Arc™

Every narrative experience must resolve an Experience Arc™ before production.

The default arc model is:

```text
1. Arrival
2. Recognition
3. Tension
4. Desire
5. Proof
6. Transformation
7. Invitation
8. Memory
```

This is not a fixed storytelling template. It is an experiential sequence.

### 5.1 Arc responsibilities

| Arc stage | Experience question |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Arrival | Where am I emotionally and visually? |
| Recognition | What truth do I recognize about myself, the brand, or the problem? |
| Tension | What is incomplete, frustrating, aspirational, risky, or desired? |
| Desire | What future state becomes attractive? |
| Proof | What makes the promise believable? |
| Transformation | What changes in perception, identity, confidence, or action? |
| Invitation | What should I do, choose, feel, save, share, buy, book, or remember? |
| Memory | What visual, phrase, ritual, object, or feeling stays with me? |

---

## 6. Moment Map™

Moment Map™ is the ordered graph of experiential moments.

Moments are not scenes.
Moments are meaningful units of experience that can later become scenes,
shots, cards, panels, sections, room states, package moments, launch assets, or
Orb-led interactions.

Each Moment™ includes:

- moment id
- arc stage
- emotional job
- audience state
- brand truth
- product/proof role
- sensory direction
- required evidence
- production notes
- possible formats
- runtime slot
- success signal

---

## 7. Proof System™

Proof System™ makes narrative believable.

A beautiful experience without proof is atmosphere only.
Proof turns atmosphere into trust.

Proof types include:

- founder proof
- product proof
- process proof
- customer proof
- transformation proof
- material proof
- behind-the-scenes proof
- comparison proof
- ritual proof
- cultural proof
- expertise proof
- data proof
- scarcity/provenance proof

Every Narrative Experience™ must define what proof is required and where it
appears in the arc.

---

## 8. Emotional Journey™

Emotional Journey™ resolves the intended emotional progression.

Examples:

```text
overwhelmed -> seen -> guided -> confident -> committed
curious -> immersed -> impressed -> trusting -> desiring
skeptical -> grounded -> convinced -> excited -> ready
ordinary -> elevated -> transformed -> remembered
```

Emotion must be specific enough to guide production decisions.

Generic goals like "premium", "fun", "engaging", or "viral" are not sufficient.

## 10. Production Brief™

Production Brief™ is the governed handoff from Narrative Intelligence™ to
production systems.

It is format-aware but not format-owned.

A Production Brief™ includes:

- narrative genome id
- production genome id
- experience arc
- moment map
- proof requirements
- emotional journey
- audience state
- brand DNA references
- product DNA references
- taste genome constraints
- creative genome references
- runtime scene slots
- required assets
- possible format adaptations
- quality gates
- anti-patterns
- canonization path

Production systems may not bypass this brief for meaningful creative work.

---

## 11. Creative Direction Studio™ role

Creative Direction Studio™ becomes the place where narrative experiences are
designed, not where assets are generated first.

It should help founders answer:

- What experience are we creating?
- What should the audience feel before, during, and after?
- What truth is the experience built around?
- What proof makes the story credible?
- What moments are required?
- What should never happen?
- How does this become a production genome?
- Which channels are adaptations, not origins?

The Studio Orb can act as Narrative Director™, but founder approval is required
before canonization.

---

## 12. Relationship to Production Genome™

Production Genome™ converts narrative into repeatable production intelligence.

It should feed:

- Creative Direction Studio™
- Studio Foundry™
- Content Engine™
- Product Photography Engine™
- Packaging Intelligence™
- Campaign Engine™
- Launch Engine™
- Experience Runtime™
- Executive Headquarters™ briefings
- Orb recommendations
- Marketplace templates

Production Genome™ is reusable. A single genome can produce:

- product shoot direction
- launch campaign
- email flow
- landing page
- package insert
- social series
- founder video
- Orb briefing
- HQ scene
- academy lesson
- marketplace listing

The genome remains stable while formats adapt.

---

## 13. Runtime relationship

Experience Runtime™ receives narrative as resolved context.

It should not invent narrative.

Runtime assembly may consume:

- active narrative genome
- moment map
- emotional journey
- proof requirements
- scene slot bindings
- current audience state
- current mission/project state

Runtime output should express the narrative through scene graph placement,
motion pacing, proof panels, Orb language, focal objects, transitions, and
interaction prompts.

---

## 14. Canonization

Narrative objects can become canon.

Canon classes:

- Company Narrative Canon™
- Brand Narrative Canon™
- Product Narrative Canon™
- Campaign Narrative Canon™
- Production Genome Canon™
- Proof Canon™
- Storyworld Canon™
- Rejected Narrative / Anti-Story™

Nothing becomes canon automatically.

Founder approval and Canon Engine™ review are required.

---

## 15. Governance rules

1. Meaningful creative work starts with Narrative Experience™, not media format.
2. Content is an output container; narrative is the source object.
3. Creative Direction Studio™ designs experiences before production.
4. Production Genome™ must be reusable across formats and channels.
5. Every Narrative Experience™ must define an Experience Arc™, Moment Map™,
   Proof System™, Emotional Journey™, and Production Brief™.
6. Narrative Intelligence™ consults Studio Intelligence Layer™, Brand DNA™,
   Audience DNA™, Product DNA™, Taste Genome™, Creative Genome™, and Canon
   Engine™ before proposing narrative.
7. Experience Runtime™ may express narrative, but may not invent or canonize it.
8. Production systems may adapt a Production Genome™, but may not silently
   mutate canonical narrative truth.
9. Rejected narratives and anti-stories are valuable memory and must be tracked.
10. Founder approval is required before a Narrative Genome™ or Production
    Genome™ becomes canonical.

---

## 16. Success criteria

Narrative Intelligence™ succeeds when:

- Creative Direction Studio™ stops thinking in formats first.
- Every asset originates from a resolved experience arc.
- Production teams can adapt one Production Genome™ across many channels.
- Orb can explain why a creative direction is right for the company.
- Executive Headquarters™ can brief the founder on narrative strategy, not
  content volume.
- Experience Runtime™ can render scenes with story intent and proof placement.
- Brand consistency improves because narrative truth is reusable.
- Production becomes faster without becoming generic.

Studio OS does not create content first.
Studio OS designs narrative experiences, compiles Production Genomes, and then
