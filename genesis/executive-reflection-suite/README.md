The Executive Reflection Suite™ is Studio OS's complete reflective operating environment.

It expands The Evolution Room™ into a full Headquarters wing where founders continuously improve themselves, their companies, Studio OS, and Genesis.

## Prime doctrine

```text
Reflection is an operating environment, not a report.
```

## Canon rule

Nothing becomes canon automatically.

The Suite may generate Genesis Improvement Proposals™, Boardroom packets, Decision Timeline entries, and Lessons Learned artifacts. Founder review remains required for canon or permanent company doctrine.
