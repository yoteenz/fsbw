This amendment rejects Business DNA™ and Operations DNA™ as new peer engines.

It establishes the correct ownership model:

- **Company Genome™** owns Business Architecture™, Operating Model™, and the
  learned Operational DNA™ preference strand.
- **Company Operating Manual™** owns doctrine, SOPs, approvals, and escalation.
- **Workflow Engine™** owns executable processes.
- **Strategic Brand DNA™** consumes business constraints through a versioned
  brief.
- **Experience Department DNA™** remains visual/environmental inheritance only.
- **Department Operating Charter™** is the separate non-visual operating model.

No implementation is authorized until Company Genome™ has a first-class Genesis
article and the amendment has completed constitutional review.
