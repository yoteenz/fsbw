# Brand Discovery Engine™ · Brand DNA™ · Brand Intelligence Layer™

**Canonical article:** [`../articles/BRAND_DISCOVERY_ENGINE.md`](../articles/BRAND_DISCOVERY_ENGINE.md)  
**Status:** Architecture drafted; runtime implementation planned  
**Purpose:** Discover, define, protect, elevate, and apply canonical strategic Brand DNA™ across every Studio OS creative and strategic system.

---

## Systems

| System | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Brand Discovery Engine™** | Discover brand from founder interview, Genome, assets, market, and channel evidence |
| **Brand DNA™ (Strategic)** | Canonical living strategic identity genome |
| **Brand Intelligence Layer™** | Reusable brand reasoning consumed by every Studio OS system |
| **Brand Elevation Engine™** | Evaluate brand health and propose DNA upgrades |
| **Brand Consistency Check™** | Score generated assets against Brand DNA before shipping |
| **Orb Brand Strategist Mode™** | Orb-led discovery, challenge, protection, and explanation |

---

## Relationship to Experience Engine

Strategic Brand DNA™ compiles into **Experience Brand DNA™** (expression layer) consumed by Experience Engine™ and Experience Runtime™.

Do not redesign Experience Engine or Runtime — expand Brand DNA upward into strategic intelligence.

```text
Brand Discovery Engine™
  -> Strategic Brand DNA™
    -> Brand Intelligence Layer™
      -> Experience Brand DNA™
      -> Packaging · Marketing · Foundry · Orb · Academy · ...
```
