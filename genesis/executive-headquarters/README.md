Executive Headquarters™ is Studio OS's flagship founder experience: the place where founders think, plan, build, decide, create, launch, learn, and operate their companies.

It is **not** an admin dashboard.

| Room | Role |

| Founder Office™ | Strategic deep work |
| Mission Control™ | Operational runway |

| Department Directory™ | Company room map |
| Knowledge Wing™ | Source-backed operating knowledge |

## Future expansion

Finance Headquarters™, Research Wing™, Automation Lab™, full Meeting Rooms™, Expansion Wings™, Studio Exchange Room, Career Worlds Room, Simulation Room.

Headquarters owns **experience composition**. It does not own company, mission, permission, command, knowledge, or analytics truth.
