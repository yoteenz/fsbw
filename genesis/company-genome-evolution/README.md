Company Genome Evolution Lifecycle™ governs how a Company Genome™ learns,
revises, projects, activates, restores, and resolves contradictions.

It is a lifecycle capability of Company Genome™, not a new peer engine.

It establishes:

- proportional Evolution Gates™
- immutable versioned revisions
- Constitutional Diffs™
- Projection Impact Plans™
- selective consumer regeneration
- forward-only restoration
- domain-specific conflict authority

Consumer implementations must pin Genome revision, snapshot hash, provenance,
and staleness policy. No runtime implementation is authorized until the
constitutional lifecycle objects and relationship rules have completed Genesis
review.
