The Evolution Room™ is where Studio OS reflects upon itself with the founder.

It is not analytics.

It is not reporting.

It is a monthly Executive Board Room experience where the founder and Studio OS review what changed, what was learned, what should evolve, and what should become legacy.

## Core experiences

| Experience | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Executive Evolution Brief™** | Orb's monthly prepared strategic brief |
| **Monthly Review** | Founder/company/platform/Genesis operating review |
| **Genesis Review** | Evidence-backed proposals, disproven assumptions, constitutional ideas |
| **Evolution Council™** | Orb-facilitated executive conversation and decision drafting |
| **Future Wall™** | Next priorities, rooms, automations, brains, products, revenue opportunities |
| **Legacy Wall™** | Permanent timeline of launches, milestones, breakthroughs, Genesis/platform evolution |

## Prime rule

```text
Nothing becomes canon automatically.
```

The room drafts decisions and Genesis proposals. The founder reviews and decides.
