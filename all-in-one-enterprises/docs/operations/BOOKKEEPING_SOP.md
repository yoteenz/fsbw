# Bookkeeping SOP (Operations)

## Lead → assessment

1. Customer completes public assessment or staff logs intake.
2. System runs `recommendBookkeepingPlan()`.
3. Lead status: `assessment_complete` or `pricing_review` if custom review required.

## Pricing review

- Staff confirms complexity profile and final price before onboarding.
- Never show $0 or unlimited flat rate without approval.

## Books Rescue

1. Assessment → months behind, accounts, software, documents.
2. Staff quote (starting at $749).
3. Payment/approval → cleanup work → mark complete.
4. Recommend recurring tier for after cleanup.

## Onboarding

Collect: business info, accounting software, account list, factoring/driver structure, documents via Document Vault. **Never** collect bank or QBO passwords in messages/forms.

## Monthly cycle

`period_open` → documents requested → customer response → categorize → reconcile → staff review → questions → reports prepared → delivered → `period_complete`.

## Reports

Label period, plan, prepared-for, disclaimer (not audited/certified). Deliver via secure portal links.

## Pause / cancellation

Staff records reason; complete open period work per configured policy.

## Escalation

Pricing disputes → bookkeeping pricing permission. Sensitive financial access → `bookkeeping:financial-sensitive`.
