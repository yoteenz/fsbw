IFTA Filing Room
composition review

The first build was mounting the new IFTA experience on the legacy AIO portal, office and service-page shells. Per your override that stopped before any visual code was written. This page audits what was leaking in, keeps the working function, and proposes a new public, client and founder/staff page family composed from the Experience Brain and the AIO IDNTY record. Nothing below is implemented. It waits for your decisions at the end.

Stopped
  • Legacy shells as hosts (portal sidebar, office layout, public service template)
  • Legacy tokens, one global caps transform over records and inputs, full lockup in every nav, card geometry, 767px split
  • Reuse of the hero truck photo and the cropped IFTA icon
Kept (function only)
  • Brain contracts vendored from SITE00 @ 5d6c074, hash-pinned

1 · Audit

41 legacy dependencies, separated into function and visuals

Every dependency touching the IFTA public, client and staff surfaces is labelled in AIO_IFTA_LEGACY_VISUAL_DEPENDENCY_AUDIT.json: 13 functional, 13 mixed, 12 visual, 2 placeholder copy, 1 environment tool. Mixed items are split; their function is kept and their visuals discarded.

Shells that run logic

The portal layout triggers runExpirationEvaluation() and runBillingEvaluation(); the office layout sets the acting staff member. The new shells keep these, so dropping the old visuals cannot break the product.

Content truth bugs found

Road Ready and Start Your Business link IFTA to slug ifta-setup, which does not exist. The Vault labels tax_fuel as "Permits". Both are fixed in implementation, not hidden by design.

One privacy fix made

My Office activity showed customer-visible events from every client. It is now scoped to the signed-in organization. This is a data fix, not a visual change.

2 · Visual authority

What actually decides how this looks

One brand foundation is locked: your forensic sprint fixed the brand lines, uppercase-primary typography, a monogram-only nav, and the palette names. Beyond that AIO has no approved visual skin: the brand-family registry lists it as NEW_SKIN_TO_DESIGN with visual authority NOT_STARTED, and IDNTY leaves mark, palette hexes and typefaces in progress. This family follows the lock exactly, and everything still open is a decision for you, not something borrowed from the old app.

19 · Your decisions

Fourteen decisions before implementation

Pick an answer for each and copy them back into the chat. Your picks are remembered in this browser only.

D-01Family shell

Give the IFTA family its own slim shell (road-office bar for clients, operations bar for staff) on the existing routes, instead of the legacy portal sidebar and office layout. Legacy pages for other families stay as they are.

D-02Public chrome

On /services/ifta-filing, use a family-scoped bar (wordmark · Services · Log in · Request filing) and a one-line footer instead of the legacy nav, plan bar and footer.

D-03Palette hex values

The names are locked; the values are not. Proposed: obsidian #121417 · charcoal #1D2024 · stone white #EDE9E1 · platinum / silver #C3C8CD · warm champagne #E2CFA0 · signature gold #C9A227 (SITE00 brand-family registry; the legacy app used #D4A017), plus black #0B0B0C and a muted stone #A39D93 from the Brain.

D-04IFTA availability on the public page

The public page says GO / Get Started; the catalog says PREPARING and fuel tax is INTERNAL_ONLY. Recommendation: REQUEST FILING opens a quote request, with the honest line "AIO staff file with your base jurisdiction".

D-05Sentence case inside records

Uppercase primary is locked and stays on every UI element. Approve one exception: sentence case inside paper records and message threads (receipt lines, return-summary lines, AIO and client messages), for document legibility. Typefaces stay as they are until the IDNTY type work.

D-06Monogram source

The lock says nav uses the simple monogram only, but no standalone monogram file exists. Crop the existing mark from the current lockup (no generation) until the IDNTY mark work delivers one.

D-07Imagery

No photography in this family. Retire the hero truck photo (IDNTY prohibits generic semi hero shots). Keep one optional fuel-island-at-night slot marked GENERATION_REQUIRED; nothing generated.

D-08Objects inside legacy hosts

The quarter card (My Office) and the correction slip (Inbox) are family-designed objects mounted in the legacy hosts. The Vault handoff gets a family route: /portal/vault/tax-fuel/ifta.

D-09Brain update: Under AIO review

Add UNDER_AIO_REVIEW (sprint §6) to the IFTA receipt classes in the Experience Brain.

D-10Brain update: activity events

Add mileage imported, review started, correction requested, payment recorded and vault package created (sprint §19) to the contract activity events.

D-11Brain update: staff buckets

Split the contract COMPLETE bucket into FILED and PAYMENT PENDING (sprint §13).

D-12Demo anchoring

Pioneer Fleet is the primary enrolled quarter. Summit Ridge, the default demo org, is a new owner-operator with no IFTA account, so it shows the not-set-up threshold with a demo link to Pioneer.

D-13Office work items

Create an Office work item per client-quarter so IFTA cases appear in My Work, Queues and Approvals too.

D-14Approval button wording

The sprint says APPROVE FOR FILING; the contract says APPROVE RETURN.
