Customer ↔ admin links

  • Order detail (customer) ↔ Order detail (admin): one order record, one status history
  • Reviews & content ↔ Approvals: submission status and decline reason
  • Concierge messages ↔ Inbox: replies appear in both
  • Rewards wallet ↔ Membership & rewards console: one ledger
  • My Hair analyses ↔ Rewards › Hair analysis credits
  • Saved builds ↔ Catalog › Build-A-Wig options and pricing

Shared data that unlocks the most

  • Server order of record: 14 pages (customer and admin)
  • Rewards ledgers: points, wallet, vouchers, codes: 10 pages
  • Shared shells + one account guard: every page, no visual change
  • Mock-data quarantine: honest numbers on 11 pages
  • Content submissions + consent: Slay Cam, reviews, affiliate

Before any page work

  • Revoke public access to the credit and usage database functions
  • Stop Slay Tickets and premium being granted without payment
  • Stop custom wigs being charged at base price
  • Stop Concierge adding test orders to real customers
  • Make "cancel subscription" cancel in Stripe
