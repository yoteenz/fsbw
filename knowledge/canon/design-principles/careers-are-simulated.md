# Careers Are Simulated™

**Design Principle #10 · ARTICLE-E01**

Studio World learning should feel like entering a profession on Day One, not taking a traditional course.

## Meaning

Professional learning should be embodied inside a living workplace:

- scenes,
- jobs,
- shifts,
- clients,
- challenges,
- projects,
- promotions.

The learner progresses by performing the profession, building reputation, recovering from mistakes, and earning promotion evidence.

## Decision guide

When building professional education, prefer:

- workplace scenes over lessons,
- jobs over modules,
- shifts over videos,
- client challenges over quizzes,
- projects over assignments,
- promotions over course completion.

If an experience can be described as “Lesson 3” without losing meaning, it has failed this principle.
