# Architecture Decision Records™

**Article:** K21  
**Layer:** Constitutional Law™  
**Status:** Accepted  

---

## Law

Major architectural decisions must preserve their reasoning as Architecture Decision Records™.

An ADR records why a decision exists, what alternatives were rejected, what tradeoffs were accepted, what systems were affected, and how the decision shaped Studio World.

---

## Enforcement

- Flagship architectural decisions generate ADR drafts automatically.
- Accepted ADRs become preserved exhibits in Constitution Hall™.
- Every accepted ADR becomes an `architectural-decision` node in the World Graph™.
- ADRs are never deleted; new decisions supersede old decisions while history remains intact.
- The Architect's Oath™ must be answerable before approval.
