# ASTRA Creative Target — World-First Entry

---

## Product class

**Astral World is a WORLD product** — a living digital place. The entry screen is **not** a marketing homepage, SaaS landing page, or admin dashboard.

---

## Desired interaction class

Behave like:

- **Video-game title screen**
- **World lobby**
- **Immersive game entry environment**
- **Spatial menu** at the threshold of a district

**Not** like:

- Hero + feature cards
- Stacked content sections
- Ecommerce landing
- Dashboard widgets floating on wallpaper

---

## Desired sensation (founder benchmark)

> "I am standing at the edge of **Astréa** and choosing how to enter."

The user should feel **about to enter** a inhabited world — atmosphere, depth, and entry verbs — not **browsing pages about** a service.

---

## World-entry verbs to choreograph (one screen)

1. **Enter Astréa** (district threshold)
2. **Choose a destination** (Suite / Mall / Coffee Shop) — spatially, not as generic cards
3. **Who's Here** — world feels populated
4. **Take Me Somewhere** — guided intent routing
5. **Find My Reader** — relationship to readers in-world

Meet My Friends may be secondary on entry (reference desktop had explicit band; current D01 uses Who's Here → Friends).
