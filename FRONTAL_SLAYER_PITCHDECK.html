CORE FEATURES

Build-A-Wig Customizer
Advanced interactive customization tool allowing clients to configure every aspect of their wig: cap size, length, density, lace type, texture, color, hairline, styling, and add-ons. Real-time price calculation and visual preview.
Membership Tiers
Three-tier loyalty system: Base state shows "MEMBER" until $500 spending, then unlocks SILVER tier. RED tier at $2,000+, BLACK tier at $4,000+. Plus PREMIUM subscription. Unlock exclusive benefits, concierge services, and priority support based on spending thresholds.
Loyalty Points System
Earn points on every purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts and rewards. Track your tier progression and unlock exclusive member benefits.
Affiliate Program
Submit photos, videos, and social media content to earn up to 5,000 loyalty points per order. Content submission system with approval workflow and period-based point tracking.
Concierge Service
Exclusive priority messaging and order tracking for PREMIUM members and BLACK tier customers. Direct communication channel with urgent request handling and order-specific support.
Advanced Order Tracking
8-stage order tracking system: Confirmation → Sourcing → Constructing → Shipped to Hub → Arrived at Hub → Prep → Customize → Finalize → Shipped. Real-time progress updates.
