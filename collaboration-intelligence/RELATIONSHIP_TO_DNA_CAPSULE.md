# Relationship to Studio DNA Capsule

The **Studio DNA Capsule™** encodes **how Studio OS should think** — taste, quality bar, canon preservation.

The **Collaboration Intelligence Capsule™** encodes **how the Founder and AI discovered that DNA** through iteration.

## Division of labor

| Studio DNA | Collaboration Intelligence |
|------------|---------------------------|
| `QUALITY_STANDARDS.md` | `AI_LESSONS.md` (mistakes that informed standards) |
| `CANON_PRESERVATION_POLICY.md` | Admin Alignment Protocol, Frontal Slayer protected pages |
| `AI_COLLABORATION_DNA.md` | `COLLABORATION_PATTERNS.md`, `FOUNDER_PREFERENCES.md` |
| `EVOLUTION.md` | `EVOLUTION_TIMELINE.md` |

## When DNA is absent from onboarding pack

Collaboration Intelligence remains **required**. DNA absence is not a validation failure. Apply taste judgments from Founder Intelligence + Collaboration patterns until DNA is read separately.
