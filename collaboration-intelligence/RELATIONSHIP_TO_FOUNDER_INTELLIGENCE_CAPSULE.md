# Relationship to Founder Intelligence Capsule

The **Founder Intelligence Capsule™** preserves **why Studio OS exists** — vision, business model, civilization layer.

The **Collaboration Intelligence Capsule™** preserves **how that vision was co-built** — debates, shorthand, rejections, and working patterns.

## Overlap (intentional)

| Founder Intelligence | Collaboration Intelligence |
|---------------------|--------------------------|
| `DECISION_HISTORY.md` (strategy) | `DECISION_HISTORY.md` (process + implementation gates) |
| `FOUNDER_PREFERENCES.md` (communication) | `FOUNDER_PREFERENCES.md` (sprint + tooling preferences) |
| `AI_COLLABORATION.md` (principles) | `COLLABORATION_PATTERNS.md` (operational workflow) |

**Rule:** Strategy canon lives in Founder Intelligence. **Collaboration mechanics and shorthand** live here. When both mention the same decision, Founder Intelligence states the *business/strategic why*; Collaboration Intelligence states the *how we got there in chat*.

## Examples

- **Marketplace:** Founder Intelligence describes civilization economy. Collaboration Intelligence tracks storefront → knowledge marketplace → civilization economy evolution.
- **Studio Workers:** Founder Intelligence describes Digital Payroll model. Collaboration Intelligence records Goosebump moment and naming debates.
