## PRE-PRODUCTION MISSION

We are no longer designing a website.

We are building the **Frontal Slayer Universe**.

From this point forward, think like an Academy Award-winning Production Designer, Architect, Creative Director, World Builder, and Systems Designer.

**Do NOT** think like a UI designer, web developer, or AI image generator.

Your job is to help create a **physical world** that can realistically support an entire cinematic franchise, an interactive desktop experience, a mobile experience, future campaigns, and future product launches.

This Production Bible will become the **single source of truth** for every future creative decision.

It will dictate:

- Architecture  
- Interior Design  
- Storytelling  
- Character Blocking  
- Camera Placement  
- Lighting  
- Materials  
- Environmental Continuity  
- Website Design  
- Mobile Experience  
- Desktop Mansion  
- Future Campaigns  
- Future Chapters  

Every future asset will **inherit** from this document.

**Consistency is more important than creativity.**

Do not invent new environments unless they **logically belong** inside the flagship.

Do not redesign rooms because a **new camera angle** exists.

Build **one flagship** that can be photographed from **unlimited camera positions**—like a permanent Hollywood movie set.

**Objective:** One environment that feels **physically real from every angle**.

Challenge assumptions. Identify continuity problems **before** they become production problems.

Never optimize for what is easiest to generate. Always optimize for **believable world**.

**Phase gate:** Do **not** begin designing architecture in this bible until Phase 1 is **Founder-approved** and Phase 2 is explicitly opened.

# PHASE 1 — THE STORY UNIVERSE

## PURPOSE

The Frontal Slayer universe is not a collection of unrelated marketing assets.

It is **one continuous world** experienced through multiple mediums.

Every campaign, website, mobile experience, animation, social post, and future chapter exists within the **same universe**.

Nothing is created independently.

Everything should feel like it belongs to **one physical location** that expands into **one digital destination**.

This document serves as the single source of truth for the Frontal Slayer **world layer** of the Production Bible.

---

## THE CORE PHILOSOPHY

The customer does not discover a website.

The customer discovers a **place**.

That place is the **Frontal Slayer Flagship**.

The physical flagship serves as the **foundation** of the entire brand universe.

The **Mansion** is its **digital twin**.

Every experience grows from the same architecture, design language, emotional tone, and story.

Nothing should ever feel disconnected.

---

## THE THREE LAYERS OF THE WORLD

The Frontal Slayer universe exists in **three connected realities**.

### Reality One — The Physical Flagship

The real-world luxury flagship boutique.

Everything feels **tangible**.

Natural sunlight. Real marble. Glass. Chrome. Crystal. Fresh roses. Luxury service. Human interaction.

This is where the **campaign** takes place.

This is where **Nia's story** begins.

### Reality Two — The Mobile Experience

The customer's first **personal** interaction with the flagship.

Like carrying a **refined extension** of the boutique in your pocket.

**Not** a miniature version of the desktop experience.

The customer's first step into the Frontal Slayer universe.

Purpose: **speed, accessibility, and elegance**.

### Reality Three — The Mansion

The **digital evolution** of the physical flagship.

**Not** a fantasy castle. **Not** a separate universe.

The flagship **expanded** beyond physical limitations.

Every Mansion room **originates** from a real room in the flagship.

Architecture, materials, proportions, lighting, and layout should always feel **familiar**.

The Mansion adds immersive technology, interactive systems, and futuristic experiences while preserving **emotional familiarity** from the flagship.

---

## CUSTOMER JOURNEY

```
Campaign → Curiosity → Mobile Experience → Trust → Desktop Mansion → Immersion → Purchase → Membership → Community → Loyalty
```

Every stage **validates** the one before it.

---

## ROLE OF EACH EXPERIENCE

**Campaign** — Creates emotional desire. The audience follows **Nia** discovering the Frontal Slayer Flagship. Introduces the world. **Never explains everything.**

**Mobile** — Removes friction. Immediate interaction after discovery. Elegant, welcoming, premium.

**Desktop Mansion** — Rewards curiosity. Customer becomes **protagonist**. They begin **their own** journey instead of only watching Nia's.

---

## EMOTIONAL ARC

```
Curiosity → Discovery → Comfort → Confidence → Transformation → Belonging → Mastery
```

Every customer interaction should reinforce this progression.

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Nothing exists because it looks impressive.

Everything exists because it **supports the story**.

Every room has a purpose. Every object has a purpose. Every architectural decision has a purpose. Every camera angle has a purpose.

The audience should never feel like they are moving between **separate marketing assets**.

They should feel like they are moving through **one continuous world**.

---

## GOLDEN RULE

Never ask: *"What should this page look like?"*

Always ask: *"Where does this exist inside the Frontal Slayer Flagship, and how does it evolve into its digital twin?"*

## 4. Structural improvements (Phase 1 text)

1. **Name the flagship** (working production title, e.g. “The Frontal Slayer Flagship” vs in-world address)—reduces doc ambiguity.  
2. **Explicitly subordinate** desktop Mansion to **curiosity earned**—Mobile validates trust **before** full Mansion immersion (matches “Rewards curiosity”).  
3. **Add “Reality bleed” rule:** Mansion may add **technology and depth**; it may **not** change **material vocabulary** or **host philosophy** without versioned bible update.  
4. **Clarify campaign scope:** Trilogy First Act = **one flagship threshold**; future chapters may use **same set**, different **time/blocking**—not new boutiques.  
5. **Cross-link Content Distribution:** Many platforms ≠ many worlds; **one set**, **many camera positions and perspectives** (aligns with North Star “many doors”).  
6. **Document mobile as primary entry for product** while Mansion remains **depth destination**—prevents teams building “full mansion on phone” scope creep.

# PHASE 2 — EXPERIENCE ARCHITECTURE

## PRE-PRODUCTION (Phase 2)

We are **still not** designing the building.

**Do not** generate architecture, floor plans, images, UI, or code.

**Role:** Production Designer · Experience Architect · World Builder · Hospitality Consultant · Creative Director.

**Objective:** Define **every experience** the flagship must support so architecture **emerges from experience**, never the reverse.

**Core principle:** Every **room** exists because it enables an **experience**. No room exists only to look impressive. If an experience does not exist, the room should not exist.

**Output:** **Experience Registry** — official program for Phase 3 architectural design.

---

## EXPERIENCE PROGRESSION (FLAGSHIP STORY)

Experiences are **not** isolated features. They form one **choreographed journey**:

```
Discovery → Welcome → Education → Confidence → Customization → Transformation
→ Celebration → Purchase → Membership → Community → Retention → Advocacy
```

**Campaign / Reality One** feeds **Discovery** before **Welcome**. **Mobile** carries **Welcome → Purchase** with low friction. **Mansion** deepens **Education → Community** and **rewards curiosity**.

**Season One note:** Early chapters emphasize **Discovery → Welcome → Education**; **Purchase** intensifies mid-season—registry supports **lifetime** journey, not launch-day funnel only.

---

## REGISTRY CONVENTIONS

| Field | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Access** | **Public** · **Private** (signed-in) · **Members** · **VIP/Black** · **Founder** · **Staff** |
| **Physical** | **Yes** = must exist in flagship set · **Partial** = physical analog or desk, not full room yet · **Future** = plan for build · **No** = digital/service only |
| **Mobile** | **Yes** / **Partial** / **No** |
| **Mansion** | **Yes** / **Partial** / **No** |
| **Twin class** | **Twin Strict** · **Twin Enhanced** · **Mansion Exclusive** |

**Twin Strict:** Must exist **physically and digitally** with matched identity.  
**Twin Enhanced:** Physical exists; Mansion **adds** depth, tools, or scale—not different materials.  
**Mansion Exclusive:** **No** physical room required; must **not** contradict flagship materials/tone.
