## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Building identity:** Luxury **gallery-hospitality** boutique (~16.5k SF guest ground + lower ops)—marble, glass, brushed metal; double-height **showroom** hero; hidden atelier/transform suites. Permanent **film set** and operating store.

**Customer journey:** Street wonder → threshold safety → reception recognition → showroom discovery → education/confidence → (invited) consult/analysis → atelier authorship → transformation → celebration → checkout → optional membership → **Mansion bridge** continuity.

**Design philosophy:** Destination before salon; **restraint**; every reveal earned; natural light leads by day; tech **in** architecture, not on top.

**Brand personality:** Calm precision, feminine hospitality (curve, roses, warmth—not pink cliché), editorial craft, **never** intimidating.

**Hospitality philosophy:** Guest **seen** at reception; hosts not stanchions; private zones by **invitation**; departure dignified.

**Digital twin:** **Strict** material identity on mobile/web/Mansion; **Enhanced** depth and tools; Mansion-only account/ops halls use **same** tone as reception/commerce—not separate SaaS aesthetic.

## 4. MATERIAL LOCK

From DOC-ENV-005. **No new materials** without Founder + ECD amendment.

### Permanent materials

| Material | Finish | Reflection | Permitted | Prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **White marble (hero)** | Polished (public), honed (atelier/work) | Medium specular | Floors, walls, reception, S2-GAL | Fake tile repeat, yellow vein overload |
| **Warm white plaster** | Matte | Low | Ceilings, most walls | Gray office paint |
| **Clear glass** | Frameless, AR when exterior | High controlled | Vitrine, partitions, rails | Green cheap glass |
| **Frosted / reeded glass** | Matte | Low | Private borders, orientation | Plastic film |
| **Brushed stainless / champagne metal** | Directional brush | Low–medium | Hardware, frames, legs | Mixed random metals |
| **Chrome (polished)** | Mirror accent | Very high | **Thin** trim only | Mirror walls |
| **Crystal / acrylic block** | Polished edge | Medium | Pedestals, UI depth refs | Sparkle/disco |
| **Matte leather** | Matte | Low | Seating | Pleather gloss |
| **Luxury wool / velvet** | Matte | None | Rugs, lounge, acoustic | Busy prints |
| **Light oak / walnut** | Oiled matte | Low | **Accent** furniture only | Rustic pine |
| **Architectural mirror** | Silver | High | **One** plane per suite max | Mirror mazes |
| **Fresh roses / sculptural greenery** | Natural | Low | S1-REC, key moments | Plastic flowers |

### Rules

- **Default triad:** marble plane + glass plane + **one** brushed metal line.  
- **Transparency:** glass over marble—never busy pattern stacks.  
- **One hero reflection** per composed frame (photography).  
- **TV Lounge (S3-TVL):** same material **tier**, matte **black** ceiling/walls—dark exception, not cheaper materials.

### Prohibited (always)

Cheap plastic gloss · vinyl wood · neon · full red rooms · fake printed marble · exposed acoustic tile grid · mall escalator aesthetic · gratuitous gold overload.

## 5. COLOR SYSTEM

| Role | Execution guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Architectural white / ivory** | Dominant field; warm, not blue-gray |
| **Champagne / warm gray** | Joints, secondary planes, meta |
| **Marble veining gray** | Natural only—do not paint faux veins |
| **Soft black** | S3-TVL envelope, screen fields |
| **Signature red** | `#EB1C24` — pulse only |

### Red usage

- **One red moment per sightline** (floral accent, single object, or digital CTA)—not all three.  
- Never red floors, walls, upholstery fields, or skin key light.  
- Dark zones: red **withdraws**.

### White balance

**Warm-neutral global** — hospitality bright (Visual Language). Grade skin kind; preserve marble highlight roll-off. No cheap green/magenta LED cast.

## 8. CONTINUITY RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. **Architecture is fixed** — massing, GS-1 axis, double-height S2-GAL, space IDs do not move.  
2. **Room relationships fixed** — adjacency/separation per DOC-ENV-006 (e.g. no checkout↔atelier direct).  
3. **Materials fixed** — Material Lock §4 only.  
4. **Lighting philosophy fixed** — §6; TV Lounge dark is **exception**, not new palette.  
5. **Technology integrates** — flush glass screens; no bolt-on kiosks in hero zones.  
6. **Evolve, don’t replace** — future wing/phasing adds capacity; **never** new brand language.  
7. **SET ID on every asset** — `SET-001:space-id` + Camera ID when applicable.  
8. **Campaign set = retail set** — same walls for Nia and daily trade.  
9. **Experiences unchanged** — Phase 2 registry; dossier does not add/remove experiences.  
10. **Unapproved = stop** — anything not in Phases 1–5 canon requires **Founder review** before production spend.

## 9. VISUALIZATION GUIDELINES

| Topic | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Photorealism target** | Believable luxury retail + film set — not fantasy palace |
| **Architectural realism** | Plausible structure, glass, stone weight; floating plinths with **shadow gap** |
| **Scale** | Human figures 5′6″–5′10″ reference; S2-GAL double-height ~18–20 ft clear |
| **Perspective** | Eye-level pedestrian for exterior; restrained wide only in S2-GAL |
| **Human presence** | Natural skin; diverse; kind light; staff calm, not aggressive sell |
| **Luxury restraint** | One focal hero; negative space; no clutter walls |
| **Brand atmosphere** | Hospitality-bright except S3-TVL; warm over cold |
| **Material accuracy** | Marble veining **subtle**; metal brush direction consistent |
| **Environmental consistency** | Same SET-001 in crop, wide, and 3D flythrough |

**Avoid:** AI hallucination (random columns, extra stairs, fantasy chandeliers), duplicate logos, mall signage, neon, fake grain, **new** room types, mirror mazes, red walls.

**Prompt discipline (OpenArt/Kling/Cursor):** Lead with `SET-001:S?_-___`, Material Lock triad, Camera ID, time-of-day from §6.
