## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**SET-001** is the permanent physical origin of the Frontal Slayer universe—a **single-build luxury flagship** designed simultaneously as:

- Operating high-touch retail + salon + atelier business  
- Permanent **Hollywood-grade** interior/exterior film set  
- **Twin Strict** reference for Mansion, mobile, and web environments  

**Resolved footprint (conceptual):**

| Level | Approx. area | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ground — Guest plate** | ~16,500 SF (1,530 m²) | Public spine, showroom, commerce, member-facing Z1–Z8 guest layers |
| **Lower — Operations plate** | ~7,000 SF (650 m²) | Z9–Z10 receiving, inventory, staff, content prep, MEP-heavy zones |
| **North wing extension (phased)** | ~4,500 SF (420 m²) **future** | VIP lounge + event/chapter hall + private suite stack — **pre-provisioned structure**, not required day one |

**Planning strategy:** **Hybrid circulation** (Phase 3)—one **Guest Spine** on ground floor; **three concealed spoke systems** (Member, Service, Production); lower plate **never** visible from street.

**Build phasing:** **Spine First** (Z1–Z2–Z7 core + minimal Z4–Z6 suites) → **Depth Second** (TV Lounge, consult/analysis, full atelier) → **Wing Third** (VIP/event/B2B/archive/broadcast futures).

## 2. BUILDING NARRATIVE

### Why SET-001 exists

Frontal Slayer is a **world**, not a SKU list. SET-001 exists so every guest **physically enters the same story** the trilogy, Mansion, and mobile imply: a luxury house where hair craft is **authored**, not grabbed.

### What it represents

**The house made visible**—precision, hospitality, and feminine calm encoded in marble, glass, and earned depth. It is **destination before salon**: gallery + concierge + private suites, one continuous choreography.

### Neighborhood relationship

SET-001 sits on an **upscale mixed-retail block** (luxury fashion, fine dining, hotel within two blocks)—not a mall interior. Foot traffic is **pedestrian discovery**; the boutique **does not shout**. Neighbors are upscale but generic; SET-001 is the **pause**—slower rhythm, warmer light, fewer signs. Campaign grammar assumes **morning sun from the street axis** (trilogy First Act) with storefront facing primary sidewalk.

### Role in the universe

| Layer | SET-001 role |
| --- | --- |
| **Reality One (physical)** | Truth set for Nia/chapters, retail, services |
| **Reality Two (mobile)** | Photography + interaction **sampled** from Strict zones |
| **Reality Three (Mansion)** | Navigable twin—Enhanced depth, same identity |

### Emotional journey (arrival → departure)

Wonder at the vitrine → **recognition** at reception → **confidence** through education/showroom → **agency** in atelier → **transformation** in suite → **pride** at celebration → **calm completion** at checkout → **belonging** tease (membership/TV) → **continuity** at Mansion bridge. Anxiety is designed out at threshold; exclusivity is **earned**, not gated at the door.

## 5. EXTERIOR DESIGN

### Street presence

Calm luxury **horizontal banding**: white marble base, full-width vitrine, minimal wordmark **centered or offset left** (trilogy framing locked to **guest-left discovery** along sidewalk). No banner sales. Planters/greenery **minimal**—roses visible at reception through glass, not cluttered sidewalk.

### Storefront composition

**Three vertical registers:** marble plinth (2 ft / 0.6 m visual base) · vitrine (full height) · thin metal coping. Entry **center-right** (creates campaign asymmetry for Nia approach). Secondary **service door** on alley—**no** guest visibility.

### Arrival experience

Approach from sidewalk → slow vitrine read → **pause** at entry airlock (automatic door or host-open)—climate and sound **mute** street. No step-down; **flush threshold** for accessibility and camera dolly.

### Daytime identity

Morning natural key (campaign); interior cove fill **warm**; vitrine anti-glare for interior readability from street.

### Nighttime identity

Warm interior glow **outward**; exterior downlights on marble base only; no neon; logo subtly backlit if at all.

### Campaign potential

Exterior set supports **FRAME 001–009** grammar: pedestrian camera, boutique awakens, Nia discovery, no staff chase. **Campaign blocking hold** allows overnight exterior prop reset without changing architecture.

### Neighbors

SET-001 is **quieter** than neighbors—slower signage, more glass, fewer mannequins in window (units as **sculpture**, not crowded).

### Architectural character

Timeless **international luxury boutique**—not Art Deco pastiche, not tech showroom, not salon strip mall. Feminine through **curve and life**, not pink branding.

## 6. INTERIOR DESIGN — MAJOR ENVIRONMENTS

Architecture only (volume, proportion, circulation, relationships)—**no decoration brief**.

### Z1 — Arrival bay (~1,850 SF / 172 m² total cluster)

**Volume:** Threshold compresses to reception **single hospitality volume** (~11 ft ceiling).  
**Relationship:** Threshold **in line** with vitrine; reception **perpendicular** to guest spine, facing showroom mouth. Orientation nook **adjacent** reception, partially screened by glass reeding.

### Z2 — Grand gallery / showroom (~3,100 SF / 288 m²)

**Volume:** **Double-height** hero void; mezzanine **not** open to public (future wing separated).  
**Proportion:** 3:2 length:width; central circulation **racetrack** around hero plinths. Texture/color stations **embedded** in north wall niches; comparison alcove **east** pocket.

### Z3 — Education arm (~1,400 SF / 130 m² incl. TV ante)

**Volume:** Standard height; **light slot** from ceiling cove toward showroom. TV Lounge **sealed dark box** within arm; ante **outside** dark door.

### Z4 — Consult spoke (~1,650 SF / 153 m²)

**Volume:** Intimate 9–10 ft; **acoustic mass** on west wall toward alley. Ante + **two** consult rooms + analysis lab **in sequence** (lab deepest, no exterior window).

### Z5 — Atelier block (~2,200 SF / 204 m²)

**Volume:** Work height 10 ft; **threshold portal** full-height glass door; floor **honed** marble. Material library **behind** atelier, visible only from atelier floor.

### Z6 — Transform cluster (~1,900 SF / 176 m²)

**Volume:** Suite privacy 9 ft; **styling bay** adjacent; installation **two-chair** suite; portrait alcove **between** transform and celebration path.

### Z7 — Commerce arm (~1,350 SF / 125 m²)

**Volume:** Calm 10 ft; **processional** celebration → checkout → departure **linear**. Membership desk **parallel** to checkout, not blocking exit.

### Z8 — Member zone (ground embed + future wing)

**Launch:** TV Lounge + ante only (~1,600 SF / 149 m² dark + 200 SF ante).  
**Future wing:** VIP + event + founder hold + community wall (~4,500 SF)—**MS-1** direct from member ante, not from vitrine.

### Z9 / Z10 — Lower plate (~7,000 SF / 650 m²)

Content creation **adjacent** to vertical lift to ground **hidden hatch** near showroom back. Receiving **alley-facing**; inventory **central**; staff support **adjacent** PSA back-of-house core under reception.
