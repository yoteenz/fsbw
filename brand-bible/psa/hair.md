## Introduction

PSA’s **hairstyle is a primary recognition marker**, not a disposable fashion choice. Members encounter PSA at small scale (mobile FAB), at broadcast scale (Lounge TV), and in still photography across commerce and education. Hair occupies a large share of the visible silhouette in every default frame. If hair drifts—length, color family, part, texture, or density—**likeness fails even when the face is correct**.

This document is **permanent production specification** for PSA’s hair only. It is **not** a prompt library, marketing headline, product shade chart, or tutorial on how to install a unit. It defines **what PSA’s hair is**, **how it behaves**, **what may change in context**, and **what must never change** without a versioned master-reference update approved by the Founder and Executive Creative Director.

> **Hierarchy:** [`design-principles.md`](./design-principles.md) (why) → [`identity.md`](./identity.md) (likeness lock) → **this document** (hair execution) → shot-specific styling notes. Prompts and model parameters live **outside** this bible (`motherboard/golden-prompts/`); they must **obey** this document.

> **Master reference authority:** Measured color swatches, turnaround photography, and strand-level references are **locked to the approved master portrait set** (canonical expression assets in `public/assets/psa-avatar-*.png` and secure master vault — [`character-turnaround.md`](./character-turnaround.md)). Where this bible describes observable canonical reference, it documents **production truth**, not aspirational redesign.

## Hair Philosophy

PSA’s hair is the **visible proof** that Frontal Slayer understands premium hair: healthy, intentional, salon-finished, and believable on camera. It must communicate the following **without** reading as a product demo or trend piece.

| Quality | How hair supports it |
| --- | --- |
| **Luxury** | Controlled finish, dimensional color, clean hairline, editorial shine — never cheap volume or synthetic plastic sheen. |
| **Confidence** | Stable silhouette and deliberate part; hair frames the face without hiding it or performing for attention. |
| **Professionalism** | Host-ready grooming; reads as someone who prepares for set, consult, and member-facing education. |
| **Timeless beauty** | Soft waves and dimensional brunette warmth — valid across years, not tied to a viral curl pattern or color cycle. |
| **Trust** | Realistic density, believable lace-front transition, natural strand behavior — members trust experts who look **real**. |
| **Health** | Lustrous but not glass; movement with weight; no brittle ends or straw texture. |
| **Refinement** | Edges and baby hairs **groomed**, not messy; flyaways **controlled**, not eliminated into helmet hair. |
| **Approachability** | Warm tones and soft movement invite connection; hair never feels aggressive, chaotic, or “trying too hard.” |
| **Quiet sophistication** | One strong hair story per frame — dimensional color and wave, not stacked trends (balayage noise + extreme length + wet look). |

### Why PSA’s hair must not chase trends

Trend-driven hair signals **disposability**—the opposite of Frontal Slayer’s luxury and education mission. Overly dramatic silhouettes (extreme height, fashion colors, beach-messy texture, influencer “glass hair”) **compete with PSA’s face** and erode recognition in circular crops. PSA is a **permanent host**, not a seasonal campaign model.

**Operational rule:** If a styling idea would be remarkable on a red carpet for one night but forgotten in five years, it is **out of scope** for PSA’s signature look unless authorized as a **non-canon** deliverable (not PSA).

## Signature Hairstyle

The following sections describe PSA’s **official signature hairstyle** as observed on the approved master reference and expression library. Each attribute includes **what it is** and **why it supports identity**.

### Overall silhouette

| Attribute | Canonical specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Read** | Long, **cascading wave** with **soft crown volume** and **asymmetrical sweep** from a deep side part | Instantly recognizable bust silhouette; reads at FAB and 16:9 without face |
| **Mass shape** | Rounded at crown; flows over shoulders and chest in a **continuous S-curve sheet** | Supports “luxury concierge” oval in circular crop per Identity Bible |
| **Asymmetry** | Heavier drape on one side from part sweep; **natural**, not wig-shifted | Human realism; avoids mirror-perfect AI symmetry |

### Length

| Zone | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Front / framing layers** | Long enough to **sweep across forehead** from side part; face-framing layers blend into main body |
| **Side / shoulder** | Passes **well below shoulder**; typical read **mid-torso to waist** on bust and 3/4 host framing |
| **Back (when visible)** | Consistent with front length — **no mullet or abrupt step** unless scripted rear view reference approves |

**Why:** Length communicates **premium raw-hair credibility** and salon investment while remaining **host-practical** (not floor-length novelty).

**Lock:** Apparent length may not shorten to bob, lob, or pixie, nor extend to exaggerated floor length, without new master reference.

### Density

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Perceived density** | **Medium-high** — full, luxurious, **not sparse**, **not helmet-thick** |
| **Scalp read** | Natural part line; no bald patches; no unnatural plug rows |
| **Edge density** | Hairline transition **full but refined** — lace-front realism |

**Why:** Frontal Slayer sells density and customization expertise; PSA’s hair must exemplify **believable fullness**, not anime volume.

### Volume

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Crown** | **Soft lift** — rounded, blowout-derived; **not** 1980s height, **not** flat pin-straight helmet |
| **Mid-lengths** | Body and bounce from wave pattern; **controlled**, not frizzy cloud |
| **Ends** | Tapered weight; **no single blunt block** unless reference updates |

**Why:** Volume signals health and styling skill; **excessive volume** reads synthetic and breaks trust.

### Weight

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical read** | Hair **falls with gravity**; waves settle **down and out**, not floating upward |
| **Shoulder interaction** | Strands **rest on shoulders/chest** with slight compression — believable mass |

**Why:** Real hair has weight; zero-gravity hair is an AI failure mode.

### Movement

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Default** | **Soft, fluid** wave motion — pendulum swing when walking; **lag** behind head turns |
| **Amplitude** | Moderate — **never whip-like** unless scripted action beat |
| **Settle** | After motion, hair **returns to signature wave pattern** within 1–2 seconds (real-time video) |

**Why:** Movement sells luxury **care and quality**; chaotic movement sells disorder.

### Flow

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Direction** | From side part, flow **forward across forehead** and **downward over shoulder line** on heavy side |
| **Layering flow** | Layers **feather into** main body — no hard stair-steps |
| **Back flow** | When visible, flows **down back** in parallel waves — no radial “sunburst” from crown |

### Shape

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Cross-section** | **Soft oval** mass around head; not triangular cone, not flat sheet |
| **Wave shape** | **Loose S-waves** — wide amplitude, **not** tight coils, **not** pin-straight |

### Texture

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Surface** | **Silky-smooth** with **visible strand separation** |
| **Touch read** | Salon-blown, lightly set — **not** wet-look gel, **not** matte dry hay |
| **Frizz** | **None visible** at production quality; micro-texture only at extreme macro |

**Why:** Texture is the category authority signal for a hair brand; PSA must look **touchably premium**.

### Wave pattern

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | **Loose S-curves**, consistent from mid-shaft through ends |
| **Root zone** | **Smoother, slightly straighter** near root — natural blowout root lift |
| **Uniformity** | Waves **repeat rhythmically**; avoid random kinks or crimps |

**Why:** This wave family is **timeless** and distinct from “beach wave” mess or spiral curl product lines.

### Layering

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Structure** | **Long layers** supporting movement and face framing |
| **Face frame** | Subtle layers integrating with side sweep — **no sharp hime cut** |
| **Visibility** | Layers **felt in motion**, not choppy static clumps |

### Part placement

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | **Deep side part** |
| **Side** | **Subject’s right** (viewer’s left) — **locked** |
| **Line** | **Clean, continuous** from hairline through crown |
| **Sweep** | Hair crosses **upper forehead** at controlled angle — **does not** blind eye |

**Why:** Part side is a **secondary recognition marker** in thumbnail crops.

**Lock:** Do not flip part for “variety” or composition convenience.

### Hairline appearance

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Front hairline** | **Lace-front realism** — gradual density transition, **melted** into skin |
| **Temples** | **Soft, believable** recession pattern — no hard wig edge |
| **Forehead** | Proportional — hairline **does not** advance or retreat vs reference |

**Why:** Frontal Slayer sells frontals and hairlines; PSA’s hairline must be **education-grade believable**.

### Flyaways

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Production** | **Remove or tame** flyaways that read as sloppiness or AI noise |
| **Permitted** | **Zero to minimal** — occasional single strand acceptable in **outdoor/wind** variants only |

**Why:** Luxury grooming; flyaways break polish in FAB size.

### Baby hairs (intentional control)

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Temple baby hairs** | **Fine, groomed**, following hairline curve — **laid**, not fuzzy cloud |
| **Count** | **Sparse and intentional** — suggests professional edge work |
| **Prohibited** | Heavy “Instagram edge lay” sculptural waves; glitter gel; heart shapes |

**Why:** Baby hairs prove lace realism; over-styling reads trend, not host.

### Surface smoothness

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Macro** | Smooth cuticle read — **reflective bands** along waves |
| **Micro** | Strand separation visible — **not** one fused polygon |

### Natural movement

Hair must **obey physics** in all media (see *Hair Behavior*). Static hero frames still **imply** weight through drape and overlap.

### Finish

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Set level** | **Soft set** — holds wave, allows bounce |
| **Product read** | **Invisible** — no visible buildup, flakes, or grease |

### Shine characteristics

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Level** | **High healthy shine** on mid-lengths and ends; **slightly softer** at root |
| **Quality** | **Silk-like specular** highlights — **not** plastic specular blob |
| **Distribution** | Shine follows **wave crests**; dimension preserved in shadow valleys |

### Root appearance

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Color** | **Shadow root** — deeper, cooler brown at root melting into mid-tone |
| **Lift** | **Soft blowout lift** at crown — not flat, not teased aggressively |
| **Part** | Root along part **slightly darker** — natural scalp illusion |

### Edge detailing

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Perimeter** | Clean perimeter on shoulders; **no thin wispy ends** (split-end read) |
| **Lace blend** | Edges at forehead/temples **color-matched** to skin undertone — no gray cast line |

### Overall realism

| Standard | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Photorealism** | Indistinguishable from high-end editorial beauty photography at intended output resolution |
| **Failure** | Plastic shine, uniform color block, wig line, impossible volume, strand direction chaos |

## Hair Color Standards

Color is **emotional architecture**: warmth invites trust; dimension signals salon investment. This section defines **permanent signature color philosophy and canonical family** locked to master reference — **not** box dye SKUs or vendor formulas.

### Primary color

| Role | Canonical family |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary base** | **Rich warm brown** — deep chocolate to chestnut family |
| **Read** | **Brunette luxury** — never flat jet black block, never ashy gray cast |

**Emotional impact:** Warm brown reads **approachable expertise** and **healthy natural hair**; flat black reads harsh or synthetic on video.

### Undertones

| Layer | Undertone |
| --- | --- |
| **Base pigment** | **Warm red-gold undertone** in mid-lengths |
| **Root shadow** | **Cooler, deeper brown** for depth — prevents orange root |
| **Skin harmony** | Color **complements** locked skin tone — no clash that shifts ethnicity read |

### Natural color variation

Variation is **intentional dimension**, not random noise:

- Root → mid-shaft → end **gradient** present  
- Strand-to-strand **micro-variation** within family  
- **No** single hex flat fill across entire mass

### Dimension

| Technique (observed read) | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Shadow root** | Depth, realism, grown-out luxury |
| **Mid-tone body** | Primary recognition color |
| **Lighter ribboning** | Movement accent on wave crests |

### Highlights

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Family** | **Copper, auburn, golden-caramel** accents — **woven**, not striped |
| **Placement** | Mid-lengths through ends; **heavier on outer wave crests** facing light |
| **Strength** | **Visible but refined** — editorial, not nightclub |

**Emotional impact:** Highlights suggest **sun-kissed vitality** and customization skill — aligned with education, not neon fashion.

### Low lights

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | Anchor depth in underlayers and near root |
| **Read** | Prevent “one flat wig color” |

### Root transition

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Blend** | **Soft melt** over **2–4 cm** visual read — no hard demarcation line |
| **Drift rule** | Root may not become **gray, blonde, or fashion tint** without new reference |

### Light reflection

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Specular** | **Long soft bands** along waves |
| **Multiple lights** | Separate specular on **highlight strands** vs base |

### Appearance by lighting context

| Context | Expected read |
| --- | --- |
| **Indoor (soft key)** | Warm brown body; caramel ribbons **subtle**; shine **controlled** |
| **Outdoor (daylight)** | Highlights **activate**; warmth **increases slightly** — **not** orange |
| **Studio beauty (key + fill + rim)** | Full dimension visible; rim may edge-kiss **cool/magenta/cyan** on holographic hosts — **hair pigment unchanged** |
| **Golden hour** | Auburn/copper **glow**; **maintain** brunette identity — not blonde conversion |
| **Cool lighting** | Base **deepens**; highlights read **copper-rose** — **no ash drain to gray wig** |
| **Warm lighting** | Gold emphasis on crests; **avoid** monochromatic orange |

### Photography vs video

| Medium | Consistency rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Photography** | Grade may shift **exposure**; **hue family locked** — same brunette-warm DNA in before/after color grade |
| **Video** | Temporal **flicker** in shine acceptable; **hue must not pulse** frame-to-frame |
| **Compression** | Banding must not collapse dimension to one brown — preserve **three-tone minimum** read |

### Color consistency principles

1. **Same person, same hair story** across expressions — color **does not** change per PNG slug.  
2. **Holographic rim light** tints **light**, not **melanin or hair dye**.  
3. **No fashion colors** (blue, pink, silver, platinum, balayage gray) in signature canon.  
4. **Retouch** may polish; **may not** recolor to new family.  
5. When in doubt, match **master neutral expression** (`psa-avatar-neutral.png` family).

## Hairstyle Variations

PSA may **adjust styling context**; she may **not** become a different character. Variations are **states of the same signature**, like a host’s on-air vs backstage grooming.

### Variation matrix

| Variation | Allowed adjustments | Locked elements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary host style** | Full signature waves, maximum polish | Length, part side, color family, density, hairline |
| **Walking style** | Slight increase in strand separation; **motion** in waves | Part, color, length, wave family |
| **Teaching style** | Hair **secured away from face** optional (soft clip **behind ear** — **not** ponytail); face unobstructed | Color, length, part side |
| **Formal presentation** | **Extra polish**, slightly tighter wave set; **more controlled** flyaways | Silhouette mass, part, color |
| **Relaxed concierge** | **Softer set**, micro-movement; **still salon-finished** | No messy bun, no downgraded length |
| **Luxury event appearance** | Slightly **stronger highlight read** under event lights | No new colors; no updo that hides signature length |
| **Outdoor appearance** | **Wind-responsive** movement; **minimal** flyaways | Part side, color, wave type |
| **Wind interaction** | Strands **lag**; **return** to pattern after gust | No horizontal “model wind machine” permanence |
| **Seated** | Hair **pools** on shoulders/chest; **compresses** realistically | No clipping through body |
| **Conversation** | Micro bounce on head nods | Identity geometry |
| **Product demonstration** | May **hold** or **point to** hair demo — PSA’s **own** hair unchanged | No “before/after” on PSA’s head |

### Movement during activity (summary)

```
Walking     → pendulum swing, delayed follow-through
Turning     → outer strands lag; inner closer to head
Seated      → weight on shoulders; part remains fixed
Demo        → hands near hair OK; hairstyle unchanged
```

**Invariant:** Any variation must pass **“silhouette test”** — PSA recognizable from hair alone at thumbnail scale.

## Hair Behavior

All media must respect **realistic hair physics**. Stylization is permitted in **timing and easing**, not in **impossible strand paths**.

### How hair falls

- Primary direction: **downward** from part and crown  
- Outer layers **overlie** inner layers with **occlusion**  
- Ends **taper** — lighter visual weight than roots  

### How it moves

| Action | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Walk cycle** | Waves **swing** side-to-side; **phase offset** between left/right mass |
| **Head turn** | Strands **follow** with 3–8 frame delay (video) or implied drag (illustration) |
| **Nod** | Short bounce at ends; crown **stable** |
| **Gesture (hands)** | Hair **does not** intersect hands unless contact scripted |

### Wind (subtle)

- **Gentle:** 2–5 strands lift at **outer crest**; settle back  
- **Moderate (outdoor only):** Temporary part **disturbance** allowed; **part side restores**  
- **Prohibited:** Permanent horizontal blow, anime wind, frozen wind shape  

### Seated settlement

Hair **accumulates** on lap/chest when leaning forward slightly; **does not** pass through clothing geometry.

### Light interaction

| Phenomenon | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Specular catch** | Moves with head; **consistent** with scene key light |
| **Subsurface** | Minimal — **not** translucent plastic |
| **Rim light** | May edge-light **strands**; **does not** recolor entire mass |

### Individual strands

- **Visible** in hero close-ups  
- **Direction** follows wave flow — **no** opposing comb directions adjacent  
- **Clumping** in groups of 3–20 strands — not one mesh

## Hair Quality Standards

**Luxury hair** within Frontal Slayer means **believable human premium**, not CGI perfection.

| Standard | Definition | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Healthy appearance** | Cuticle shine, flexible wave, no breakage frizz | Straw, brittle tips |
| **Natural density** | Full but scalp believable | Helmet, see-through |
| **Realistic shine** | Silk specular | Mirror plastic |
| **Premium texture** | Smooth with separation | Fused polygon |
| **Strand separation** | Visible in mid-light | Single blob |
| **Natural imperfections** | Micro asymmetry in wave | Perfect sine wave |
| **Soft movement** | Weighted bounce | Stiff statue |
| **Natural weight** | Gravity pull | Anti-gravity float |
| **Luxury finish** | Invisible professional set | Crunchy gel |
| **Photorealism** | Passes beauty editorial QC | Uncanny wig |

> **Anti-perfection:** Eliminating **all** micro-variation creates **AI tell**. Luxury = **controlled**, not **sterile**.

## Hair Continuity Standards

Strict rules for production, QC, and versioning.

### Non-negotiable locks

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Recognizable silhouette** | Long soft wave, side-swept mass — **always** |
| **Length drift** | **Forbidden** — no bob, lob, pixie, or extra floor length |
| **Density drift** | **Forbidden** — no sudden thin or thick |
| **Color drift** | **Forbidden** — stay in warm brunette + copper/caramel dimension family |
| **Part placement** | **Subject’s right** — **always** |
| **Hairline** | **Melted lace realism** — **always** |
| **Texture family** | **Loose S-wave** — **always** (no curl reset) |
| **Movement realism** | **Always** physics-compliant |
| **Volume ceiling** | **No** afro-scale, **no** pageant tease |
| **AI artifacts** | **Zero tolerance** — melted strands, extra part lines, color banding |
| **Strand direction** | **Consistent** flow; no opposing whorls |

### Continuity checklist (production sign-off)

- [ ] Silhouette matches master reference at thumbnail scale  
- [ ] Part on **correct side**  
- [ ] Length read **mid-torso to waist** on standard bust  
- [ ] Wave pattern **loose S**, not coil or straight  
- [ ] **Three-tone** color dimension (root / body / highlight)  
- [ ] Hairline **melted**; baby hairs **groomed minimal**  
- [ ] Shine **silky**, not plastic  
- [ ] Density **medium-high**, not helmet  
- [ ] No fashion color intrusion  
- [ ] Motion (if animated) **settles** to signature  
- [ ] Face still reads as PSA if face cropped out — **hair alone** recognizable  
- [ ] Holographic grade **does not** shift hair hue

## Styling Rules

### Permitted

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Approved styling changes** | Tighter or softer **set** within same wave; slight **face-framing tuck** behind ear for teaching |
| **Temporary styling** | Clip **hidden** behind ear; **clear** band for demo only if face remains primary |
| **Special occasions** | **Red carpet** expression = **stronger polish**, same geometry (`red-carpet` avatar family) |
| **Weather variations** | Controlled outdoor wind response |
| **Professional adjustments** | Flyaway cleanup, edge lay **minimal**, color grade **exposure only** |
| **Seasonal adjustments** | **None to identity** — seasonal **lighting** only, not new cuts/colors |
| **Emergency continuity fixes** | Recomposite from master reference; **do not** inpaint new hairstyle |

### Prohibited

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Fashion colors** | Blue, pink, green, silver, platinum, gray fashion, neon |
| **Extreme curls** | Tight coils, spiral ringlets, crimp |
| **Beach waves** | Messy, salt-spray, undefined pattern |
| **Messy styles** | Bed head, undone bun, claw clip chaos |
| **Trend-driven cuts** | Wolf cut, shag, hime, buzz, bangs that hide eyes |
| **Overly dramatic styling** | Wet look, slicked gel sculpt, extreme height |
| **Artificial shine** | Mirror wig, oily scalp |
| **Plastic appearance** | Single-specular mesh |
| **Influencer aesthetics** | “Glass hair,” extreme length fake, color blocking |
| **Recognition weakeners** | Part flip, length change, texture family swap |
| **Product demo on PSA** | Before/after color on PSA’s own hair in canon assets |

## Hair In Different Mediums

| Medium | Translation standard |
| --- | --- |
| **Photography** | Reference truth; retouch per Identity Bible — **no** recolor or length liquify |
| **Video** | Temporal consistency; motion physics; **same** color grade rules as stills |
| **Animation (2D)** | Simplified strands OK; **silhouette + part + color triad** preserved |
| **3D** | Groom/clump guides follow wave flow; physics sim **capped** to luxury calm |
| **Illustration** | Line weight may stylize; **wave read + part side** locked |
| **Motion graphics** | PSA hair **not** replaced by abstract shapes; if simplified, **must** match silhouette |
| **AR / VR** | Real-time LOD may reduce strand count; **never** reduce to different haircut |
| **Interactive media** | Expression changes **only** — hair obeys same lock as avatar PNG library |
| **Future technologies** | Inherit this document before new pipelines ship |

**Cross-medium test:** Export still from each pipeline → **grayscale silhouette overlay** on master → **≥ 90% overlap** of mass (Art Director judgment).
