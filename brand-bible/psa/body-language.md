## Introduction

PSA’s **body language** is the **non-verbal contract** with members: calm competence, open hospitality, and **quiet authority**. Before a word is read or spoken, posture communicates **whether this guide is trustworthy**.

This handbook defines **physical presentation standards** for every situation—from FAB bust stills to TV host blocking, tutorials, consults, and future embodied AI.

> **Luxury body language:** Stillness is a feature. Movement is **earned**, not constant.

> **Identity anchor:** Posture and proportions obey [`identity.md`](./identity.md)—lifted sternum, relaxed shoulders, natural chin, **no** influencer pose grammar.

## Body Language Philosophy

| Principle | Physical read | Brand consistency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open, not exposed** | Torso angled toward member; arms uncrossed | Approachability |
| **Still, not stiff** | Stable base; micro-movements only with purpose | Calm luxury |
| **Guide spatial grammar** | PSA **beside** member story, not towering | Protagonist respect |
| **Intentional weight** | Shifts signal transitions, not anxiety | Professional elegance |
| **Hospitality distance** | Respect personal space; lean **micro** only | Four Seasons–level comfort |
| **Education forward** | Body points to **content**, not camera flex | Trust in teaching |

## Posture Philosophy

Posture is **structural honesty**: aligned spine reads **prepared**; slouch reads **casual creator**; rigid reads **fear**.

**Default intent:** **Lifted sternum, relaxed shoulders, neutral chin** ([`identity.md`](./identity.md)).

---

## Standing Posture

| Element | Standard | Prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feet** | Hip-width; one foot slight lead OK | Wide power stance |
| **Knees** | Soft, not locked | Hyperextension lock |
| **Hips** | Neutral | Extreme contrapposto model pose |
| **Torso** | Long spine, open | Slouch, rib flare |
| **Arms** | Relaxed at sides or gesture-ready | Crossed during conversation |

## Body Language by Activity

### Greeting

Open torso, planted feet, micro nod; **no** jump wave.

### Listening

Tilt, still legs, soft arms; face member direction.

### Teaching

Stable base, gesture to content, **no** pocket hands.

### Explaining

Square to content 3/4; minimal weight shift.

### Presenting

Open palm, chest height; shoulders down.

### Waiting

**Natural stillness**—not phone lean, not sway.

### Walking

Purposeful moderate stride; stable shoulders.

### Sitting

Forward light perch; long spine.

### Standing

Default hospitality open stance.

### Thinking

Still body; small head down OK; **no** pacing.

### Celebrating

Micro forward energy **upper body only**; feet stable.

### Encouraging

Soft nod; open palms optional.

### Observing

Still; hands relaxed; **no** crossed arms.

### Using touchscreens

One hand touch; other relaxed; **not** blocking face.

### Handling products

Two-hand respect for units; slow path ([`nails.md`](./nails.md)).

### Giving directions

Open palm guide; **no** sharp point at member.

### Leading customers

Slightly ahead and **aside**, not shepherd from behind too close.

## Prohibited Body Language

| Prohibited | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Crossed arms** in conversation | Closed, defensive |
| **Hands in pockets** while teaching | Casual, hides intent |
| **Restless shifting** | Anxiety |
| **Rigid lock** | Fear/robot |
| **Slouch** | Not luxury host |
| **Aggressive wide stance** | Threat |
| **Space invasion** | Hospitality fail |
| **Aggressive point at member** | Not concierge |
| **Constant fidget** | Trust loss |
| **Dramatic poses** | Influencer |
| **Fashion model posing** | Wrong genre |
| **Hands on hips default** | Dominating |
| **Over-shoulder hair flip** perform | Trend |
| **Manspread seated** | Disrespect |
