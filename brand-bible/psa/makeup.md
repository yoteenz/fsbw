## Introduction

PSA’s **makeup is a trust instrument**, not a trend canvas. Members judge competence in milliseconds—especially on a **88px FAB**, a **16:9 host frame**, and lip-readable education video. Makeup must **enhance recognition**, **preserve likeness**, and signal **luxury editorial polish** while remaining **human and believable**.

This document is **permanent production specification** for PSA’s makeup only. It is **not** a prompt library, product tutorial, shade sell sheet, or step-by-step MUA consumer guide. It defines **what PSA’s makeup is**, **how it reads under light**, **what may adjust by context**, and **what must never change** without a versioned master-reference update approved by the Founder and Executive Creative Director.

> **Hierarchy:** [`design-principles.md`](./design-principles.md) (why) → [`identity.md`](./identity.md) (likeness, skin, feature geometry) → **this document** (makeup execution) → shot-specific call sheets. Prompts live **outside** this bible; they must **obey** this document.

> **Master reference authority:** Face charts, pigment references, and lighting tests are **locked to the approved master portrait set** (canonical expression assets in `public/assets/psa-avatar-*.png` and secure master vault — see *Future Expansion*). Observable canonical reference below is **production truth**, not an aspirational redesign.

> **Focal hierarchy:** **Face → expression → hair → wardrobe.** Makeup must **never** become the primary story. If a viewer remembers the eyeliner before they remember PSA, the look **fails**.

## Makeup Philosophy

PSA’s makeup communicates **institutional beauty**—the same calm precision members expect from a flagship salon and a premium network host.

| Quality | How makeup supports it |
| --- | --- |
| **Quiet luxury** | Refined neutrals, controlled shine, invisible “heavy product” read — elegance through **edit**, not accumulation. |
| **Confidence** | Defined structure (brow, soft contour) without aggressive sculpt; eyes **lifted**, not **shouted**. |
| **Professionalism** | Camera-ready finish suitable for education, consult tone, and host segments — never club or costume. |
| **Warmth** | Peach-terracotta blush family, golden highlights, nude-warm lips — **inviting**, not cold editorial gray. |
| **Trust** | **Real skin texture** visible at intended resolution; no filter-face or uncanny perfection. |
| **Intelligence** | Eyes **clear and readable**; makeup supports **teaching** and **eye contact**, not mystery. |
| **Editorial polish** | Campaign-grade blend and finish — **soft glam** tier, not bare minimal, not drag maximal. |
| **Human authenticity** | Asymmetry preserved; pores and micro-variation **allowed**; teeth and sclera **natural**. |
| **Timeless elegance** | Warm neutral eye, classic wing family, nude-plus lip — **valid for years**, not one TikTok cycle. |

### Why makeup must not become the focal point

Frontal Slayer’s mission is **education and guidance**. PSA’s face carries **expression and voice alignment**. Trend makeup (graphic liner cycles, hyper-gloss skin, overlined lips, extreme cut crease) **steals attention** and **dates** the brand. Makeup exists to **stabilize identity** across media, the same way a news anchor’s grooming supports the message.

**Operational rule:** Makeup changes **intensity** by context; it does **not** change **identity geometry** or **skin ethnicity read** (see [`identity.md`](./identity.md)).

## Signature Makeup Look

Canonical read: **Editorial soft glam** — luminous satin skin, warm neutral eyes with controlled wing, full-but-believable lashes, groomed arched brows, nude-plus lip with definition. Each section: **what** + **why**.

### Overall finish

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier** | **Soft glam / editorial luxury** | Host + concierge credibility |
| **Balance** | **Polished face, real skin** | Trust over filter |
| **Read at distance** | Even tone, defined eyes, lip shape legible | FAB + TV |
| **Read at close-up** | Texture present, blend seamless | Education close-ups |

### Skin finish

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Finish type** | **Luminous satin** — lit-from-within radiance | Luxury hospitality lighting |
| **Not** | Dead matte, oily slick, wet glass skin | Matte reads flat on video; glass reads filter |
| **Sheen zones** | High points only (see highlight) | Dimension without grease |

### Foundation appearance

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coverage** | **Medium-to-full** seamless evenness | On-camera consistency |
| **Match** | **Locked to reference skin tone and undertone** | Ethnicity preservation |
| **Edge** | Melted into hairline, ears, jaw — **no mask line** | Lace-front education credibility |
| **Body** | Face and visible neck **matched** | Bust framing |

**Why:** Foundation **evens**; it must **not** repaint identity or lighten melanin for “brightening bias.”

### Complexion balance

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Goal** | **Unified warm golden-brown read** with dimensional shadow | Healthy, rested expert |
| **Discoloration** | Neutralized **subtly** — no gray cast | Avoid ashy AI skin |
| **Redness** | Controlled, not erased entirely | Human blood flow |

### Concealer philosophy

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Placement** | Under-eye **triangle softening**, spot correction only as needed | Brightens without raccoon reverse |
| **Opacity** | **Medium** — blend to skin, not lighter stripe | Prevents “concealer mask” |
| **Philosophy** | **Correct, don’t erase character** | Keeps natural facial story |

### Contour placement

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product family** | **Warm contour** (not gray mud) | Harmonizes with skin undertone |
| **Zones** | Hollow of cheek, jaw shadow, sides of nose **soft** | Structure for camera |
| **Depth** | **Visible in studio light**, **invisible in daylight** | No striped Instagram contour |
| **Prohibited** | Snatched jaw, razor cheek, new bone structure | Identity lock |

**Why:** Contour **supports** reference bone structure; it **must not sculpt a new face**.

### Bronzer usage

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Role** | **Warmth and dimension** adjunct to contour | Sun-kissed health |
| **Placement** | Perimeter of face, optional nose bridge warmth | Subtle |
| **Avoid** | Orange stripe, all-over tan mask | Cheap glam read |

### Blush placement

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Color family** | **Warm peach / terracotta / rose-peach** | Warmth + approachability |
| **Placement** | Apples upward toward temple; **merge** with highlight | Cohesive lift |
| **Intensity** | **Moderate** default; **stronger** only in red-carpet tier | Readable on video without clown |

### Highlight placement

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tone** | **Champagne gold** (warm, not icy white) | Luxury metal language |
| **Zones** | Cheekbone crest, nose bridge + tip, brow bone, inner eye corner | Editorial dimension |
| **Intensity** | **Prominent in studio**; scale down for flat daylight in post if needed | Avoid stripe highlight |
| **Prohibited** | Holographic glitter, nose stripe meme, over-baked strobe | Trend noise |

### Eye makeup

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overall** | **Warm neutral soft glam** | Timeless host |
| **Goal** | Eyes **larger and brighter**, not **replaced** | Expression readability |

### Eyeshadow style

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crease** | **Matte warm transition** (taupe, soft brown, terracotta-neutral) | Depth without color story |
| **Lid** | **Champagne gold shimmer** center lid | Light catch for video |
| **Outer V** | **Soft deepen** — optional micro-smoke | Lift; not smokey club |
| **Lower lid** | **Clean or very soft brown smoke** | Fresh, not tired |
| **Prohibited** | Color washes (blue, pink, green), cut crease, graphic shapes | Trend + distraction |

### Liner philosophy

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Type** | **Black liquid** upper lash line | Classic definition |
| **Shape** | **Cat-eye wing** — elongated upward **lift** | Host alertness |
| **Thickness** | **Medium** — tapers to wing; **not** block graphic | Readable at mid-shot |
| **Lower line** | **None to minimal** — prefer shadow over hard pencil | Softer luxury |
| **Philosophy** | Liner **extends eye shape**, **does not** redraw eye size beyond reference | Prevents AI eye drift |

### Mascara appearance

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Upper** | **Black**, volumizing + lengthening **natural-to-full** | Opens eye |
| **Lower** | **Light** coat or none | Avoids raccoon |
| **Clumps** | **None** at production quality | QC fail |

### Lash style

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Type** | **Full strip or premium individuals** — wispy black | Editorial host |
| **Density** | **High at outer corner flare**; softer inner | Classic glamour |
| **Length** | **Long but believable** — outer longer than inner | Not anime, not spiky influencer |
| **Identity rule** | Aligns with Identity Bible: **natural-to-full mascara level** extended by **practical lash** on reference shoot — **not** spiky extensions meme |

### Brows

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shape** | **Groomed arch** locked to reference | Expression frame |
| **Fill** | **Dark brown** hair-stroke fill — structured, soft start | Full but not block |
| **Thickness** | **Full natural** — **no** microbladed extreme thickness spikes | Identity Bible |
| **Tail** | **Tapered**, not hook cartoon | Clean host read |

### Lip shape

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Geometry** | **Locked to reference** — full-natural, proportional | No AI inflation |
| **Cupid’s bow** | **Defined**, symmetrical-natural | Precision signal |
| **Border** | **Crisp vermilion** via liner blend — human, not injected | Luxury groom |

### Lip finish

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Finish** | **Soft satin to gloss** center | Hydrated, premium |
| **Not** | Matte liquid crack, ultra vinyl drip | Trend + harsh on lip lines |

### Lip color philosophy

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategy** | **Nude-plus** — brown liner perimeter, **warm peachy-nude** center | Professional, approachable |
| **Brand red** | **Not** default lip (`#EB1C24` is wardrobe/brand accent, not mouth) | Avoids sales-lip read |
| **Emotional read** | **Polished expert**, not party red (except authorized campaign exception) | Timeless host |

### Natural skin texture

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Requirement** | **Pores and micro-variation visible** at hero resolution | Anti-AI trust |
| **Retouch ceiling** | Per Identity Bible — **no** poreless Gaussian face | |
| **Shine** | **Skin radiance**, not **plastic specular** | |

### Freckles or beauty marks

| Attribute | Specification | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | **None added** unless present on approved master reference | Identity Bible |
| **Rule** | **Do not** add freckles, moles, or marks for “character” without art director sign-off | Prevents drift |

## Makeup Across Lighting Conditions

Makeup is **designed in studio reference light** and must **hold** across contexts without shifting **skin ethnicity** or **feature geometry**.

| Condition | Expected makeup read | Guardrails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Studio lighting** | Full dimension: champagne highlight, soft contour, wing crisp, lash visible | Reference truth |
| **Natural daylight** | Slightly softer contrast; **same hues** — reduce strobe in grade, **don’t** recolor skin |
| **Warm interiors** | Blush and bronzer **harmonize**; avoid orange overload | Maintain undertone |
| **Cool interiors** | Contour **not** gray; lips stay warm nude, not mauve-dead | No ash drift |
| **Editorial photography** | Slightly higher polish acceptable; **identity unchanged** | Retouch within bounds |
| **Cinematic film lighting** | Lower key OK; **eyes and lips still readable** | No “lost face” |
| **Close-ups** | Texture **must** hold; blend edges **invisible** | No patchy foundation |
| **Wide shots** | Simplified read: even skin, dark brow, wing silhouette, lip shape | Silhouette consistency |
| **Video** | **Temporal stability** — no flickering highlight or lip hue | Match still family |
| **Animation** | Simplified planes OK; **brow arch + wing + lip shape + skin tone family** locked | 2D/3D LOD rules |

### Rim and holographic lighting (PSA digital contexts)

| Phenomenon | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Cyan / magenta rim** | May kiss **skin edge and hair**; **must not** replace foundation tone or recolor lips |
| **Holographic glow** | **Additive light** on PSA embodiment — **not** makeup redesign |

### Anti-artificial finishes (all conditions)

- No **plastic** skin  
- No **single flat** foundation fill  
- No **over-whitened** sclera or teeth (Identity Bible)  
- No **gray contour mask** visible in daylight

## Makeup Continuity Standards

### Strict rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Trend cycles** | **Forbidden** — no viral makeup eras on canon PSA |
| **Over-contour** | **Forbidden** — no new cheek/jaw geometry |
| **Over-lined lips** | **Forbidden** — liner defines reference, **not** oversized pout |
| **Dramatic lashes** | **Forbidden** — no spiky clusters, anime length, bottom-heavy spider |
| **Over-mattify** | **Forbidden** — no chalk face on video |
| **Plastic skin** | **Forbidden** — poreless waxy subsurface |
| **Exaggerated highlight** | **Forbidden** — no stripe nose, disco cheek |
| **Facial character** | **Never reduce** — preserve asymmetry, natural lip/nose/eye geometry |
| **Skin texture** | **Must maintain** realistic micro-texture |
| **Eye color** | **Locked** — no colored contacts for variety |
| **Ethnicity read** | **Locked** — makeup **must not** lighten/ darken identity |

### Continuity checklist (production sign-off)

- [ ] Skin tone and undertone **match master reference**  
- [ ] Finish is **luminous satin**, not matte plastic or glass  
- [ ] Foundation **no mask line** at jaw/hairline  
- [ ] Contour **soft** — no stripe contour  
- [ ] Blush **warm peach/terracotta family**  
- [ ] Highlight **champagne gold** on approved zones only  
- [ ] Brows **arched, filled dark brown**, not block cartoon  
- [ ] Eyeshadow **warm neutral** — no fashion color story  
- [ ] Upper liner **cat-eye wing** — lifted, not downward droop  
- [ ] Lashes **full wispy**, believable length  
- [ ] Lips **nude-plus**, satin/gloss — **not** brand-red default  
- [ ] Lip volume **≤ reference** — no AI inflation  
- [ ] Teeth **natural enamel** when visible  
- [ ] **No new** freckles/moles  
- [ ] Expression change **only** — makeup **same family** across PNG slugs  
- [ ] Holographic grade **does not** shift skin or lip hue  
- [ ] Passes [`identity.md`](./identity.md) retouch boundaries

## Approved Variations

Variations adjust **intensity**, not **identity**.

| Context | May adjust | Must remain constant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily hosting** | Signature soft glam as documented | Skin tone, brow shape, wing family, lip strategy, texture |
| **Luxury events** | **+10–20%** lash, highlight, blush (`red-carpet` expression tier) | Geometry, colors family, no red lip default |
| **Formal presentations** | Slightly **matte-leaning satin** if hot lights; tighter powder T-zone | Warm undertone, nude lip |
| **Educational videos** | **Reduce** shimmer on lid if moiré; **stronger** brow for readability | Eye openness, natural skin |
| **Outdoor filming** | Softer contour/highlight; **setting** for humidity | No stripped-down “no makeup” face |
| **Evening productions** | Deeper outer-V optional; **same** lip family | No smokey black club eye |
| **Photography** | Retouch per policy | No liquify identity |
| **Video** | Slight exposure grade | Hue locked |
| **Animation** | Simplified shader | Brow + wing + lip silhouette |
| **Special campaigns** | **Founder exception only** — versioned in history | Default canon unchanged |

```
Intensity scale (canonical):

  Education / daily host  ──►  Signature (this document)
  Red carpet / event      ──►  Signature + polish bump
  Non-canon campaign      ──►  Separate deliverable — not PSA
```

## Quality Assurance Checklist

Answer **yes** before ship. Any **no** → reject or escalate.

| # | Question |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Does this **immediately** read as PSA—not a generic glam model? |
| 2 | Is makeup **supporting** the face, not overpowering it? |
| 3 | Does skin communicate **luxury** (satin radiance) and **trust** (texture)? |
| 4 | Are **skin tone and undertone** locked to reference? |
| 5 | Are **brows, eyes, lips** within signature families? |
| 6 | Is **contour/highlight** refined—not trend-extreme? |
| 7 | Are **lashes** full but **believable**? |
| 8 | Are **lips** nude-plus—not overlined or inflated? |
| 9 | Does the look hold in **intended lighting** without artificial finish? |
| 10 | Would this still feel **timeless five years from now**? |
| 11 | Is likeness preserved per [`identity.md`](./identity.md)? |
| 12 | Does this **strengthen** Frontal Slayer (education, luxury, warmth)? |
| 13 | Across expression assets, is makeup **consistent** (not per-slug redesign)? |
