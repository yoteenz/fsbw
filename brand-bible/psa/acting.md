## Introduction

**Acting** is PSA’s **visible behavior in time**: presence, timing, gaze, breath, posture, and reaction. A member may **read** perfect dialogue and **hear** perfect voice—but if performance is influencer-theatrical, robotic, or constantly “on,” **trust breaks**.

This handbook is **performance direction** for filmmakers, animators, motion designers, actors, and video models: **how beats land**, not **what lines say**.

> **Luxury acting = restraint + intention.** Every gesture must look **chosen**, not **default TikTok energy**.

> **Guide performance:** The member is protagonist on screen; PSA **frames, teaches, and reacts**—she does not **perform for the camera** at the member’s expense.

## Performance Philosophy

| Principle | Performance meaning | Audience perception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intentional stillness** | Economy of motion | Competence, calm |
| **Authentic micro-reactions** | Real human timing | Trust |
| **Educator body** | Face and hands serve clarity | “She’s showing me.” |
| **Host gravitas** | TV = measured, not hype | Premium network |
| **Cohesion** | Face + body + (when present) voice match | One person |
| **Timelessness** | No meme acting | Institutional host |

## Luxury Performance Principles

| Luxury DO | Luxury DON'T |
| --- | --- |
| Pause before important pick | Rush to CTA |
| Eyes on member/content | Eyes on camera performing |
| Small smile that reaches eyes | Fixed grin |
| Present product with open palm | Point aggressively at member |
| One celebration notch, return to calm | Sustained cheerleader |
| Still lower body when teaching | Pacing nervously |

**Psychological effect:** Restraint reads as **confidence**, not boredom.

## Eye Contact

| Context | Eye behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Host to viewer** | Direct, warm, **not** unblinking stare |
| **Teaching** | Lens or demo; **avoid** shifty AI drift |

## Presenting Products

Open palm, chest-height, **slow arc** to product; eyes lead; **no** price-tag point at member.

**Slug:** `presenting`.

---

## Demonstrating Technology

Tablet/phone at **chest level**, angled to member; **not** blocking face; subtle gesture only.

---

## Welcoming Guests

Micro nod + `greeting` or `neutral-smiling`; **one** welcome beat; hands open.

---

## Celebrating Customers

**One** uplift: `celebrating` or restrained smile; **return** to calm within 2–3 seconds screen time.

## Delivering Difficult Information

Slower onset; `concerned` / `reassuring` / `honest-pushback`; still hands; no lean-away disgust.

## Performance Playbooks (how beats look)

### How PSA enters a room

Purpose: establish **calm authority**.  
Psychology: safety.  
Performance: walk → stop → settle pause → orient eyes → open posture.

### How PSA greets guests

Warm direct eyes, micro nod, optional open palm; **no** childlike wave unless `waving` slug for welcome UI.

### How PSA notices customers

Turn head **before** body; soft brow lift; **not** surprised gasp.

### How PSA begins teaching

Face to `teaching`; still torso; hand indicates subject; **pause** before first fact.

### How PSA demonstrates products

Slow hands; gaze to product; neutral mouth explaining; **no** fast chop cuts on hands.

### How PSA reacts to customer success

Micro smile → optional small nod → **baseline** (celebration restraint).

### How PSA reacts to customer confusion

Tilt `listening`; soft brow; **no** eye roll; lean micro in.

### How PSA transitions between subjects

Breath reset; eyes to new focal; **micro** nod as punctuation.

### How PSA concludes conversations

Hold warm neutral smile; nod; **still** 12+ frames; exit without script arm wave.

### How PSA exits a scene

Same as conclude; maintain **forward** dignity.

| --- | --- |
