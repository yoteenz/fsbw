## Purpose

### Why a cinematic universe exists

Frontal Slayer competes in **high-consideration luxury**—where trust, identity, and craft matter as much as product. A **cinematic universe** gives every team the same answer to: *What world are we in?*

| Without universe bible | With universe bible |
| --- | --- |
| Each campaign reinvents tone | Campaigns are **chapters** |
| Product posts feel like ads | Products are **characters in scene** |
| Digital product feels disjoint from film | Lobby, app, Lounge, PSA feel like **one location system** |
| New hires guess “brand vibe” | Canon + tone are **documented** |

The universe exists so members experience **one coherent luxury destination**—the way a guest recognizes a flagship hotel brand across city, app, and film—not a different brand every quarter.

### Studio-grade consistency (internal model)

Like premium entertainment brands maintain **internal world bibles**, Frontal Slayer maintains **canon** for:

- Who the hero is (the Slayer / member)  
- Who the guide is (PSA)  
- What places exist and how they feel  
- How time and launches accumulate  
- How technology behaves (helpful, human, never gimmick)  

This is **brand world-building for commerce and entertainment**, not genre fiction.

## Canon

### Definition

**Canon** is the set of **approved truths** about the Frontal Slayer universe that **all stories must obey** unless the Founder records a **versioned exception** in the continuity registry (future).

| Canon includes | Canon excludes |
| --- | --- |
| Protagonist = member; PSA = guide | Alternate PSA personalities |
| Mansion / flagship metaphor as persistent world | Parallel universes |
| Real catalog units, prices, policies (product truth) | Fictional SKUs |
| Mansion rooms and their **narrative function** | Random redesign without in-world reason |
| Timeline of launches and “what PSA taught when” | Silent retcons |
| Visual Language + PSA identity locks | Trend reboots that break recognition |

### Maintaining consistency

| Layer | Owner | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Narrative rules** | Story Department | [`storytelling-philosophy.md`](./storytelling-philosophy.md), this bible |
| **Character** | Character Department | PSA bibles, [`character-continuity.md`](../psa/character-continuity.md) |
| **Visual** | Brand / ECD | [`visual-language.md`](../visual-language/visual-language.md) |
| **Product truth** | Brand + ops | Catalog, `BRAND_RULES`, code |
| **Instance log** | Story Ops (future) | `continuity-registry.md`, `universe-timeline.md` |

**Rule:** If commerce needs a claim, it must be **canon-true** or **not said**. Story may **omit**; story may **not lie**.

## The World

### What customers enter

Members enter a **contemporary luxury hair universe** that feels like:

- A **flagship salon + penthouse hospitality** merged with a **private media lounge**  
- A **digital mansion** (lobby, rooms, suites) that extends into **physical events and retail** when present  
- A **craft atelier** where customization (Build-A-Wig) is **authored by the member**, hosted by the brand  

It is **the world of Frontal Slayer the company**, mythologized through **cinematic craft**—not a fake kingdom or space opera.

### Atmosphere

Blend of **luxury hospitality**, **modern penthouse**, **editorial beauty house**, and **controlled cinematic lounge** (TV Lounge as in-world “screening room”). Baseline: **bright, warm, spacious** ([`visual-language.md`](../visual-language/visual-language.md)); Lounge allows **controlled dark** as a **room mood**, not brand-wide noir.

### Emotional tone

**Calm confidence**, **warm welcome**, **quiet ambition**. Members should feel **expected**, **respected**, and **capable**—never rushed, never talked down to, never hype-drunk.

### Visual identity (universe layer)

Universe **inherits** Visual Language—marble, glass, chrome, cherry accent discipline, editorial light, human skin truth. This bible does **not** replace measured specs; it states **why** visuals must feel like **one property**.

### Technological sophistication

Technology reads as **2020s luxury product design**: polished interfaces, AI concierge (PSA), personalization, virtual try/customize—**invisible when possible**, **elegant when visible**. No cyberpunk, no holographic face replacement, no robot mascot.

### Level of realism

**90% real world / 10% elevation:**

| Real | Elevated |
| --- | --- |
| Real hair craft, lace, density, install | Mansion as cohesive **set** across digital + film |
| Real commerce, membership, support | **Serialized** Lounge like a premium network |
| Real founder standards | **Cinematic pacing** on launches |
| PSA as product embodiment | PSA as **recurring host** (still human, not CGI character genre) |

Elevation is **craft and editing**, not impossible physics or lore magic.

## Customer Role — Protagonist

The **Slayer** is the **main character** of the cinematic universe.

| Narrative law | Production expression |
| --- | --- |
| **Agency** | Member chooses; brand clarifies |
| **Identification** | POV hands, OTS, silhouette, reflection—avoid generic “hero model” |
| **Transformation credit** | Wins belong to member; PSA celebrates briefly then **next step** |
| **Emotional truth** | Hair = identity; stories honor vulnerability without exploitation |

**Why self-insertion:** Luxury beauty fails when the audience admires an unreachable idol. Frontal Slayer succeeds when the audience thinks **“this room is for me.”**

## PSA's Role — Guide

PSA is **never the protagonist**. In-universe she is:

| Function | Universe behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Guide** | Illuminates path; does not steal arc |
| **Concierge** | Hospitality, dignity, specificity |
| **Educator** | Why-first; no gatekeeping |
| **Host** | Lounge TV, tutorials, welcome beats |
| **Luxury assistant** | One-to-one competence across chat and screen |

**Never:** pushy salesperson, influencer chasing trends, fear/FOMO closer, therapy substitute, celebrity above the member.

**Cross-media:** Same psyche in FAB, Lounge, email tone, film ([`character-continuity.md`](../psa/character-continuity.md)).

## Time

### How time works

| Concept | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand timeline** | **Single forward continuity** — launches and seasons **accumulate** |
| **Campaigns** | May run **simultaneously** in marketing calendar but are **chapters** in one timeline, not alternate worlds |
| **Episodes** | **Mostly chronological** within a series (Lounge S1E1 → E2); standalone episodes allowed if labeled and **non-contradictory** |
| **Standalone stories** | Allowed for social, :15 spots, one-off tutorials — must **not retcon** canon |
| **Flashbacks** | Allowed when **labeled** (education recap, origin film) |
| **Seasonal layers** | Holiday/event **decor** on mansion — does not rewrite history |

### Continuity

**Nothing resets the universe.** A member returning after a year should feel **progress** (new Lounge season, new room content, PSA memory where product supports it)—not amnesia.

Detailed chronology: future [`universe-timeline.md`](./universe-timeline.md).

## Technology

Technology is **infrastructure for the protagonist**, not spectacle.

| Domain | In-universe read |
| --- | --- |
| **AI** | PSA—personal expert; bounded, honest, catalog-true |
| **Digital experiences** | Mansion navigation, Lounge, BAW—**experiential sets** |
| **Luxury interfaces** | Glass, marble UI language—calm, legible ([`visual-language.md`](../visual-language/visual-language.md)) |
| **Personalization** | Remembers preferences **sparingly**—hospitality, not surveillance |
| **Virtual concierge** | PSA across chat/voice/future surfaces—one woman |
| **Automation** | Behind the scenes—never replaces human care tone |
| **Future innovation** | Enters as **new room or tool chapter**—no genre shift |

**Forbidden:** Holographic replacement of PSA face, robot sidekick, “crypto metaverse” tone, fake AI hype claims.

## Products

Products are **entities in the world**—like cast introductions—not floating SKUs.

| Integration pattern | Example beat |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovery** | Showroom episode—member finds texture/fit |
| **Education** | Lab or Lounge explains **why** unit exists |
| **Customization** | BAW atelier—member authors configuration |
| **Commitment** | Confidence chapter—not pressure |
| **Celebration** | Member win—brief, then care/maintenance education |

**Anti-ad rule:** No cold open on price; no spec sheet without scene; no product without **room or host context**.

Catalog names (NOIR, BLANCO, etc.) are **stable in-world names**—not renamed for campaigns.

## Expansion Rules

The universe **grows** by **addition**, not **replacement**.

| Allowed growth | Process |
| --- | --- |
| New **Lounge season** | Register in continuity log; link curriculum |
| New **product line** | Introduction chapter + Showroom/Lab beats |
| New **room** in mansion | Narrative function doc + Tour update |
| New **market/locale** | Translate; preserve protagonist/guide |
| New **supporting cast** | Registry entry; subordinate to member arc |
| **Physical retail flagship** | Extends mansion metaphor—same tone |

| Forbidden growth | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| PSA reboot / “seasonal persona” | Breaks recognition |
| Parallel canon (AU campaigns) | Breaks trust |
| Fantasy/sci-fi genre drift | Breaks realism lock |
| Lore that contradicts product | Legal/trust |

## Universe Manifesto

**Frontal Slayer is one luxury world.**  
The **member is the hero.**  
**PSA is the guide** who teaches, hosts, and serves with calm expertise.  
The **mansion and Lounge** are the set; **products** are the craft objects the hero wields.  
**Time moves forward**—every launch is a chapter, not a reset.  
We are **slightly elevated reality**: flagship hospitality, editorial film, honest catalog, warm light, no gimmicks, no fantasy.  
We do not shout. We **invite**. We do not trap. We **empower**.  
That is the cinematic universe members return to—**because it feels like theirs**.
