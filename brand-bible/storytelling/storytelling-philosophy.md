## Introduction

Frontal Slayer does **not** create advertisements in the traditional sense. Frontal Slayer creates **experiences** — chapters in one connected luxury universe where hair, craft, membership, and self-expression unfold over time.

Every launch film, lobby interaction, **TV Lounge** episode, tutorial, PSA conversation, email, social post, onboarding step, and product page is **story**, not collateral. The member should rarely think, “I am being marketed to.” They should think, **“I am somewhere”** — and want to see what happens next.

This document defines **how** Frontal Slayer tells stories: narrative rules, roles, continuity, and approval standards. It is **not** a campaign plan, script library, or generative prompt pack. Individual campaigns and series bibles will reference this philosophy; they may not contradict it.

> **Objective:** A **timeless storytelling framework** that still reads correctly five years from now — the narrative foundation of the Frontal Slayer cinematic universe (customer-facing world, not internal admin platform fiction).

## Narrative Philosophy

Storytelling beliefs are **non-negotiable defaults**. When commerce pressure conflicts with a row below, **story wins** unless the Founder explicitly approves a documented exception.

| Principle | Meaning | Why it exists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stories before selling** | Plot and purpose precede SKU mentions | Trust compounds; hard sell erodes luxury |
| **Experiences before transactions** | The member should *feel* arrival, exploration, mastery | Checkout is a beat, not the whole film |
| **Education before persuasion** | Teach why before ask | Frontal Slayer sells high-consideration craft |
| **Entertainment before promotion** | Value the watch/read/listen on its own | Earns attention without bait |
| **Emotion before information** | Lead with feeling, follow with facts | Hair is identity, not specs alone |
| **Trust before conversion** | Honesty, including “you may not need this” | Canon in PSA voice and brand rules |
| **Curiosity before explanation** | Invite questions; do not lecture first | Exploration drives the Mansion and Lounge |
| **World-building before product demos** | Place product inside lived space | Demos without world feel like QVC |
| **Luxury through immersion** | Pace, silence, space — hospitality pacing | Luxury is time and attention |
| **Consistency through continuity** | One timeline, one mansion, one PSA | Recognition = brand equity |

## The Customer Is The Protagonist

**Rule:** The member (the **Slayer**) is always the **main character**. Frontal Slayer never steals the hero arc for the brand, the founder, or PSA.

### Narrative implementation

The audience should **insert themselves** into the world by default.

| Technique | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Avoid showing the customer’s face** (when casting “member” POV) | Preserves universal identification |
| **First-person perspective** | UI copy, guided tours, optional POV film |
| **Over-the-shoulder framing** | Build-A-Wig, mirror moments, TV Lounge as viewer |
| **Silhouettes** | Transformation beats, arrival |
| **Reflections** | Showroom mirrors, glass — face optional |
| **Hands interacting with product** | Lace, units, tools — protagonist action without casting |

### Why this works

Identification beats aspiration casting. Luxury beauty members do not need a model to be the hero; they need to **feel authorized** to be the hero. Face-forward “customer” actors often break immersion or narrow who belongs. Protagonist grammar keeps the universe **about them**.

### Exceptions (require narrative approval)

- **Community spotlights** with consent — real members as themselves, framed as *their* chapter, not the brand hero.  
- **Testimonial documentaries** — clearly labeled, still subordinate to member agency.

## PSA Is The Guide

PSA (Personal Slay Assistant) is **never the protagonist**. She is the **trusted guide** — luxury concierge, educator, mentor, host, expert who helps the member win.

| PSA is | PSA is not |
| --- | --- |
| Guide, host, teacher, concierge | Hero of the member’s story |
| Founder-aligned standard-bearer | Influencer chasing trends |
| Warm expert | Celebrity above the member |
| Network host (**Lounge TV**) | Sales closer |

### Relationship grammar

- **Member decides; PSA clarifies.**  
- **Member transforms; PSA illuminates path.**  
- **Member earns confidence; PSA removes gatekeeping.**

Visual and voice consistency: [`../psa/identity.md`](../psa/identity.md), [`../psa/design-principles.md`](../psa/design-principles.md), `api/_lib/psaInstructions.ts` (behavioral voice — cross-reference only).

Across chat, TV, email sign-offs, and film, PSA’s **screen time** may be high, but **story function** remains secondary to the member’s arc.

## The Mansion Is A Character

The Frontal Slayer **digital mansion** (lobby, rooms, suites — including onboarding **Mansion Tour** in product) is not wallpaper. It is a **recurring character**: stable, evolving, believable luxury architecture — **never fantasy castle**, always **hospitality-real**.

Each room must have:

| Dimension | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Why the member is here (shop, learn, watch, customize, celebrate) |
| **Emotional identity** | Calm, excitement, intimacy, prestige — one dominant mood |
| **Atmosphere** | Light, material, sound pacing per Visual Language |
| **Personality** | Reception feels welcoming; Lab feels precise; Lounge feels cinematic |
| **Pacing** | Fast commerce rooms vs slow education rooms — intentional |
| **Visual language** | Marble/glass/chrome DNA — see Visual Language handbook |
| **Narrative function** | What chapter of the journey this room advances |

The mansion **remembers** continuity: a member returning should feel **progress**, not a reset landing page.

> **Alive, not fantastical:** No magic portals, no cartoon geography. Elevation through **craft and service**, not genre fiction.

## Episodic Storytelling

All content types are **episodes** in one continuity:

| Content type | Episodic role |
| --- | --- |
| **Launch films** | Season premieres — establish stakes, do not reboot lore |
| **Reception / Lobby** | Pilot + daily return — orientation and invitation |
| **Hair Showroom** | Discovery episodes — product as character introduction |
| **Hair Analysis Lab** | Investigation beats — science as plot |
| **Build-A-Wig Atelier** | Creation arc — choice, preview, commitment |
| **Transformation Suite** | Change chapter — before/after emotional peak |
| **TV Lounge** | Serialized originals — PSA-hosted programming grid |
| **Rewards Gallery** | Recognition chapter — loyalty as story reward |
| **Founder Suite** | Occasional authority beat — rare, not weekly hero |
| **PSA conversations** | Side episodes — personalized B-plots |
| **Email / social** | Short scenes — recap, tease, deepen |
| **Tutorials** | Skill episodes — numbered progression where applicable |
| **Product launches** | New character enters universe — backstory in-world |
| **Customer education** | Curriculum season — references prior rooms |

**Rule:** Nothing feels like a **disconnected microsite**. Campaign URLs are **doors into the mansion**, not alternate universes.

## Educational Entertainment

Frontal Slayer teaches through **edutainment** — education embedded in exploration, never a lecture bolted onto an ad.

| Traditional failure | Frontal Slayer approach |
| --- | --- |
| Tutorial interrupts story | Lesson **is** the scene (PSA in Lab, host in Lounge) |
| Spec sheet front-load | Discovery → question → answer |
| “Buy now” before why | **Why** creates desire aligned with trust |
| Jargon | Plain luxury language — expert but human |

### Transformations

| Format | Story shape |
| --- | --- |
| **Tutorials** | Conversations with PSA or host — member question drives beat |
| **Product education** | Showroom / atelier discovery — touch, compare, preview |
| **Hair science** | Lab curiosity — “what happens if…” |
| **Purchasing** | Confidence chapter — member chooses with agency |

Learning outcomes should map to **emotional journey** stages (trust, confidence), not only feature checklists.

## Emotional Journey

Every touchpoint should advance or reinforce one or more stages:

| Stage | Member feeling | Story tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Curiosity** | “What is this place?” | Lobby scale, mystery of rooms, teaser cuts |
| **Wonder** | “This is unlike anywhere else” | Architecture, film, Lounge premiere |
| **Comfort** | “I belong here” | Warm light, PSA tone, hospitality pacing |
| **Trust** | “They will not play me” | Honest PSA, no fake urgency, real catalog |
| **Discovery** | “I found something for me” | Showroom, search, recommendations |
| **Confidence** | “I can do this” | Education, Build-A-Wig clarity, tutorials |
| **Excitement** | “I want this moment” | Event, launch, transformation |
| **Empowerment** | “This is my choice” | Protagonist framing, no pressure close |
| **Belonging** | “I am part of something” | Rewards, community spotlights, membership |
| **Loyalty** | “I return by choice” | Continuity, memory (PSA recall), new seasons |

Environments carry emotion (Visual Language); dialogue carries emotion (Dialogue Philosophy); **pacing** carries emotion — luxury allows pauses.

## Campaign Architecture

Campaigns are **chapters**, not resets.

| Campaign type | Narrative rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Grand Opening** | Origin chapter — establishes mansion; later campaigns reference “since opening” |
| **New product releases** | New cast member (unit/texture) enters existing world |
| **Holiday events** | Seasonal **decor layer** on mansion — does not rewrite identity |
| **Member experiences** | Side quests for members — deepen belonging |
| **Rewards campaigns** | Payoff chapters — recognition, not bribery tone |
| **Educational series** | Season with episode numbers — Lounge + Lab alignment |
| **Founder messages** | Rare fireside — authority, not daily protagonist |
| **Seasonal updates** | World **evolves** — new room content, not new universe |
| **Community spotlights** | Anthology episodes — member as hero |

**Nothing resets the universe.** Assets may archive; **continuity memory** remains (what launched when, what PSA taught before).

## Dialogue Philosophy

Spoken and written copy (scripts, PSA, UI microcopy with narrative weight) follows **luxury hospitality dialogue**:

| Quality | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| **Warm** | Person-to-person, not ticket system |
| **Patient** | Space for member pace; no interrupting |
| **Knowledgeable** | Specific, catalog-true |
| **Professional** | No slang chaos; no corporate script |
| **Never pushy** | Trust over sales (brand rule) |
| **Never overly scripted** | Natural variation; avoid repeated filler phrases |
| **Never exaggerated hype** | No “OMG BEST EVER” energy |
| **Silence allowed** | Pauses in film; short replies in chat |
| **Curiosity invited** | Questions open doors |
| **Discovery rewarded** | Answers feel earned |

Dialogue **supports immersion** when it sounds like a **five-star concierge who respects you**, not a conversion bot.

**PSA-specific:** Uppercase display in app is a **product convention**; narrative intent remains human (see voice docs).

## Cinematic Story Structure

Recurring **beat patterns** for long and short form:

| Beat | Function | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Arrival** | Orient; establish luxury and safety | Reception, app open, film open |
| **Exploration** | Curiosity; room-to-room | Mansion tour, lobby scroll |
| **Discovery** | Find fit, find lesson | Showroom, search, Lounge browse |
| **Learning** | Skill + trust | Tutorial, PSA, Lab |
| **Transformation** | Emotional peak | Install, reveal, makeover |
| **Celebration** | Joy without cringe | Order complete, reward unlock |
| **Reflection** | Integrate meaning | “This is me now” moment |
| **Invitation to continue** | Sequel hook | Next episode, next room, return tomorrow |

Short-form (15s social) may use **two beats** (Arrival + Invitation). Long-form (launch film) runs full chain. **TV Lounge** episodes typically: Discovery → Learning → Invitation.

## Continuity Standards

| Rule | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Characters** | PSA likeness/voice/role consistent; founder rare; catalog units real and stable |
| **Environments** | Mansion rooms evolve; no random redesign without in-world reason |
| **Products** | NOIR, BLANCO, textures, etc. appear as established entities — no fictional SKUs |
| **Timeline** | Story progression **chronological** unless labeled flashback |
| **Visual continuity** | Visual Language + PSA bibles honored |
| **Education** | Reference prior lessons when advanced (“remember lace buffer…”) |
| **No contradictions** | Lore, pricing claims, capability claims match product truth |
| **No disconnected campaigns** | Campaign creative lives **in** mansion metaphor |
| **No unnecessary retcons** | Fix errors; do not rewrite history for convenience |

When product changes (new unit), story adds **chapter**, not **rewrite**.

## Immersion Standards

### Avoid (break immersion)

- Breaking the fourth wall without purpose (“buy now because quarterly goals”)  
- Traditional ad tropes (fake countdown, screaming announcer)  
- Hard sales language and artificial urgency  
- Clickbait thumbnails/copy  
- Generic stock narratives unrelated to mansion  

### Prioritize

- **Natural discovery** — member finds, PSA does not shove  
- **Conversation** — chat, host, email as dialogue  
- **Hospitality** — service rhythm  
- **Exploration** — rooms, rails, episodes  
- **Luxury pacing** — breath between beats  
- **Authenticity** — honest limits, real outcomes

## Storytelling Decision Framework

Approve content only when checks pass. **Two or more failures** → revise or escalate.

### Universe & journey

- [ ] Does this **strengthen the Frontal Slayer universe**?  
- [ ] Would this still make sense **mid-journey** for a returning member?  
- [ ] Does this **deepen the world** rather than only advertise a product?

### Roles

- [ ] Is the **customer still the hero/protagonist**?  
- [ ] Does **PSA remain the guide**, not the hero or influencer?

### Craft

- [ ] Does this **educate naturally** inside the scene?  
- [ ] Does this feel **immersive** (not ad-like)?  
- [ ] Would this still work **five years from now**?

### Engagement

- [ ] Does this **invite continued exploration** (next room, next episode)?  
- [ ] Aligned with **Visual Language** and **trust-over-sales** voice?

> **Hierarchy on conflict:** Storytelling Philosophy → Visual Language → channel brief → asset.
