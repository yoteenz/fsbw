## Film Intent

**One paragraph intent:**  
Film I is the **opening chapter** of a luxury cinematic franchise: a guest **discovers a threshold**, **pauses**, and **chooses** to approach; the world **recognizes** her and **opens** (not for business hours—for **her arrival**); she **crosses**, **breathes** inside a **partial** property, **glimpses** depth and **craft seriousness**, and **stops** on the edge of **what she has not yet seen**—so the audience leaves asking **What is Frontal Slayer?** and **needing Film II**.

**Intent is not:** Sell hair, explain the company, complete the hero journey, or close the season arc.

**Success intent:** Screenplay reads as **hosted world entry**, not **spot** or **store tour**.

## Audience Promise

| We promise the viewer | We do not promise |
| --- | --- |
| A **place** worth returning to | A **deal** or **SKU** |
| **Ceremony** without gimmick | A **twist villain** or **conflict** |
| **Host** (PSA) as guide | PSA **closing** the sale |
| **More world** than one room | Full **map** or **lore bible** |
| **Film II** will matter | **Self-contained** ad satisfaction |
| **Mute legibility** of OPEN myth | VO **explaining** the brand |

**Broken promise = failed draft** regardless of prose quality.

## PSA Objectives

Execution refs Character Department; **dramatic** obligations for writer:

- **Calm authority**—movement **unhurried**.  
- **Eye line** = **respect**, not **wallet**.  
- **OPEN** action = **world state change for guest**—writer must stage so **mute audience** understands **not retail hours**.  
- **No** product pitch, **no** membership mention, **no** “welcome to our store” literalism.  
- **Screen time:** Guide **visible**; protagonist frame **protected** post-threshold.

*(Audience-as-guest meta-objectives the screenplay must serve)*

- Feel **invited**, not **targeted**.  
- Feel **smart** for noticing details—not **confused** by lore dumps.  
- Feel **pause is honored**—pacing **permission**.  
- Feel **Film II is necessary**—not **optional sequel bait**.

## Beginning Requirements

**Narrative function:** NOTICE → PAUSE → start APPROACH ([`film-i-narrative-design-bible.md`](./film-i-narrative-design-bible.md)).

| Must include | Must exclude |
| --- | --- |
| Pattern break vs generic ad | Logo **manifesto** |
| **Threshold** introduced | Interior **payoff** |
| Guest **pause** mirrored | Price, promo, **SKU** |
| **Pull** toward interior | PSA **pitch** |
| Emotional phases: **Armor → Arrest → Pull** | Dialogue **explaining** FS |

**Approximate weight:** ~20–25% of total dramatic spine—**ECD may adjust** in edit, not **collapse** pause.

## Middle Requirements

**Narrative function:** HOST RECOGNITION → OPEN → CROSS → GLIMPSE DEPTH.

| Must include | Must exclude |
| --- | --- |
| **Required turning points** (see below) | Product **montage** |
| **Primary emotional peak** at OPEN | **B-plot** |
| Guest **inside** partial interior | Full **tour** |
| **One** strong depth glimpse | **Five** equal rooms |
| Trust **seed** | **Membership** |

**Approximate weight:** ~50%—peak **centered**.

## Required Turning Points

**Non-optional dramatic state changes:**

1. **Guest commits attention** (notice → pause).  
2. **Guest commits approach** (pause → threshold).  
3. **Host commits recognition** (awareness of guest).  
4. **World commits OPEN** (state change—not schedule).  
5. **Guest commits cross** (entry).  
6. **World commits depth reveal** (one axis).  
7. **Story commits stop** (forward vector without completion).

Missing **#4** or **#7** = **structural fail**.

## Dialogue Philosophy

| Rule | Writer application |
| --- | --- |

## Scene Philosophy

**Scenes are **containers for state change**, not locations for dialogue.**

| Good scene | Bad scene |
| --- | --- |
| **One** dramatic turn | **Multiple** turns + filler |
| **Clear** emotional phase | **Orphan** beauty |
| **Exit** different from **entry** | **Same** curiosity level |
| **Hospitality** or **wonder** job | **Exposition** job |

**Scene count:** **Low**—prefer **few long breaths** over **many choppy** retail beats. ECD expects **feature-short** density, not **TV ad** count.

**Do not** deliver scene list in this packet.

---

## Transition Philosophy

**Transitions = belief change**, not just location change.

- After OPEN: **allow breath** before montage impulse.  
- **Interior progression:** **One vector** (deeper in)—not **random** room hops.  
- **Time:** Continuous **single arrival** evening—**no** time jumps without ECD approval.

---

## Pacing Philosophy

| Zone | Pacing |
| --- | --- |
| Open | **Slow** — honor armor drop |
| Pre-OPEN | **Hold** — do not **cut** ritual for “retention” |
| Post-OPEN | **Breath**, then **moderate** discovery |
| End | **Slow sustained** — longing needs **time** |

**Hero target length band:** Communicate with production—**draft** prioritizes **phase timing** over page count; typical hero **~90 seconds–3 minutes** editorial band **unless** ECD expands for YouTube master—**one spine** regardless.

## Success Criteria

Draft **succeeds** when:

| Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Table read** | Listeners ask **what is FS**—not **what does it cost** |
| **Mute view** | OPEN myth **clear** |
| **Spine** | No **missing** turning point |
| **Emotion** | Honor peak **felt** |
| **End** | **Longing**—immediate **want** for Film II |
| **Experience** | **Guest**, not **shopper** |
| **Production** | Clear **dramatic** staging without **shot list** in script |

## Failure Indicators

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| “Great ad script” | Rebuild as **scene philosophy** |
| Explains OPEN in dialogue | Cut / restage |
| PSA closes sale | Reassign guide function |
| Ends on product | Re-end on **vector** |
| Too many scenes | Consolidate **state changes** |
| Retail hours read | Restage **ritual** |
