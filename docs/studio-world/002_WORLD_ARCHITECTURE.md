# 002 — World Architecture™

**Status:** Canonical — Part 1 of Studio World Bible  
**Scope:** Physical organization of Studio World™ headquarters

---

## Architecture Thesis

Studio World™ is one **connected campus** — a persistent headquarters universe organized in zones of access, function, and narrative. The founder walks; they do not navigate menus.

Every destination resolves to a physical address. No orphan features. No floating UI.

---

## Campus Containment Model

```
STUDIO WORLD™ (universe)
└── COMPANY™ (organizational root)
    └── HEADQUARTERS™ (single connected property)
        ├── EXECUTIVE HEADQUARTERS (restricted · founder-primary)
        ├── DEPARTMENT WINGS (operational · founder + staff)
        ├── CREATIVE FLOORS (production-intent · high activity)
        ├── MEETING ROOMS (distributed · scheduled)
        ├── HALLWAYS & CIRCULATION (connective tissue)
        ├── PUBLIC AREAS (arrival · orientation · visitor)
        ├── RESTRICTED AREAS (canon · legal · vault)
        └── AI-ONLY AREAS (background operations · rarely visited)
```

## Physical Organization Laws

1. **One campus** — all companies have one headquarters property (expression varies)
2. **No teleport without transition** — walking · elevator · corridor
3. **Adjacency implies relationship** — creative near creative · production south
4. **Vertical scale implies hierarchy** — executive above · production below
5. **Glass implies connection** — never isolated full-screen rooms
6. **Restricted implies weight** — canon · legal · chronicle
7. **Public implies curation** — visitor-appropriate narrative
8. **AI-only implies background** — outcomes visible · process hidden
