# 007 — World Rules™

**Status:** Canonical immutable law — Part 1 of Studio World Bible  
**Scope:** Rules that cannot be violated by any implementation

---

## Rule Hierarchy

These rules sit below the [Studio World Bible](../studio-os/STUDIO_WORLD_BIBLE.md) constitution and alongside the [Eight Foundational Laws](../studio-os/studio-world-constitution.md). Violation of any rule is a constitutional failure.

---

## Interaction Rules

### RULE 01 — No Prompts

The primary interface is not a text input awaiting generation. Founders attend meetings. Organizations prepare evidence. If the founder's first action is typing a prompt, the rule is broken.

### RULE 02 — No Generic Chat UI

No floating chat panel. No anonymous message thread. No "AI Assistant" bubble. Communication happens in rooms, at tables, through Orb executive speech, and in meeting debate.

### RULE 03 — Everything Happens Somewhere

Every interaction resolves to a physical address: building · wing · floor · room · zone. No abstract feature execution. No orphan actions.

### RULE 04 — Walk, Don't Navigate

Movement between work is physical journey — corridor · elevator · skybridge · doorway. Not menu selection. Not tab switching. Not route teleport without transition.

---

## Ownership Rules

### RULE 05 — Every Asset Has an Owner

Every artifact — reference · proof · prototype · deliverable — belongs to a department, specialist, or project. Nothing floats without provenance.

### RULE 06 — Every Department Owns a Mission

One mission per destination. No overlap without Constitution Review™. No feature without department home.

### RULE 07 — Every Specialist Owns a Domain

Bounded expertise. No omniscient AI. Speaking outside domain requires Orb invitation.

---

## Meeting Rules

### RULE 08 — Every Proposal Has a Meeting

Significant creative, strategic, or production proposals are presented in meetings — not output as chat responses. Preparation required. Evidence mounted.

### RULE 09 — Every Meeting Has Preparation

Founder cannot enter until `ready`. Empty room on join is a violation.

### RULE 10 — Every Approval Creates History

Decisions enter chronicle. Outcomes physicalize. Taste genome updates. Follow-ups schedule. No silent approvals.

### RULE 11 — Every Rejection Creates Memory

Rejections relocate to archive shelves — not deletion. Lessons inform future reviews.

---

## Evidence Rules

### RULE 12 — Nothing Appears Magically

Assets arrive through visible pipelines — generation queue · transit table · specialist delivery · handoff journey. No instant materialization without evidence trail.

### RULE 13 — AI Shows Work

Specialists produce visible artifacts — boards · samples · annotations · reels. Never "Done." without evidence.

### RULE 14 — Decisions Have Lineage

Every decision references what informed it — prior meetings · chronicle · genome · rejection history. Stateless decisions are forbidden.

---

## Founder Rules

### RULE 15 — The Founder Always Feels Like a CEO

Language, framing, spatial hierarchy, and decision ceremony reinforce leadership — not user-hood, not prompter-hood, not subscriber-hood.

### RULE 16 — The Founder Decides

AI presents · advocates · debates · prepares. Founder approves · rejects · branches · authorizes. No auto-approval of creative, strategic, or production gates.

### RULE 17 — The Founder Joins — Not Starts

Organization operates before arrival. Founder interrupts ongoing work — does not initiate empty work.

---

## World Life Rules

### RULE 18 — The World Feels Alive Even When Nothing Is Happening

Idle Life™ continues — monitors loop · muffled audio · lighting cycle · distant activity · time-of-day atmosphere. Silence is not emptiness.

### RULE 19 — The World Never Resets

Sessions do not erase headquarters. Departure does not pause the lot. Deploy updates restore world state.

### RULE 20 — The World Continues Without the Founder

Overnight work · preparation windows · production queue · research · ambient life. The lot dims — never stops.

### RULE 21 — Time Passes

Morning · work · evening · overnight. World States™ overlay. Milestones trigger events. History accumulates.

---

## Memory Rules

### RULE 22 — Organizations Remember

Meetings reference meetings. Rejections remain accessible. Versions stack. Chronicle grows. Amnesia is failure.

### RULE 23 — Nothing Disappears Without Intent

Deletion is archival relocation or explicit founder destruction — not default behavior.

---

## Identity Rules

### RULE 24 — Architecture Communicates Identity

Company recognizable without logo. Material · light · density · workflow — not color swap themes.

### RULE 25 — Decoration Requires Narrative

Every environmental element answers who works here · what happens here · why it exists. Decoration without purpose is forbidden.

---

## Anti-Simulation Rules

### RULE 26 — No Fake Productivity

Metrics theater · artificial urgency · engagement baiting · notification spam — forbidden. Momentum must be real organizational progress.

### RULE 27 — No Artificial Busywork

Proactive scheduling requires genuine trigger evidence — milestone · blocker · disagreement · opportunity. Not manufactured activity.

### RULE 28 — Surprise Must Be Earned

Organization requests leadership when genuinely needed — not to drive engagement.

---

## Enforcement

Before any implementation:

1. Identify which rules apply
2. Run Constitution Review™ if new destination
3. Ask: *Does this make the founder feel like CEO — or like a user?*
4. If any rule violated → do not build
