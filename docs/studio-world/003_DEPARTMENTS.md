**Status:** Canonical — Part 1 of Studio World Bible  
**Scope:** Every governed department in Studio World™

---

## Department Schema

Every department in this document includes:

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Why the department exists |
| **Responsibilities** | What it owns |
| **Deliverables** | What it produces |
| **Meeting types** | Reviews it originates or hosts |
| **Participants** | AI specialists · founder role |
| **Inputs** | What it receives |
| **Outputs** | What it hands off |
| **Relationships** | Upstream · downstream · lateral |

**Law:** A department is a destination with a mission — not a feature category.
