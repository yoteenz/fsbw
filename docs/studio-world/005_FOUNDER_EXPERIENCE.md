# 005 — Founder Experience™

**Status:** Canonical — Part 1 of Studio World Bible  
**Scope:** How founders operate a company inside Studio World™

---

## Experience Thesis

The founder does not prompt. The founder **operates** — books meetings (via organizational calendar), attends reviews, walks departments, approves work, requests revisions, and signs production authorization.

Everything feels like running a real company from inside a living headquarters.

---

## Founder Identity

| Attribute | Definition |
|-----------|--------|
| **Title** | CEO / Creative Director of their company |
| **Authority** | Sole approval · direction lock · production authorization · canon |
| **Movement** | Walk · enter · join · point · approve · depart |
| **Communication** | Executive speech to Orb · gestures · meeting direction |

## The Founder Day

### 1 — Arrival

Founder enters headquarters — not a login screen.

- Grand Atrium™ or last known location restored (World Persistence™)
- Overnight progress visible — monitors · new proofs · prepared rooms
- Orb approaches with orientation
- No empty state · no "What would you like to do?"

*Feeling:* *"My company was already working."*

### 2 — Morning Executive Briefing™

First ritual. Orb projects:

- Overnight accomplishments by department
- Decision queue — meetings ready · gates blocked
- Today's calendar — scheduled rituals
- Risks · disagreements needing arbitration
- Recommended priority

*Feeling:* *"I know what needs my leadership."*

### 3 — Calendar-Driven Prioritization

Founder selects from organizational schedule — not feature menu:

| Priority signal | Action |
|-----------------|--------|
| Meeting `ready` | Walk to prepared room |
| Production `blocked` | Attend Production Readiness |
| Disagreement `unresolved` | Founder Boardroom arbitration |
| Creative branch `needs direction` | Walk to Creative Atelier sandbox |
| No urgent queue | Walk the Business™ · deep creative stewardship |

### 4 — Attending Reviews

For each meeting:

1. **Transition** — corridor · elevator · doorway (not route change)
2. **Enter active room** — specialists mid-discussion · evidence mounted
3. **Orb frames** — agenda · expected decisions · prior meeting refs
4. **Watch presentation** — boards · reels · samples · prototypes
5. **Hear debate** — specialists advocate · respectful disagreement
6. **Direct** — ask · point · clarify · decide
7. **Outcome physicalizes** — vault · shelf · branch · production signal
8. **Depart** — meeting remnants persist · follow-ups scheduled

*Feeling:* *"I walked into a meeting that was ready for me."*

### 5 — Walking Departments

Between meetings, founder moves through campus:

- **Creative Atelier™** — pin references · review mood wall · check sandbox branches
- **Brand Strategy Library** — review positioning · genome alignment
- **Packaging Lab** — handle material samples · tactile evaluation
- **Production Floor** — observe manufacture · check transit artifacts
- **Asset Registry** — browse catalog · retrieve versions
- **Archives** — revisit rejections · compare history

Walking is not tourism — it is **executive oversight through presence**.

### 6 — Approving Work

Approval is a **ceremony** — not a button:

| Approval type | Experience |
|---------------|------------|
| Direction lock | Creative Review → vault ceremony → lighting shift |
| Packaging | Tactile table sign-off → material to production |
| Production authorization | Readiness theater → founder gesture → warehouse activates |
| Launch | Campaign War Room → launch decision → World State™ Launch Day™ |
| Canon | Vault placement → permanent wall migration |

### 7 — Requesting Revisions

Founder does not re-prompt. Founder **directs in context**:

- Points at specific object on board
- Verbal direction captured by Founder Memory Concierge™
- Revision spec pinned to artifact
- Follow-up meeting scheduled if needed
- Branch spawned if exploration required

*"Warmer lighting on the hero — keep everything else"* — not a new generation request.

### 8 — Production Authorization

No production begins without founder authorization at Production Readiness Review:

- Production Director presents evidence
- QA presents compliance
- Legal presents clearance (if material)
- Creative Director confirms direction alignment
- Founder authorizes → transit artifact dispatched → Foundry queue activates

### 9 — Departure

Founder leaves — no logout anxiety:

- Work remains on tables
- Organization continues overnight
- Orb: *"I'll continue research on motion references"*
- Lighting shifts to evening
- Calendar prepares tomorrow's windows

*Feeling:* *"The company keeps running."*
