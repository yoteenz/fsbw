# 06 — Backgrounds

## Master background (locked)

- **Name:** Pure White Studio
- **Target:** RGB 255,255,255 in master PNG/TIFF exports
- **Set:** Seamless white cyclorama or digital equivalent

## Channel-specific backgrounds

Marble, acrylic, and environmental treatments belong in **marketing crops** (email, advertising, StudioOS preview) — not in the locked 4096×4096 master.

## Media kit folders

- `hero-portrait/` — white studio master
- `advertising/` — composited / environmental (future)
- `studioos-preview/` — admin preview crops

See [11-media-kit.md](./11-media-kit.md).
