# 04 — Composition

## Master frame (1:1)

- Product occupies **55–65%** of frame height at bust crown through mid-length fall.
- Vertical centerline aligned to bust center.
- Breathing room above crown; minimal floor shadow if any.

| Three Quarter Portrait | Editorial, campaigns |
