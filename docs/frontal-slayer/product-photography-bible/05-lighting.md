# 05 — Lighting

## Studio lighting (locked — V1.0)

- **Key:** Soft large-source, 45° front-left, eye level.
- **Fill:** Controlled bounce, shadow detail preserved in lace.
- **Rim:** Subtle separation on hair edge; no harsh halo.
- **Background:** Even pure white, no gradient falloff on master.

## Prohibited

- Colored gels on product masters.
- Heavy beauty retouch that alters texture truth.
- Mixed color temperatures.

## Status

**Locked** in Photography System V1.0. Updates ship as V1.1+ only.
