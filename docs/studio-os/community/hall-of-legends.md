# Hall of Legends™

**Every Guild preserves its greatest work forever**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical legacy architecture (docs only)  
**Parent:** [Guild System™](./README.md)

---

## Purpose

Every Guild maintains a **Hall of Legends™** — permanent exhibits of the greatest work in that craft inside Studio World™.

Legendary work is never forgotten.

---

## Core Law

```
THE GREATEST CRAFT BECOMES PERMANENT EXHIBIT — NOT ARCHIVED DATA
```

---

---

## Induction Criteria

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Craft excellence** | Studio Certified™ + seasonal award |
| **Founder impact** | Documented company outcomes |
| **Originality** | Genre-defining work |
| **Longevity** | Active installs · years of use |
| **Community vote** | Guild Council™ + editorial — not pure popularity |
| **Cross-guild respect** | Optional peer recognition |

Induction is **rare** — prestige requires scarcity.

---

## Exhibit Experience

Legends are **walkable** — not thumbnails:

```
Enter Hall of Legends™ wing in Guild Hall™
        ↓
Arrival Sequence™ — ceremony lighting
        ↓
Plinth / portal — enter legendary HQ · brand · pipeline
        ↓
Story panel — architect · year · founder impact
        ↓
Orb narration — guild history context
```

Aligns with [Archive Integration™](../world/archive-integration.md) — legends are **curated immortality**.

## Relationship to Hall of Legacy™

| System | Scope |
|--------|-------|
| **Hall of Legends™** | Guild craft immortality — professional society |
| **Hall of Legacy™** | Company milestones — founder org memory |
| **The Archive™** | Full version history |

Legends are **greatest hits** — curated · ceremonial · public.
