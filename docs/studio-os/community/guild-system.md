## Guild Membership

| Rule | Law |
|------|-----|
| **Primary guild** | Matches primary Studio Professional™ discipline |
| **Admission** | Declare profession · publish or certify |
| **Dual guild** | Future professions only — one primary |
| **Founders** | May visit Guild Halls™ · not members by default |
| **Honorary** | Platform may honor cross-discipline contributors |

---

## The Purpose of a Guild™

Guilds are places where professionals:

| Activity | Guild role |
|----------|------------|
| **Learn** | Knowledge Library™ · masterclasses |
| **Teach** | Lecture Theater™ · workshops |
| **Collaborate** | Community Projects™ |
| **Mentor** | Mentorship Lounge™ |
| **Compete** | Design Competitions™ |
| **Experiment** | Innovation Lab™ |
| **Publish** | Community Gallery™ |
| **Review** | Live Critiques™ |
| **Innovate** | Challenge Board™ |
| **Celebrate** | Award Hall™ · Hall of Excellence™ |

**Detail:** [guild-halls.md](./guild-halls.md) · [guild-activities.md](./guild-activities.md)

## Guild Governance (Philosophy)

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| **Guild Council™** | Certified Masters + Legends — standards |
| **Editorial Board™** | Seasonal awards · Editor's Choice |
| **Mentorship Council™** | Mentor matching · ethics |
| **Innovation Chair™** | Challenge themes · hackathons |

Not democratic chaos — **institutional stewardship**.

## Orb & Guilds

The Orb understands Guild history and notifies members:

> *"The Architects' Guild has nominated your Headquarters for this season's awards."*

> *"Your Innovation Lab has been selected as an Editor's Choice."*

> *"You've been invited to mentor Emerging Architects."*

Founders and professionals feel they belong to a **living professional society**.
