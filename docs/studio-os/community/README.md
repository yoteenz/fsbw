# Guild System™

**The Professional Societies of Studio World™**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical community architecture (docs only)  
**Phase:** Community evolution (post-Studio Professionals™)  
**Scope:** Guild institutions · culture · mentorship · excellence

---

> **Studio World™ is not populated by anonymous users. It is populated by professionals who belong to Guilds.**

---

## Mission

We have established [Studio Professionals™](../ecosystem/README.md).

Now we establish **where those professionals belong**.

Studio OS should not feel like a freelance marketplace.

It should feel like an **ecosystem of respected professions**.

Every profession belongs to a **Guild™**.

Guilds become the **cultural backbone** of Studio World™.

---

## One Sentence

A **Guild™** is an institution that preserves craft knowledge · recognizes excellence · develops standards · mentors newcomers · and pushes an entire Studio Professional™ discipline forward.

## Guild vs Group

| Group (wrong) | Guild™ (right) |
|---------------|----------------|
| Chat channel | **Institution** |
| Follower list | **Professional society** |
| Seller category | **Craft preservation** |
| Forum | **Guild Hall™** — living headquarters |

Guilds are **not groups**. Guilds are **institutions**.
