# Visual DNA™ — Company Aesthetic Identity

**Module:** `studio.creative-blueprint.v1.visual-dna`  
**Status:** Canonical aesthetic archetype layer

---

## Definition

**Visual DNA™** is the top of the creative hierarchy — the recognizable aesthetic identity every company develops.

Every future visual decision references Visual DNA™ **first**.

---

## Visual DNA™ vs Company DNA™

| Term | Layer | Nature |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Visual DNA™** | Creative Blueprint Engine™ | **Intentional** design identity archetype |
| **Company DNA™** | Asset Intelligence Engine™ | **Emergent** coherence measured from approved assets + reuse |

Visual DNA™ is chosen and evolved. Company DNA™ is measured and protected. They reinforce each other.

---

## Canonical Archetypes (Launch)

| Archetype | Character |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Editorial Luxury™** | High-fashion atelier · bronze · smoked glass · warm editorial light |
| **Modern Scandinavian™** | Pale wood · soft daylight · minimal ornament |
| **Japanese Minimal™** | Restraint · natural materials · shadow discipline |
| **Neo Futurism™** | Glass · holographic accents · cool volumetrics |
| **Industrial Loft™** | Exposed structure · metal · practical light |
| **Luxury Hospitality™** | Warm welcome · rich materials · layered atmosphere |
| **Executive Modern™** | Confident · dark stone · precise lighting |
| **Art Deco™** | Geometry · brass · dramatic contrast |
| **Organic Wellness™** | Natural tones · soft light · botanical motion |

Archetypes are **starting points** — companies may blend · evolve · fork.

## Selection Timing

| Moment | Visual DNA™ Role |
|--------|------------------|
| Company founding | Recommended from Mode™ · Industry · Founder Taste |
| First Golden Build™ | Locked or confirmed |
| New department | Inherited default |
| Expedition milestone | May declare DNA chapter transition |
| Founder evolution | Gradual archetype shift · not sudden regen |

---

## Decision Filter

Before any creative operation:

```
Does this strengthen Visual DNA™?
Does this conflict with active Blueprint™?
Does this introduce a third material family without intent?
```

Asset Intelligence drift warnings reference Visual DNA™ rules.

---

## Multi-Blueprint Companies

Mature companies may run **primary + accent** blueprints:

```
Visual DNA™: Editorial Luxury™
├── Primary: Editorial Luxury Blueprint™ (CDS · Executive · HQ)
└── Accent: Neo Futurism Blueprint™ (Innovation Lab only)
```

Accent blueprints must declare **relationship** to primary (contrast · extension · seasonal).

---

## Learning

Founder Taste Engine™ refines Visual DNA™ over time:

| Signal | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Repeated approvals of warm bronze | Strengthen materialPhilosophy |
| Rejections of cool slate | Add to soft forbidden |
| Branch explorations | Candidate variant blueprints |

Studio OS understands aesthetic **before** generation begins.
