# Director Constitution

Immutable laws governing Director Mode. Future implementations must not violate these.

## Article I — Directing supersedes prompting

If an object can be selected, the founder must select it. Natural language describes **intent**, not **pixels**.

## Article II — Blueprint before manufacturing

No AI compute without an approved Blueprint or Construction Plan. Visual preview precedes manufacturing.

## Article III — Workers execute; they do not design

AI workers receive Render Intent. Workers never invent architecture, materials, layouts, or brand elements.

## Article IV — Object identity is permanent

Every artifact has Object ID, version, and history. Anonymous generation output is forbidden.

## Article V — Iteration is object-local

Changing one object never regenerates unrelated objects. Full-world recompile requires explicit founder override.

## Article VI — Materials are library-owned

Objects reference organization materials. Generic marble, invented stone, and material substitution are forbidden.

## Article VII — Verification compares to specification

Quality Guard and Immune System compare output to Blueprint, DNA, and Render Intent — not to the original prompt.

## Article VIII — Approval is human

Founders approve manufacturing. Systems may recommend; they may not auto-deploy to Living World.

## Article IX — History is preserved

Every decision, version, worker, inspection, and repair is recorded. Director History is never discarded.

## Article X — Blueprint Author is sacred

Blueprint Author remains the planning subsystem. Director Mode elevates it; Director Mode does not replace it.

## Relationship to LOS Constitution

Director Constitution governs **creative interaction**. LOS Constitution governs **Living Object System runtime**. Both apply; neither overrides the other without founder instruction.
