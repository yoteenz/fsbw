# Founder Directing Guide

How founders interact with Studio World under Director Mode.

## You are the Creative Director

You do not write prompts. You direct a team:

- **Architect** (Blueprint Author) — plans the world
- **Engineering** (Asset DNA) — specifies every object
- **Manufacturing** (Render Intent + Workers) — builds what you approved
- **Inspector** (Quality Guard) — certifies every object
- **Maintenance** (Immune System) — repairs drift

## Typical workflow (Environment Studio)

### 1. Vision

Express intent once: "Executive reception with concierge desk and crystal landmark."

Blueprint Author translates this to a Construction Plan. AI never receives your raw words directly.

### 2. Walk the world (Construction Mode)

Open Construction Mode. You see:

- White architecture shell
- Gray placeholders where objects will go
- Blue sockets showing exact placement
- Camera anchors, navigation paths, lighting volumes

**No AI cost yet.**

### 3. Inspect

Click any placeholder:

- Asset Inspector — status, dimensions, materials, dependencies
- DNA Inspector — silhouette, forbidden variations, repair rules
- Render Intent Inspector — manufacturing instructions workers will receive

### 4. Direct (optional)

Select an object and direct a change:

- Replace, change material, move, upgrade

Only the selected object enters iteration. The room stays untouched.

### 5. Approve

Review the Construction Plan Dashboard:

- Architecture, hero assets, furniture, materials, lighting
- Estimated cost, time, models, retries

Tap **Compile World** to authorize manufacturing.

### 6. Watch construction

Live Construction view shows every job:

- Architecture → Desk → Landmark → Furniture → Lighting
- Progress bars, inspection results, worker status

Completed assets replace placeholders in real time.

### 7. Living World

When construction completes, scaffolding fades. The world becomes fully interactive.

## What you never do

- ❌ Prompt "make it more luxurious"
- ❌ Hope the AI understood
- ❌ Regenerate the entire room because one desk failed
- ❌ Accept generic marble instead of Founder Marble
- ❌ Spend AI compute before approving the plan

## What you always do

- ✅ Walk the world before manufacturing
- ✅ Inspect DNA and Render Intent
- ✅ Approve before AI spend
- ✅ Watch construction live
- ✅ Direct object-local changes only
