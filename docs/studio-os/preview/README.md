# Studio Preview™

**Experience Your Future Company**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical acquisition experience architecture (docs only)  
**Phase:** Product experience sprint (pre-implementation)  
**Scope:** Studio OS primary marketing · acquisition · belief creation

---

> **Don't explain. Demonstrate. Don't describe. Immerse.**

---

## Mission

Studio OS is too different to explain through screenshots · videos · or feature lists.

**People need to experience it.**

Instead of telling visitors what Studio OS does — Studio OS lets them **step inside a future version of their company**.

Seeing becomes understanding.

---

## One Sentence

**Studio Preview™** is the acquisition experience where visitors answer a few Orb questions · choose a **Future Company Experience™** · and walk a curated interactive Headquarters™ — creating belief before any signup or commitment.

---

## The Invitation

Visitors do not land on features · pricing · or dashboards.

They receive one invitation:

## **Step Into Your Future Company™**

The experience begins immediately.

---

## Experience Stack

```
Visitor arrives
        ↓
Light Discovery (Orb · handful of questions)
        ↓
Future Company Experiences™ offered
        ↓
Visitor chooses one
        ↓
Curated Interactive Headquarters™ demo
        ↓
Belief: "I understand · I want this · I can picture myself here"
        ↓
Multiple Futures™ (optional · explore more archetypes)
        ↓
Conversion: "Would you like to build this company together?"
```
