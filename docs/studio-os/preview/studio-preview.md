# Studio Preview™ — Master Specification

**Experience Your Future Company**

---

## Purpose

Define **Studio Preview™** — Studio OS's most important **acquisition experience** — replacing feature marketing with immersive demonstration.

---

## Core Declaration

```
MARKETING ERA (wrong)     →  Features · pricing · screenshots · demo videos
PREVIEW ERA (right)       →  Step Into Your Future Company™
GOAL                      →  Belief — not software education
```

---

## Foundational Philosophy

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| Explain | **Demonstrate** |
| Describe | **Immerse** |
| Market software | **Let people experience their future** |

---

## What Studio Preview™ Is

| Preview **is** | Preview **is not** |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Primary acquisition experience | Landing page with hero + CTA |
| Curated interactive HQ demo | Full Studio OS access |
| Belief creation engine | Feature tutorial |
| Simulator of possible futures | Static recommendation text |
| Visitor-first · no account required | Signup wall first |
| Marketing through experience | Marketing through copy |

---

## The Visitor Journey (Summary)

| Phase | Experience |
|-------|------------|
| **1. Arrival** | One invitation — Step Into Your Future Company™ |
| **2. Light Discovery** | Orb · handful of natural questions |
| **3. Future selection** | Choose Future Company Experience™ |
| **4. The Demo** | Transported to curated Headquarters™ |
| **5. Exploration** | Walk · Orb · departments · scenes · moments |
| **6. Multiple Futures™** | Optional other archetypes |
| **7. Conversion** | Orb: *"Would you like to build this company together?"* |

**Source:** [preview-flow.md](./preview-flow.md)

---

## Light Discovery

Orb asks only a **handful** of questions — not full [Founder Discovery™](../founder-discovery/README.md):

- What kind of work excites you?
- What skills do you enjoy using?
- Freedom · income · creativity · impact — or combination?
- What lifestyle are you building?
- What industries have always interested you?

**Conversation — not a quiz.**

Enough signal to personalize Future Company Experiences™ — not full genome.

---

## The Preview™ vs The Demo

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **The Preview™** | The overall acquisition product |
| **Future Company Experience™** | One archetype package (Luxury Hair Brand™ · etc.) |
| **The Demo** | Interactive HQ walkthrough of chosen experience |

---

## Curated Demo Scope

**Not every feature. Not the whole operating system.**

| Included | Excluded |
|----------|----------|
| Walking Headquarters™ | Full account settings |
| Orb greeting · guidance | Real data persistence |
| Sample departments · scenes | Every department |
| Living Set expression | Full pipeline generation |
| Scripted work moments | Production APIs |
| Typical workday beat | Routines engine live |
| Launch moment · celebration | Billing · admin |

**Source:** [future-company-experience.md](./future-company-experience.md)

---

## Purpose: Belief

The goal is **not** to teach software.

The visitor should think:

| Thought | Signal |
|---------|--------|
| *"I understand."* | Comprehension without feature list |
| *"I want this."* | Desire · aspiration |
| *"I can picture myself here."* | Identity projection |

---

## Multiple Futures™

Visitors may explore multiple businesses:

- *"What would my life look like as a salon owner?"*
- *"What if I built a software company?"*
- *"What if I launched a fashion brand?"*

Studio OS becomes a **simulator of possible futures**.

**Source:** [company-simulator.md](./company-simulator.md)

---

## Marketing Position

Studio Preview™ **is** the primary marketing experience.

Advertising invites:

> **Step Into Your Future Company™**

Not: Watch a demo · Read docs · View screenshots.

**Source:** [interactive-marketing.md](./interactive-marketing.md)

---

## Conversion Gate

Conversion happens **only after** preview ends.

Orb asks:

> *"Would you like to build this company together?"*

Visitor now understands exactly what Studio OS is.

**Source:** [visitor-conversion.md](./visitor-conversion.md)

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Feature grid first | Explains instead of demonstrates |
| Signup before preview | No belief yet |
| Full OS demo | Overwhelming · not curated |
| Text-only recommendations | Immersion broken |
| Teach every button | Goal is belief not training |
| Fake static video | Not interactive · not Studio OS |
