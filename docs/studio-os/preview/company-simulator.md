# Company Simulator™ (Preview)

**Simulator of Possible Futures**

---

## Purpose

Define how **Studio Preview™** functions as a **simulator of possible futures** — visitors comparing life as salon owner · founder · agency principal · etc.

**Distinct from:** [Business Simulator™ — Founder Discovery](../founder-discovery/business-simulator.md) (deeper · post-intent).

---

## Core Law

Visitors explore **multiple futures** — not one recommendation.

> *"What would my life look like as a salon owner?"*  
> *"What if I built a software company?"*

---

## Simulator Layers

| Layer | Preview depth |
|-------|---------------|
| **Environment** | Walk HQ · feel atmosphere |
| **Rhythm** | Scripted workday beat |
| **Energy** | Creative vs operational vs clinical |
| **Scale** | Solo vs team hints |
| **Celebration** | Milestone emotional peak |
| **Comparison** | Orb contrast after 2+ futures |

Full financial · stress · hiring simulation = **Founder Discovery™** post-conversion.

---

## Comparison Experience

After exploring 2+ archetypes:

| Orb facilitation | Example |
|------------------|---------|
| **Contrast** | "Salon is tactile · startup is iterative." |
| **Lifestyle** | "Salon: local roots. Startup: scale horizon." |
| **Energy** | "Which felt more energizing?" |
| **No winner** | Visitor chooses identity |

Optional: simple visual comparison wall — not spreadsheet.

## Simulator Ethics

| Rule | Law |
|------|-----|
| **Honest archetypes** | No fantasy income promises |
| **Include hard moments** | Brief · not only glamour |
| **Diverse futures** | Many industries · paths |
| **No shame on exit** | Exploration is success |

## Handoff to Deep Simulator

On conversion → [Founder Discovery Business Simulator™](../founder-discovery/business-simulator.md):

- Preview archetype becomes starting hypothesis
- Full day/week/year simulation unlocks
- Cost Engine™ attaches

Preview teases · Discovery completes.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| One future only | Simulator requires choice |
| Identical demos reskinned | Each archetype unique |
| Comparison as ranking | Identity not score |
| Share requires signup | Viral loop broken |
