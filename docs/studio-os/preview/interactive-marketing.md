# Interactive Marketing™

**Preview as Primary Marketing**

---

## Purpose

Define how **Studio Preview™** replaces traditional software marketing — ads · landing pages · demo requests · feature lists.

---

## Core Law

Studio Preview™ **is** the marketing experience.

Advertising invites people to **Step Into Your Future Company™** — not to watch · read · or compare features.

---

## Marketing Shift

| Traditional SaaS | Studio Preview™ |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Hero + feature grid | Immediate invitation |
| "Book a demo" | "Step inside" |
| Screenshot carousel | Walkable HQ |
| Pricing page first | Belief first · pricing later |
| Case study PDF | Experienced future |
| Explainer video | Interactive immersion |
| "See all features" | Curated belief moments |

---

## Campaign Architecture

### Primary CTA (Universal)

```
Step Into Your Future Company™
```

Every channel converges on **one experience** — not fragmented landing pages.

### Channel Examples

| Channel | Message frame |
|---------|---------------|
| **Paid social** | "What if you could walk through your business before building it?" |
| **YouTube** | 15s hook → click to preview (not feature montage) |
| **Podcast** | Host: "I stepped inside my future company" |
| **Influencer** | Shared My Future Company™ card |
| **SEO** | "Experience your future business" (experience intent) |
| **Email** | Single CTA · no feature bullet list |

---

## No Feature Marketing in Acquisition

| Forbidden at acquisition | Allowed |
|--------------------------|---------|
| "AI-powered dashboard" | "Meet your future headquarters" |
| "Integrations list" | "Watch your launch day" |
| "Compare plans" | After preview · if at all |
| "ROI calculator" | Lifestyle simulator inside preview |

Features are discovered **inside** the experience — not sold upfront.

## Competitive Positioning

Studio OS markets unlike any software:

> *"The first platform that lets you experience your future company — before you build it."*

Not: *"All-in-one business OS with AI."*

Differentiation is **experiential** — not feature comparative.

## Brand Voice in Marketing

| Tone | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Invitational** | "Step inside." |
| **Aspirational** | "Your future headquarters is waiting." |
| **Confident** | No apology · no hype stack |
| **Human** | Orb-forward · not enterprise cold |

## Pricing & Plans in Acquisition

| Rule | Law |
|------|-----|
| **Pricing not on preview entry** | Belief before transaction |
| **Pricing after conversion ask** | Visitor understands value |
| **No countdown timers** | Not discount SaaS |
| **Studio OS subscription** | Mentioned in Cost context post-preview if path continues |

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Separate marketing site + app | One experience |
| Feature comparison tables | Commoditizes magic |
| "Request demo" sales flow | Self-serve belief |
| Preview behind email gate | Kills immersion start |
