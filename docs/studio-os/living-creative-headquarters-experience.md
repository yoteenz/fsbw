# Living Creative Headquarters Experience™

**Studio OS™ — Architectural Experience Amendment**

**Type:** Experience DNA™ · canonical experience philosophy (not a Design Revision · not a new milestone)

**Status:** Governing experiential standard for all Studio OS products

**Applies to:** Experience DNA™ · Creative Direction Studio™ · Studio Production Engine™ · Living Headquarters™ · [Studio Project™ Model](./project-model.md) · all future creative workflows

## Executive mandate

**Do not** treat Studio OS like:

- Productivity software  
- A dashboard  
- A collection of pages  

**Do** treat Studio OS like **managing a living creative headquarters** inspired by games such as **The Movies**.

The founder runs a **luxury creative studio**. Projects **physically travel** through departments. Every department is a **destination** with identity, atmosphere, tools, and purpose.

| Role | Who |
|------|-----|
| **Project protagonist** | The active **Project** in production — never static |
| **Studio Director** | Founder |
| **Executive Creative Director** | Studio Orb |
| **Department Head** | Concierge |

Projects move through **headquarters**, not through **forms**.

Every department feels like **entering another building on the studio lot**.

---

## Experience principles

### The Sims → Ownership

Build something that **feels alive**. Everything evolves over time.

**Studio OS:** Living headquarters · evolving Creative Direction · parallel branches · unlockable buildings · persistent project passport.

### The Movies → Production management

Watch projects **physically progress** through the studio. Every department has **meaningful work**.

**Studio OS:** Ceremonial dispatch between buildings · one department at a time · Continue = travel on the lot · exit artifacts = handoff props.

### Watch Dogs → Ambient intelligence

Live contextual overlays. Information exists **in the environment**, not in static dashboards.

**Studio OS:** Studio Orb ambient layer · department arrival overlays · asset passport in-room · intelligence woven into atmosphere copy.

### Xbox → Progress psychology

Milestones · unlocks · achievements · completion · mastery — **without feeling childish**.

**Studio OS:** Building clearance count · mastery tiers (Developing → In Production → On Lot → Premiere Ready) · locked future buildings · executive tone.

## Design goal

When using Studio OS, the founder should feel like they are **running a luxury creative headquarters** — not operating software.

The UI communicates **movement**, **progression**, **ownership**, and **momentum**.

Projects never feel static. Departments never feel like pages. The headquarters feels **alive**.
