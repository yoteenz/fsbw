# Identity Graph™ (M163)

**Foundational people intelligence layer for Studio OS.**

## Philosophy

People are first-class citizens inside Studio OS. Organizations are built from people — Studio OS should understand both.

Rather than treating people as simple user accounts, Identity Graph™ understands their relationships, expertise, responsibilities, history, and organizational context. Every identity becomes a **living profile**.

## Identity Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Founder | Organizational vision and judgment anchor |
| Employee | Internal team members and department leads |
| Customer | Client organizations and served relationships |
| Expert | Marketplace expertise engagements |
| Vendor | Supplier and vendor relationships |
| Partner | Strategic and network partnerships |
| Investor | Capital and governance stakeholders |
| Advisor | Executive mentorship relationships |
| Contractor | Project-scoped specialists |
| Applicant | Hiring pipeline candidates |

## Living Profile Fields

Each person maintains:

- Personal & organization profile summaries
- Department and role
- Skills, expertise, responsibilities
- Projects and knowledge contributions
- Achievements and learning history
- Communication preferences
- Life & Culture Preferences™
- Permissions and goals
- Professional interests

## Relationship Edges

Studio Intelligence™ maps:

- Reports to · Works with · Mentors · Collaborates with
- Referred by · Family business · Ownership
- Clients served · Teams · Departments · Organizations

Identity Graph™ syncs from:
