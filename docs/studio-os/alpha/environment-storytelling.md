# Environment Storytelling — Creative Direction Studio™ Alpha

**Discipline owners:** Environment art · Lighting · Material · Concept art  
**Purpose:** The room tells the story before any UI appears

---

## Story Being Told

> *"You are the creative authority of an extraordinary company. This space was built for decisions that matter — where references become direction and direction becomes everything downstream."*

Environmental storytelling communicates **creativity · luxury · intelligence · possibility** through:

- Scale (hero wall · double height)
- Material honesty (stone · brass · glass — not plastic UI)
- Light behavior (editorial rig · not flat fill)
- Evidence of work (pins · branches · references — not empty states)
- Intelligence presence (Orb · Observatory — not chat widget)

## Lighting Design

### Rig: Editorial Three-Point (object `lighting-rig-cds`)

| Light | Role | Mood Wall | Table | Alcove |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Key | 45° left · soft | Hero texture | Glass edge | Dome accent |
| Fill | Ambient panel ceiling | Shadow open | Readable surface | — |
| Rim | Back accent | Wall separation | Object pop | Observatory glow |

### Dynamic Behavior

| Event | Light response |
|-------|------------------|
| Arrival bloom | Ceiling 0→80% over 1.5s |
| Pin to Mood Wall | Local accent pulse at pin |
| Branch spawn | Sandbox key warms 10% |
| Approval ceremony | Global dim 20% · pedestal spot |
| Walk the Room | Sequential zone spots |

No flashing. No club lighting. Editorial only.

## Anti-SaaS Environmental Law

Forbidden in environment art:

- Card-shaped floating panels with drop shadows
- Grid backgrounds
- Kanban column visuals
- Sticky note textures as hero surfaces
- Fluorescent even office light
- Generic stock photography as room art
- Visible browser chrome metaphor
