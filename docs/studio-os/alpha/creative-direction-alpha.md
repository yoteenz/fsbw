## What This Room Is

Creative Direction Studio™ is the **living creative brain of the company** — a double-height editorial atelier where creative intent is formed, challenged, evolved, and approved **before** anything enters production.

It is **not** a web page. It is **not** a dashboard. It is **not** a form.

It is a **room**.

When the founder crosses the entry threshold, they must immediately think:

> *"I have entered Creative Direction."*

## Studio Orb Ownership

The Orb **owns**:

- Arrival greeting and zone orientation (first visit)
- Routing speak commands to concierges
- Creative Direction Approval ceremony orchestration
- Walk the Room™ presentation mode entry
- Proactive status one-liners (references overnight · branch awaiting)
- Exit handoff line toward Discover Department

The Orb **never**:

- Auto-approves creative direction
- Replaces founder judgment
- Displays SaaS notifications or toast UI
