# Expeditions™

**The Transformational Journeys of Studio World™**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical OS transformation architecture (docs only)  
**Phase:** Workflow evolution sprint (post-Routines™)  
**Scope:** Strategic · long-duration · company-transforming work

---

> **Routines™ improve a company. Expeditions™ transform a company.**

---

## Mission

We have defined [Routines™](../routines/README.md) — repeatable operational workflows.

Not every objective is repeatable.

Some objectives **fundamentally transform the company itself**.

These become **Expeditions™**.

---

## One Sentence

An **Expedition™** is a milestone-driven transformational journey through Studio World™ that changes where a company is going — guided by the Orb™ over weeks or months, remembered forever in Company Memory™.

---

## The Two Categories of Work

| Category | Nature | Duration | Examples |
|----------|--------|----------|----------|
| **[Routine™](../routines/README.md)** | Operational · repeatable | Minutes · hours · days | Weekly Executive Review™ · Hire Employee™ |
| **Expedition™** | Transformational · strategic | Weeks · months | Launch Company™ · Complete Rebrand™ |

**Law:** Both exist inside Studio World™. Both are guided by the Orb™.
