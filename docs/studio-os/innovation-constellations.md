# Innovation Constellations™

**Status:** Canonical living knowledge universe — July 2026  
**Philosophy:** Business innovation visualized, explored, celebrated, and evolved as a living universe.

---

## Mission

Innovation Lineage™ records history. Innovation Constellations™ transforms that history into a **living universe** founders explore — not another Marketplace view or search page.

---

## Celestial hierarchy

| Level | Trademark | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Universe™ | Studio World itself | One living knowledge fabric |
| Galaxies™ | Industry markets | Retail, Healthcare, Beauty, Technology |
| Constellations™ | Categories | Luxury Beauty™, Customer Experience™, AI Operations™ |
| Solar Systems™ | Families | Headquarters™, Blueprint Families™, Workflow Families™ |
| Stars™ / Suns™ | Individual inventions | Blueprints, departments, automation systems |

---

## Influence tiers

Stars change over time:

- **Blue Star™** — Emerging innovation
- **White Star™** — Growing adoption
- **Gold Star™** — Industry standard
- **Red Giant™** — Historic breakthrough
- **Constellation Anchor™** — Foundation of an entire category

Engine: `src/studio-os-core/innovation-constellations/star-influence.ts`

## Live evolution

Universe syncs from Innovation Lineage™, Collaborative Innovation Network™, World Graph™, and Marketplace.

- New stars appear as founders invent
- Stars brighten as ideas evolve

- Constellations and galaxies expand in real time
