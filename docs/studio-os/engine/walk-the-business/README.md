# Walk the Business™

**The Daily Executive Ritual**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical Experience Specification  
**Type:** Flagship Studio OS Experience — the platform heartbeat  
**Parent:** [Headquarters Engine™](../../headquarters-engine.md) · [Walk the Room™](../walk-the-room/README.md) · [Experience Engine™](../../experience-engine.md)  
**Engine ID:** `studio.walk-the-business.v1`

---

> **People don't start their workday staring at spreadsheets. They ask: "What needs my attention today?" Studio OS answers by letting them walk through their business.**

Walk the Business™ is the **primary daily experience** of Studio OS — the canonical executive ritual where the founder physically arrives at their Headquarters every morning and walks through a living business before work begins.

Not a dashboard. Not an analytics page. Not a homepage.

**The Headquarters itself is the dashboard.**

---

## What This Experience Does

| Does | Does Not |
|------|----------|
| Begin every day with executive arrival | Open to a widget grid |
| Communicate health through environment | Reduce business to charts alone |
| Guide founder via Orb and AI employees | Flood with notification banners |
| Surface priorities through spatial walk | Require reading status reports |
| Evolve Headquarters visibly over time | Feel static between sessions |
| Conclude with actionable daily focus | Leave founder without direction |
| Support natural voice commands | Demand navigation menus |
| Integrate with department Runtime | Build another admin dashboard |

## Walk the Business vs Walk the Room

| Dimension | Walk the Business™ | Walk the Room™ |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| Cadence | Daily · morning ritual | On-demand · review/critique |
| Scope | Entire Headquarters | Single department · project · subject |
| Purpose | What needs attention? | How do we make this better? |
| Tone | Executive orientation | Creative critique |
| Duration | 10–25 minutes | 15–40 minutes |
| Mode | Morning · Founder arrival | Presentation · review |

Both are spatial. Both use Orb. **Different rituals.**
