# Orb As Host™

**The Orb anchors the room — emotionally and spatially**

---

## Purpose

Reset Orb role from **decoration** to **host of the department**.

The Orb welcomes · observes · recommends · explains · celebrates · questions.

Everything arranges around it.

---

## Core Law

**The Orb is the spatial center of Creative Direction Studio™.**

It floats above the **Story Table™** — not in a corner · not in a card · not in a chat widget.

---

## Spatial Placement

```
                    [Orb™]
                       │
              ═══ Story Table™ ═══
                 (spatial center)
                       │
        ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
        │              │              │
   Founder Notes™   Mood Wall™    Review Area™
```

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Anchor object | Story Table™ |
| Height | Above table · eye-level when seated |
| Sightline | Visible from Arrival Zone™ by second beat |
| Audio origin | Spatial · reverb matches room |
| Light | Soft emission · pulses with speech |

---

## Host Behaviors

| Behavior | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Welcome** | "Welcome to Creative Direction. Your Mood Wall is ready." |
| **Observe** | Watches founder approach workstations · comments contextually |
| **Recommend** | Concept directions · pipeline next step · taste alignment |
| **Explain** | Braintrust summary · why concept prioritized |
| **Celebrate** | Golden milestone · approval · branch saved |
| **Question** | "Which direction excites you?" · gentle probes |
| **Guide** | Gesture-light toward undiscovered zone |

---

## Alive Orb

The Orb must feel **alive**:

| Signal | Expression |
|--------|------------|
| Idle | Slow breathe · soft particle halo |
| Listening | Brighten · lean toward founder |
| Thinking | Subtle orbit · chromatic shift |
| Speaking | Pulse sync · spatial audio |
| Celebrating | Warm bloom · brief particle burst |

Not theatrical — **present · professional · warm**.

---

## Orb vs Workstations

| Situation | Orb role |
|-----------|----------|
| Founder at Pipeline board | Orb may float nearer board · comment status |
| Founder at Review Area™ | Orb facilitator for Braintrust |
| Founder at Mood Wall | Orb quiet — wall is hero |
| Founder idle | Orb may offer gentle observation |

Orb **never** blocks hero sightlines on arrival.
