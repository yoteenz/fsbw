# Arrival Zone™

**The founder never sees the entire department immediately**

---

## Purpose

Solve the **"entire room on one screen"** problem.

The founder arrives in **Arrival Zone™** — the first physical space. The rest of the department is **discovered** through movement · curiosity · and scale.

---

## Core Law

**Reveal · don't display.**

---

## The Problem (Prototype)

| Prototype behavior | Why it fails |
|--------------------|--------------|
| Full room visible on load | No scale · no mystery · feels like illustration |
| All workstations at once | Cognitive overload · dashboard feeling |
| Static wide shot | Wallpaper · not place |
| Immediate pipeline panel | Software first · room second |

---

## Arrival Zone™ Design

### First Frame

Founder sees **only**:

| Visible | Hidden (discoverable) |
|---------|----------------------|
| Entry threshold · floor · partial wall | Full Mood Wall extent |
| Glimpse of hero wall edge · light bloom | Timeline Table details |
| Orb pulse · distant greeting | Pipeline board |
| Corridor depth hint | Asset Console |
| Architectural scale (ceiling partial) | Review Area |
| Ambient sound · HVAC · distant activity | Gallery wall |

### Emotional beat

> *"I'm inside something large. I want to walk forward."*

---

## Discovery Sequence

```
1. ARRIVAL ZONE™ — threshold · partial sightlines
        ↓
2. FORWARD MOVEMENT — camera walk · curiosity
        ↓
3. HERO REVEAL — Mood Wall scale unfolds
        ↓
4. ORB GREETING — host acknowledges at Story Table™
        ↓
5. WORKSTATION DISCOVERY — Timeline · boards · alcoves on exploration
        ↓
6. FULL DEPARTMENT KNOWN — earned through movement · not given on load
```

---

## Camera on Arrival

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Initial FOV | Narrower — intimate threshold |
| Initial position | Entry portal · back of arrival zone |
| First motion | Slow dolly forward · 2–4 seconds |
| Yaw freedom | Limited initially · opens after greeting |
| Zoom | Disabled on arrival — walk first |

Reduced motion path: instant shell with Orb line only — accessibility preserved.

---

## Sightline Design

Priority reveals as founder advances:

1. Light bloom on hero wall edge
2. Mood Wall scale (vertical reveal)
3. Story Table™ + Orb center
4. Timeline glass catch light
5. Pipeline board LEDs (peripheral left)
6. Library shelving depth (right)
7. Observatory alcove glow (left discovery)

---

## Curiosity Hooks

| Hook | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Partially visible doorway | Headquarters beyond |
| Animated display in distance | Work happening |
| Unlit workstation | "What is that?" |
| Orb gesture toward zone | Guided discovery without menu |
| Ambient voices (muffled) | Living building |

---

## Relationship to Arrival Sequence™

[Arrival Sequence™](../foundational-experience-systems/arrival-sequence.md) governs Set entry globally.

**Arrival Zone™** is the **CDS-specific first chamber** within that sequence — the spatial implementation of "do not show everything at once."

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| God-view on load | Kills scale |
| Minimap showing all zones day one | Discovery spoiled |
| Skip arrival for returning founder | Short re-orientation beat still required |
| Instant teleport to workstation | Breaks place continuity |

Returning founders: **abbreviated** arrival (1 second re-orient) — not full skip.
