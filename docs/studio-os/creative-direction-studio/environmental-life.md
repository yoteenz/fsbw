# Environmental Life™

**The department never feels static**

---

## Purpose

Define **Idle Life™** and ambient systems for Creative Direction Studio™ — subtle environmental motion so the founder feels work is **constantly happening**.

---

## Core Law

**Static rooms are dead software. Living rooms breathe.**

---

## Ambient Life Catalog

| Effect | Location | Intensity |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Moving light across surfaces** | Floor · Mood Wall · table glass | Slow · 30–60s cycle |
| **Soft reflections** | Polished floor · Timeline glass | Shimmer on light pass |
| **Ambient holograms** | Observatory · distant displays | Gentle orbit |
| **Dynamic displays** | Pipeline board · Asset Console | Status updates · not flicker |
| **Production screens updating** | Background monitors | Muffled activity |
| **Particles** | Key light shaft · Orb halo | Dust · subtle |
| **Depth parallax** | All layers | On camera move |
| **Animated architecture** | Skylight · louvers | Micro motion |
| **Subtle audio** | HVAC · distant voices · machine hum | Spatial · low |
| **Environmental breathing** | Mood Wall color temperature | Genome-modulated |

---

## Interactive vs Ambient

| Interactive (founder triggers) | Ambient (always on) |
|--------------------------------|---------------------|
| Mood Wall pin | Wall parallax drift |
| Pipeline card move | Board LED idle pulse |
| Timeline scrub | Floor reflection |
| Orb speak | Particle dust |
| Review approve | Display celebration burst |

**Ambient never stops** when founder idle.

---

## Production Activity Illusion

Founder should sense **production team at work**:

| Cue | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| Distant display flicker | Another department active |
| Pipeline board light advance | Stage progressing (Background Intelligence™) |
| Muffled notification tone | System prepared work |
| Shadow pass window | Time-of-day shift |
| Console screen refresh | Generation completing |

Not fake people — **environmental evidence of work**.

## Genome Modulation

[Room DNA™](../departments/creative-direction-studio/room-dna.json) sliders affect life intensity:

| Slider | Life effect |
|--------|-------------|
| Energy | Particle · display activity level |
| Warmth | Color breathe speed |
| Luxury | Reflection sharpness |
| Focus | Ambient audio reduction |

[Company Genome™](../intelligence/company-genome.md) may dampen celebration in crisis World State™.

---

## Performance Tiers

| Tier | Life features |
|------|---------------|
| **High** | Full particles · parallax · reflections |
| **Medium** | Reduced particles · static reflections |
| **Reduced motion** | Light breathe only · no particles |

Accessibility: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — room stays beautiful · not cinematic.

---

## Relationship to Idle Life™

[Idle Life™](../foundational-experience-systems/idle-life.md) is System 002 — mandatory for all Sets™.

CDS V2 **implements** Idle Life at AAA standard — prototype had minimal ambient.

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|

| Flickering UI chrome | SaaS not place |
| Life stops on menu open | World persists |
