# World Building™

**One room inside a much larger headquarters**

---

## Purpose

Creative Direction Studio™ is **not software** — it is one department inside **Studio World™**.

The founder must always sense **other departments beyond the walls**.

---

## Core Law

**This is part of a living headquarters — not an isolated admin page.**

---

## World Context Cues

| Cue | Implementation |
|-----|----------------|
| **Glass wall · windows** | Exterior horizon · HQ campus · weather |
| **Visible doorways** | Hallways to Marketing · Product · Legacy |
| **Ambient movement** | Shadows · distant figures (silhouette) · cart pass |
| **Layered audio** | Muffled meeting · machinery · elevator ding |
| **Lighting continuity** | Same time-of-day as Headquarters |
| **Signage** | Department plaques · wayfinding |
| **Transitions™** | Exit portal returns to lobby |

---

## Beyond the Walls

Founder should believe:

> *"If I walk through that door, I'll be somewhere else in my company."*

| Beyond wall | Suggested presence |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Right glass wall | City · campus · sky (genome) |
| Left solid wall | Adjacent department muffled |
| Back corridor | Headquarters circulation |
| Ceiling | Other floors · skylight to world |

## Department Neighbors (Canonical)

```
Executive Lobby™
        ↓ corridor
Creative Direction Studio™  ← you are here
        ↓ skybridge
Marketing War Room™
        ↓ elevator
Product Lab™
```

Not all visible at once — **sensed** through audio · signage · Orb references.

---

## Exit & Return

Leaving CDS is a **Transition™** — not browser back.

| Exit | Journey |
|------|---------|
| To Headquarters lobby | Walk corridor · door |
| To another department | Named transition type |
| To Mission Control | Executive corridor |

Founder never feels they "closed a page."

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Isolated full-screen with no exits | Trapped in page |
| Generic gradient background | Not headquarters |
| No audio beyond room | Dead building |
| Inconsistent world time | Breaks persistence |
