# Arrival Experience™

**Doors · hallway · transition · reveal**

---

## Purpose

Define how the founder **enters** Creative Direction Studio™ through an actual **Arrival Sequence™** — not by loading a dashboard.

---

## Core Law

**Creative Direction Studio™ opens before them — it is not dumped on screen.**

---

## Sequence (Canonical)

```
1. TRANSITION™ — approach department (hallway · door · corridor)
        ↓
2. THRESHOLD — door · portal · department signage
        ↓
3. ARRIVAL ZONE™ — partial room · limited sightlines
        ↓
4. CAMERA DOLLY — slow forward · light bloom
        ↓
5. ORB WELCOME — host acknowledges by name
        ↓
6. HERO REVEAL — Mood Wall scale unfolds
        ↓
7. EXPLORATION UNLOCK — walk · orbit enabled
        ↓
8. WORK — founder discovers workstations naturally
```

---

## Pre-Room Transition™

Founder does not spawn inside CDS from a menu click alone.

| Entry path | Transition |
|------------|--------------|
| From Headquarters lobby | Walk corridor · glass door |
| From Studio Overview | Elevator or skybridge · department signage |
| From Mission Control | Executive corridor · Creative wing |
| Deep link / return | Abbreviated re-orientation beat |

**Detail:** [Transitions™](../world/transitions/README.md)

---

## Reveal Choreography

| Beat | Duration | Visual |
|------|----------|--------|
| Door open | 0.5–1s | Light spill from inside |
| Threshold pause | 0.5s | Scale hint · audio swell |
| Forward dolly | 2–4s | Arrival Zone™ → Mood Wall edge |
| Orb pulse + line | sync | "Welcome to Creative Direction." |
| Mood Wall bloom | 1–2s | Vertical scale reveal |

Total arrival: **5–10 seconds** — never instant full room (reduced motion: 1s shell + Orb only).

---

## What NOT to Expose on Arrival

| Hidden at arrival | Revealed through |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Full pipeline status | Walk to board |
| All concept branches | Gallery discovery |
| Asset Console detail | Approach console |
| Complete Timeline history | Timeline approach |
| Review Area seating | Right exploration |

---

## Returning Founder

| Visit | Arrival |
|-------|---------|
| First ever | Full sequence |
| Same day return | 2s re-orient · camera restores last zone |
| New session | Short arrival · Mood Wall breathe + Orb line |

World Memory™ restores last workstation — arrival acknowledges continuity.

---

## Audio Design

| Layer | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Transition | Footsteps · door · hallway tone |
| Arrival | Room tone · HVAC · distant activity |
| Orb | Spatial voice · warm · not sales |
| Post-arrival | Workstation-specific on approach |

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Instant full room | Kills arrival |
| Loading spinner | Transitions™ forbidden pattern |
| Form before room | Software first |
| Skip arrival always | No scale |
