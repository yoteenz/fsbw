# Studio World Atlas™ — Parallel Futures™ (Phase 4)

**Route:** `/admin/studio/world-atlas` → **PARALLEL FUTURES™** mode  
**Philosophy:** Design first. Build second. Nothing generates until a future is explicitly approved.

## Parallel Futures™

Inside Master Planner™, founders compare multiple complete master plans before committing construction:

| Future | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| Future A™ | Luxury Flagship™ |
| Future B™ | Enterprise Scale™ |
| Future C™ | Lean Startup™ |
| Future D™ | Experimental Vision™ |

Each future includes campus layout, departments, roads, expansion strategy, construction phases, and full analysis metrics.

## Side-by-side comparison

Compare simultaneously: build cost, timeline, Creative Equity, Marketplace value, navigation efficiency, expansion capacity, reuse %, AI workforce.

## Simulate the Future™

**SIMULATE THE FUTURE™** walks the campus — enter buildings, preview departments, observe traffic and AI movement. Nothing is built.

## Future Analysis™

Every future displays Creative Budget, generation cost, build duration, equity, reuse, marketplace potential, expansion flexibility, AI workforce, navigation, complexity, maintainability, founder workload, risk, and growth projection.

## Committing

**APPROVE & COMMIT FUTURE →** shows asset count, production cost/time, reusable vs new assets, and savings. No Scene Stack™ generation until explicit approval.

## Version History & Master Planning Library™

Committed, forked, and revived visions persist in `studioWorldAtlasDiscovery_v4` — fork, revive, and compare years later.

## Studio Orb™

Strategic advisor in Parallel Futures mode — recommends futures with WHY (reuse, brand, budget, marketplace).
