# Succession Mode™ V1.0 (Milestone 98)

**Route:** `/admin/studio/succession-mode`

## Purpose

**Succession Mode™** determines whether an organization can continue operating if its founder becomes unavailable.

Succession planning is **not about replacing founders** — it is about **preserving everything they have built**.

The greatest business risk is not competition — **it is irreplaceable knowledge**.

## Succession Readiness Score

Nine dimensions + overall score:

- Knowledge Preservation
- Employee Readiness
- Documentation
- Profession Brain Coverage
- Automation
- Department Independence
- Leadership Delegation
- Customer Continuity
- Critical Process Coverage
- **Overall Succession Readiness**

Score improves as knowledge becomes less dependent on one individual.

## Knowledge Dependency Map

Identifies where knowledge exists only in one person's head:

| Type | Risk |
|------|------|
| Founder Only | High — preserve in Profession Brain™ |
| Manager Only | Medium — cross-train via Studio Institute™ |
| Employee Only | High when Brain coverage thin |
| Vendor Only | External dependency |
| Uncaptured | Critical — immediate Brain capture |

## Succession Recommendations

Continuously recommends:

- Knowledge to preserve
- Processes to document
- Employees requiring cross-training
- Missing SOPs
- Automation opportunities
- Leadership delegation
- Studio Institute™ training

## Legacy Continuity

Organizations should eventually operate independently while preserving founder philosophy and standards.
