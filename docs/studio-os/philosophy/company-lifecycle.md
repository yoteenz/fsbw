# Company Lifecycle™

**Every company progresses through an ongoing journey inside Studio OS**

---

## Purpose

The **Company Lifecycle™** defines how a business moves from first spark to lasting legacy — and how every Studio OS feature maps to that journey.

This extends [Production Lifecycle™](../production-lifecycle/production-lifecycle.md) (experience maturity) to **whole-company scale**.

---

## The Lifecycle

```
Discover
    ↓
Imagine
    ↓
Blueprint™
    ↓
Golden Build™
    ↓
Certified™
    ↓
Launch
    ↓
Operate
    ↓
Grow
    ↓
Optimize
    ↓
Expand
    ↓
Preserve
    ↓
Legacy™
```

Every feature inside Studio OS should support **one or more stages** of this lifecycle.

---

## Stage Definitions

| Stage | Founder state | Studio OS role |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| **Discover** | Exploring possibility | Business Discovery Blueprint™ · industry packs |
| **Imagine** | Vision forming | Founder Journey™ · Inauguration · genome seeding |
| **Blueprint™** | Intent documented | Department definitions · no production yet |
| **Golden Build™** | First proof exists | Creative Atelier™ · engine validation |
| **Certified™** | Quality assured | Validation Loop™ · Creative Review™ · Walk the Room™ |
| **Launch** | Go-live | Launch Day™ World State™ · campaigns · distribution |
| **Operate** | Daily business | Mission Control · operations · AI employees |
| **Grow** | Revenue · team · reach | Expansion Center · new departments · marketplace |
| **Optimize** | Efficiency · margin | Analytics · Profession Brain™ · automation |
| **Expand** | New lines · brands · markets | Multi-wing HQ · holding company graph |
| **Preserve** | Continuity risk addressed | Succession Mode™ · knowledge capture |
| **Legacy™** | History honored | The Archive™ · Founder Chronicle™ · Hall of Legacy™ |

## Overlap with Production Lifecycle™

| Company Lifecycle | Production Lifecycle™ |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Blueprint™ | Blueprint™ (experience) |
| Golden Build™ | Golden Build™ |
| Certified™ | Certified™ |
| Launch | Live™ |

**Production Lifecycle™** governs *individual experiences* (Sets™ · departments).  
**Company Lifecycle™** governs *the whole business*.

## Lifecycle Is Non-Linear

Companies **revisit** stages:

- Regenerate Environment Shell™ → back to Golden Build™ for that Set™
- New department pack → Expand triggers new Blueprint™ for that wing
- Crisis™ World State™ → Operate with emergency posture
- Re-certification → Certified™ again after major Evolution™

The lifecycle is a **spiral**, not a one-way door.

---

## Lifecycle Signals

Studio OS infers company lifecycle position from:

| Signal | Stage hint |
|--------|------------|
| Blueprint incomplete | Discover · Imagine |
| Golden Build in progress | Golden Build™ |
| No Certified™ Sets™ | Pre-Launch |
| Active orders · campaigns | Operate · Launch |
| Expansion Center installs | Grow · Expand |
| Archive exhibits growing | Legacy™ emerging |

[World States™](../world/world-states.md) and [World Events™](../world/world-events.md) express lifecycle moments spatially.
