# Studio OS Manifesto™

**The Company Engine™ — canonical statement**

---

## The World's First Company Engine™

Studio OS is software designed **not merely to create software** — but to **create companies**.

It helps founders **imagine, design, launch, operate, optimize, grow, preserve, and evolve** businesses throughout their entire lifecycle.

---

## What Studio OS Is Not

| Capability | Status |
|------------|--------|
| Website builder | Output channel |
| App builder | Output channel |
| AI assistant | Executive team behavior |
| Business dashboard | One lens on the company |
| Project management | One operation inside the company |

These are **capabilities**. They serve the company. They are not the product.

---

## What Studio OS Is

**The operating system for companies.**

From first idea to lasting legacy — one system, one truth, one place the founder inhabits.

## The Product Hierarchy

```
OUTPUTS (evidence)
    websites · apps · campaigns · packaging · content · posts

COMPANY (product)
    identity · strategy · operations · people · products · customers · legacy
```

Websites are outputs.  
Products are outputs.  
Marketing is an output.  
Apps are outputs.

**The company is the product.**

Everything else exists to help it thrive.
