# Company-First Philosophy™

**The decision filter for every Studio OS feature**

---

## The Core Question

Before designing, building, or shipping anything in Studio OS:

> **"How does this help build, operate, improve, or preserve a company?"**

If there is no honest answer, the feature does not belong — or must be reframed until it does.

---

## Company-First vs Output-First

| Output-First (forbidden default) | Company-First (required) |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------|
| "Build a landing page" | "Strengthen how the company presents itself" |
| "Generate a logo" | "Express company identity in the genome" |
| "Post to social" | "Advance a launch inside the company's growth stage" |
| "Fix this dashboard widget" | "Improve how the founder operates the business" |
| "Add a new admin route" | "Give the company a new place to work" |

Outputs are **valid** — when they clearly serve the company lifecycle.

---

## The Six Verbs

Every feature must map to at least one:

| Verb | Test question |
|------|---------------|
| **Create** | Does it help birth or imagine the company? |
| **Operate** | Does it help run daily business? |
| **Enhance** | Does it make the company better? |
| **Scale** | Does it help growth without fragmentation? |
| **Preserve** | Does it protect knowledge · culture · continuity? |
| **Evolve** | Does it help the company adapt over time? |

**Zero verbs = reject or redesign.**

---

## Feature Review Checklist

```
□ Anchored to Company™ object model
□ Maps to Company Lifecycle™ stage(s)
□ Serves founder "building my company" experience
□ Does not treat outputs as the product
□ Fits Studio World™ (if experiential) OR platform layer (if infrastructure)
□ AI role is executive — not chatbot novelty
□ No duplicate company truth in siloed tool
```

---

## When Capabilities Are Allowed

Website builders, app builders, dashboards, and AI chat **are allowed** as:

| Role | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Output channel** | Storefront pages publish company products |
| **Operation surface** | Orders live under company operations |
| **Executive function** | Orb briefs on company health |
| **Evidence** | Campaign proves marketing department exists |

They fail when they become **the reason Studio OS exists**.

---

## Red Flags

| Red flag | Response |
|----------|----------|
| "Let's add another SaaS dashboard" | Reframe as company operation in HQ |
| "Quick page builder feature" | Tie to product · launch · department Set™ |
| "AI that only chats" | Upgrade to executive function with company context |
| "Tool without Company™ scope" | Add object model anchor |
| "Feature competitors have" | Only if it serves the six verbs |

---

## Relationship to Platform Freeze

Platform architecture is frozen — but **philosophy is never frozen**.

New features do not require new engines. They require **company-first intent** expressed through existing:

- Studio World™
- Company Genome™
- Department Packages
- Production Lifecycle™
- Creative Approval Pipeline™

---

## Canonical Reframes

| Old framing | Company-First framing |
|-------------|----------------------|

| Generation Queue | Creative Approval Pipeline™ — director workflow for company environments |
| Admin marketing tab | Company growth operation |
