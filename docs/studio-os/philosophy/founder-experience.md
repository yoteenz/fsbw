# Founder Experience™ — Company Engine Perspective

**"I'm continuing to build my company."**

---

## The Shift

When entering Studio OS, the founder should **not** think:

> "I need to design a page."

They should think:

> **"I'm continuing to build my company."**

Studio OS should automatically understand what that means.

---

## Entry Experience

| Forbidden entry feeling | Required entry feeling |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Opened admin software | Arrived at headquarters |
| Loaded a dashboard | Resumed building the business |
| Started a new task | Continued the company journey |
| Used an AI tool | Met with the executive team |

**Arrival Sequence™** · **Studio World™** · **Orb briefing** — all reinforce company continuity.

---

## Daily Rhythm

```
1. ARRIVE     — Headquarters · time-of-day · World State™
2. BRIEF      — Orb · Mission Control · what matters today
3. WORK       — Departments · Sets™ · projects as places
4. REVIEW     — Creative Review™ · approvals · decisions
5. PROGRESS   — Launches · growth · milestones celebrated
6. DEPART     — World Memory™ persists — company continues
```

The founder never **finishes** Studio OS. They **return** to it.

---

## Language Law

| Software language | Company language |
|-------------------|------------------|
| User | Founder |
| Dashboard | Headquarters · Mission Control |
| Page | Set™ · department |
| Feature | Capability serving the company |
| Save | Leave it in the company memory |
| Settings | Company operations |
| Upload asset | Register company evidence |
| Generate image | Art department delivers stage |

## Lifecycle-Aware Experience

Founder experience **modulates** by [Company Lifecycle™](./company-lifecycle.md) stage:

| Stage | Dominant experience |
|-------|---------------------|
| Discover · Imagine | Blueprint · Inauguration · guided discovery |
| Golden Build™ | Creative Director workflow · Atelier |
| Launch | Celebration™ · campaign · distribution |
| Operate | Mission Control · departments · daily brief |
| Grow · Expand | Expansion Center · new wings |
| Legacy™ | Archive™ · reflective pacing |

[Founder Journey™](../alpha/founder-journey.md) tracks maturity within the lifecycle.

---

## Emotional Contract

The founder should feel:

1. **Agency** — I am the Executive Creative Director; AI advises, I decide
2. **Continuity** — My company persisted while I was away
3. **Place** — I know where I am in my business
4. **Progress** — The company is visibly growing
5. **Pride** — "I built this" — not "the AI built this"
6. **Legacy** — What matters will be remembered

---

## Creative Production as Company Building

The [Creative Approval Pipeline™](../alpha/studio-builder/production-flow.md) is not "waiting for AI."

It is:

> **"My art department delivered Stage 1. The Braintrust reviewed it. I direct the company forward."**

That is company-building — not asset babysitting.

---

## Multi-Company Future

Holding companies · agencies · portfolios:

Founder switches **Company™** — not "workspace tab."  
Each company has its own Headquarters · lifecycle · memory.

Experience stays company-first at every scale.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Founder configures 47 settings before value | Company should feel alive immediately |
| Empty HQ with "add your first page" | Company has Blueprint™ · genome · inauguration |
| Success = published URL | Success = company stage advanced |
| AI does everything | Founder directs; AI executes |
