# Executive AI Model™

**Studio OS thinks like a world-class executive team — not a chatbot**

---

## Purpose

Define how AI inside Studio OS serves the **Company Engine™** — proactively building, operating, and preserving companies.

---

## What AI Is Not

| AI is NOT | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| A chatbot | Reactive · stateless · novelty |
| A content spinner | Output-first |
| A replacement founder | Authority stays human |
| A black box | Must explain · review · remember |
| All specialists talking at once | Orb facilitates — one voice |

---

## What AI Is

**A world-class executive team** embedded in the company.

| Executive function | Studio OS expression |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| **Create** | Asset generation · department compilation · Blueprint sessions |
| **Review** | Creative Review™ Braintrust · Validation Loop™ |
| **Improve** | Evolution™ · regeneration · Profession Brain™ learning |
| **Question** | Critique Sessions™ · executive challenges |
| **Plan** | Campaign Orchestrator™ · Mission Control briefings |
| **Design** | Creative Direction · genome · Set DNA™ |
| **Optimize** | Analytics · Profession Brain™ · KPI surfaces |
| **Forecast** | Executive AI Director™ · trend signals |
| **Protect** | Succession Mode™ · crisis World State™ |
| **Celebrate** | World Events™ · Launch Day™ |
| **Remember** | World Memory™ · Profession Brain™ · Legacy™ |

Everything moves the **company** forward.

---

## The Orb™ — Executive Producer

The [Studio Orb™](../world/studio-world.md) is not a mascot.

| Role | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| **Facilitator** | One voice in reviews — specialists speak when asked |
| **Briefing** | Morning™ · Mission Control · lifecycle-aware |
| **Guide** | Transitions™ · Walk the Business™ |
| **Context** | Knows Company™ · Project · active Set™ |
| **Respect** | Never overrides founder authority |

**Creative Review™** exemplifies the model: Braintrust reviews first · Orb facilitates founder decision.

---

## AI Employee Model

| Entity | Belongs to | Behavior |
|--------|------------|----------|
| **Concierge** | Department | Operational · bounded by Profession Brain™ |
| **Braintrust specialist** | Review context | Invited per stage · not always-on |
| **Executive AI Director™** | Mission Control | Strategic · advisory |
| **Profession Brain™** | Company knowledge | Policy authority — never invents |

AI employees **work in the company** — they do not float above it.

---

## Proactivity Rules

AI should initiate when:

| Trigger | AI action |
|---------|-----------|
| Stage ready for review | Orb notification — not blocking spinner |
| Launch approaching | Campaign Orchestrator prep |
| Knowledge gap | Succession Mode™ flag |
| Milestone earned | World Event™ |
| Crisis signal | Crisis™ state · executive path |

AI should **not** initiate when:

- Quiet Focus™ active
- Founder in Self Review™
- Trust My Instinct™ chosen (report saved quietly)
- Life & Culture says acknowledge quietly

---

## Learning Model

AI improves per company via:

| Source | Feeds |
|--------|-------|
| Director's Notes™ | Project Genome™ · future generations |
| Approved styles | Braintrust calibration |
| Rejected styles | Regeneration patterns |
| Company Genome™ | All creative output |
| Previous Golden Builds™ | Certified™ reference |
| Conversation history | Profession Brain™ memory graph |

**Learning serves the company** — not global model training without consent.

---

## Authority Hierarchy

```
FOUNDER (final authority)
    ↓
Orb (facilitation · briefing)
    ↓
Executive AI Director™ (strategy)
    ↓
Braintrust · Concierges · Profession Brain™ (domain)
    ↓
Generation · Automation (execution)
```

AI **advises**. Founder **decides**.

That relationship defines every creative and operational decision.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| AI approves without founder | Violates Creative Approval Pipeline™ |
| Generic ChatGPT wrapper | Not executive team |
| AI invents company policy | Profession Brain™ violation |
| Spammy proactive notifications | Breaks trust |
| Specialists all speak at once | Orb exists to prevent this |
