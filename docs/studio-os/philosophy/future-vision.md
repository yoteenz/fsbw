### Phase 0 — Philosophy (current)

Company Engine™ canon defined.  
Studio World™ · Production Lifecycle™ · Creative Pipeline live or specified.

### Phase 1 — Inhabit the Company (now → near)

Founder lives in Headquarters daily.  
Sets™ Certified™. Creative Review™ on all stages.  
Company object model reflected in data architecture.

### Phase 2 — Operate the Company

Orders · campaigns · finance · support — all anchored to Company™.  
Mission Control as true executive cockpit.

### Phase 3 — Grow the Company

Expansion Center · marketplace · multi-department.  
Holding company topology.  
Expert Marketplace™ revenue.

### Phase 4 — Preserve the Company

Succession Mode™ production.  
Archive™ · Chronicle™ complete.

### Phase 5 — The Full Company Engine

Create-to-legacy without external OS.  
Founder statement: **"My company was built inside Studio OS."**
