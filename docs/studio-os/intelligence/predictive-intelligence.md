# Predictive Intelligence™

**Studio OS thinks ahead of the founder**

---

## Purpose

Define how Founder Intelligence System™ makes **proactive recommendations** — increasingly aligned with founder instincts over time.

Also referenced as **Predictive Design™** in taste-specific contexts.

---

## Core Law

**Predict with transparency. Founder retains authority.**

Studio OS advises · founder decides.

---

## Maturity Progression

| Maturity | Predictive behavior |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Nascent** | Little prediction · maximum exploration |
| **Developing** | Mixed weighting · explain reasoning |
| **Established** | Strong prioritization · one exploratory outlier |
| **Deep** | Concepts feel pre-imagined · minimal explanation |

---

## Example Behaviors

### Concept prioritization

> **"We prioritized these concepts because they align with your established creative preferences."**

### Conflict elimination

> **"We removed several concepts because they consistently conflict with your Founder Taste Genome™."**

### Pattern application

> **"We emphasized immersive architecture because it has become a defining characteristic of your work."**

### Scale prediction

> **"We increased architectural scale because your previous Golden Builds consistently favored dramatic environments."**

### Operational prediction (Founder Genome™)

> **"I prepared a visual summary first — you typically prefer to review designs before reading specifications."**

## Transparency Report

Every predictive generation may include:

```typescript
interface PredictiveIntelligenceReport {
  conceptsGenerated: number;
  conceptsEliminated: number;
  eliminationReasons: string[];
  tasteDimensionsApplied: string[];
  founderGenomeFactorsApplied: string[];
  companyConstraintsApplied: string[];
  explorationConceptId?: string;
  confidence: 'developing' | 'established' | 'deep';
}
```

Founder may request report · Orb summarizes conversationally.

---

## Prediction Errors as Learning

When founder selects unpredicted concept:

| Event | Value |
|-------|-------|
| High surprise selection | Strong taste learning signal |
| Founder rejects prediction explanation | Calibrate model |
| "Surprise me" request | Bypass filter · pure exploration |

Errors make the system **smarter** — not failures.

---

## Boundaries

| Predictive Intelligence™ does | Does not |
|-------------------------------|----------|
| Prioritize concepts | Force single option |
| Eliminate weak matches | Hide elimination |
| Suggest defaults | Auto-approve |
| Explain reasoning | Override founder |
| Learn from surprises | Punish exploration |

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Filter to one "perfect" concept | No choice · no learning |
| Black-box personalization | Trust destruction |
| Predict without genome evidence | Fabricated alignment |
| Company violations for taste | Brand damage |
| Never explore | Taste evolves |
