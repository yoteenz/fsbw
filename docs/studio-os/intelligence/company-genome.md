# Company Genome™

**What makes THIS company unique**

---

## Purpose

Define **Company Genome™** — the permanent identity layer for each organization inside Studio OS.

**Belongs to the company — not the founder.**

When a founder creates a second venture, Company Genome™ starts fresh. Founder Genomes™ travel.

---

## Implementation Reference

Company Genome™ is implemented as **Organization Genome™ (M95)**:

- **Route:** `/admin/studio/organization-genome`
- **Code:** `src/studio-os-core/organization-genome/`
- **Docs:** [../organization-genome.md](../organization-genome.md)

This document defines Company Genome™ within the **Three Genomes™** architecture. Terminology is equivalent:

| Architecture term | Implementation term |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Company Genome™ | Organization Genome™ |
| Company | Organization |

## Distinction from Founder Genomes

| Layer | Scope | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Founder Genome™** | Founder across companies | Teena is a visionary founder-led leader |
| **Founder Taste Genome™** | Founder's creative instincts | Teena prefers immersive architecture |
| **Company Genome™** | One organization | Frontal Slayer is premium hair · gatekeeping-free |

NDXBOOK gets a **new** Company Genome™ — but inherits Teena's Founder Genomes™ on day one.

## AI Consultation Law

Every AI interaction consults Company Genome™ before generating work:

- Emails · workflows · proposals
- Concierge responses
- Marketing campaigns
- Customer communications
- Creative generation (with Taste Genome)

```typescript
consultOrganizationGenome(context)  // implementation API
```

Company Genome™ + Founder Taste Genome™ + Founder Genome™ compose full context.

---

## Company vs Founder Conflict Resolution

When genomes disagree:

| Scenario | Resolution |
|----------|------------|
| Founder taste minimal · company brand expressive | Company Genome wins on **brand output** · taste informs **exploration** |
| Founder risk bold · company risk conservative | Company governance wins on **customer-facing** decisions |
| New company · fresh genome | No inherited company identity — only founder layers |

Founder may set **Company Taste Overlay** for intentional divergence — see [../founder-taste-engine/taste-transfer.md](../founder-taste-engine/taste-transfer.md).

## Lifecycle

```
Company created
        ↓
Adaptive Onboarding™ seeds genome
        ↓
Blueprint™ deepens identity
        ↓
Inauguration™ seals Charter
        ↓
Canon™ locks creative direction
        ↓
Golden Builds™ certify expression
        ↓
Evolution™ continuous updates
        ↓
Legacy™ preserved in Archive™
```

Company Genome™ never deleted — archived when organization sunsets.

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Copy Company A genome to Company B | Each company unique |
| Founder genome replaces company genome | Wrong scope |
| Genome static after inauguration | Companies evolve |
| Skip genome on generation | Brand drift |
