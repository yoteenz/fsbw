# Founder Taste Genome™

**How the founder thinks creatively**

---

## Purpose

Define **Founder Taste Genome™** within the Founder Intelligence System™ — the permanent profile of **creative instincts** learned from every approval · rejection · comparison · and concept selection.

**Full taste engine specification:** [../founder-taste-engine/](../founder-taste-engine/)

This document defines taste genome's **role in the three-genome architecture**.

---

## Core Law

Every approval teaches Studio OS something.  
Every rejection teaches Studio OS something.  
Every comparison teaches Studio OS something.

The important part is **WHY** — not simply **WHAT** was selected.

## Taste Learning™

### Concept First

Important creative decisions present **multiple complete concepts**:

Room · website · packaging · logo · campaign · animation · product · photography · UI · marketing · launch strategy · social graphics · brand identity · office design · event theme.

### Learning Flow

```
Present concepts A · B · C
        ↓
Founder selects direction
        ↓
Analyze WHY (traits in selected vs rejected)
        ↓
Update Founder Taste Genome™ weights
        ↓
Feed Predictive Intelligence™
```

**Detail:** [../founder-taste-engine/taste-learning.md](../founder-taste-engine/taste-learning.md)

## Scope & Portability

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Owner** | Founder ID |
| **Persistence** | Permanent · cross-company |
| **Maturity** | Increases with every creative decision |
| **Transfer** | Active on day one of new company |

Company Genome™ changes per venture. **Founder taste does not.**

## Founder Genome vs Taste Genome

| Situation | Genome |
|-----------|--------|
| Founder prefers morning strategic meetings | Founder Genome™ (working habits) |
| Founder prefers cinematic glass environments | Taste Genome™ (architectural taste) |
| Founder makes fast instinct decisions | Founder Genome™ (decision style) |
| Founder rejects cluttered dashboards | Taste Genome™ (minimalism · interaction) |

Both consult together — never merge into one profile.

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
