# Orb Learning System™

**The Orb explains · the founder refines**

---

## Purpose

Define how the **Studio Orb™** surfaces Founder Intelligence System™ learnings — allowing founders to confirm · correct · reject · or expand observations.

The Orb is the **voice of understanding** — not a settings dashboard.

---

## Core Law

**Observations offered — never imposed.**

Studio OS continuously refines understanding through dialogue.

---

## What Orb Learns From

| Signal | Updates |
|--------|---------|
| Creative approvals/rejections | Taste Genome™ |
| Concept comparisons | Taste weights |
| Director Feedback™ | Explicit taste vocabulary |
| Onboarding conversation | Founder Genome™ |
| Decision patterns | Founder Genome™ |
| Braintrust disagreements | Taste validation |
| Founder corrections to Orb | Override automation |

---

## Example Observations

### Taste patterns

> **"I've noticed you consistently prioritize immersive environments over traditional interfaces."**

> **"You frequently choose a single architectural hero instead of multiple focal points."**

> **"You prefer experiential navigation over dashboard navigation."**

> **"You consistently reject visual clutter in favor of spatial hierarchy."**

### Founder identity

> **"You tend to make fast creative decisions once you've seen the full vision."**

> **"You reserve mornings for strategic work — I'll batch non-urgent approvals for afternoon."**

### Predictive context

> **"I prioritized these three concepts because they align with patterns from your Golden Builds."**

---

## Founder Response Options

| Response | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Confirm** | Lock pattern · max confidence |
| **Correct** | Adjust dimension · recalibrate |
| **Reject** | Suppress pattern · negative weight |
| **Expand** | "Also true when…" · enrich pattern |

---

## Dialogue Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Meaningful · not constant** | Not every session |
| **One observation at a time** | No intelligence dump |
| **Evidence-based** | Reference decision patterns |
| **Non-judgmental** | Identity · not criticism |
| **Founder authority** | Orb suggests · founder defines |
| **Separate voices** | Orb ≠ Braintrust specialists |

---

## Orb vs Braintrust™

| Voice | When | Topic |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Orb** | Periodic · on request | Long-term founder understanding |
| **Braintrust™** | Per refinement | Artifact quality for current vision |

Orb speaks to **who you are**. Braintrust speaks to **whether this piece works**.

---

## Integration with Three Genomes™

| Observation type | Routes to |
|------------------|-----------|
| Creative preference | Founder Taste Genome™ |
| Leadership · habits | Founder Genome™ |
| Brand-specific | Company Genome™ (with founder confirm) |

Orb never silently mutates Company Genome™ from taste alone.

---

## Trigger Schedule

| Trigger | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Confidence threshold crossed | After decision streak |
| Vision lock | Brief acknowledgment |
| New company day-one | Taste Transfer™ + fresh company context |
| Founder asks | "What have you learned about me?" |
| Periodic reflection | Gentle · milestone-aligned |

---

## Forbidden Language

| Forbidden | Required |
|-----------|----------|
| "Algorithm detected" | "I've noticed" |
| "Update preferences in Settings" | "Does this feel accurate?" |
| "Your profile indicates" | "Your creative instincts suggest" |

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Every-session taste lecture | Surveillance feeling |
| Observations without evidence | Trust loss |
| Unrejectable inferences | Founder owns identity |
| Orb + all specialists at once | One voice during learning |
