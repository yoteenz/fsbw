# Taste Transfer™

**Founder taste travels. Company genome changes. The founder does not.**

---

## Purpose

Define how **Founder Taste Genome™** persists across every company a founder creates — Studio OS already understands their instincts on day one of company two.

---

## Core Law

**Taste is founder-scoped. Not company-scoped.**

---

## The Scenario

```
Founder builds Company A (Frontal Slayer)
        ↓
Hundreds of creative decisions
        ↓
Founder Taste Genome™ matures
        ↓
Founder creates Company B (new venture)
        ↓
Company Genome™ = fresh (new brand · mission · industry)
Founder Taste Genome™ = inherited (creative instincts intact)
        ↓
Day-one concepts already feel familiar
```

---

## What Transfers

| Transfers | Does not transfer |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Visual taste patterns | Company mission · vision |
| Material preferences | Industry-specific knowledge |
| Architectural instincts | Brand colors · logos |
| Motion · interaction taste | Customer personas |
| Creative risk tolerance | Operational workflows |
| Decision patterns | Profession Brain™ content |
| Established taste patterns | Organization Charter |

---

## Day-One Experience (Company N)

When founder enters new company:

| Without Taste Transfer™ | With Taste Transfer™ |
|-------------------------|----------------------|
| Generic concept generation | Taste-weighted concepts immediately |
| Re-learn preferences from zero | "I already know you prefer minimal luxury" |
| Full exploration phase | Faster alignment · one exploratory outlier |
| Founder re-explains aesthetic | Orb applies inherited taste |

Orb may acknowledge:

> **"I'll apply your established creative instincts to this new company. Your Company Genome™ will be unique — but I already understand how you think."**

## Company Taste Overlay

Founder may intentionally **diverge** for a specific company:

```typescript
interface CompanyTasteOverlay {
  companyId: string;
  founderId: string;
  adjustments: Partial<TasteDimensionMap>;
  reason: string;
  expiresAt?: string;           // optional temporary overlay
}
```

Example: "This brand is playful — override my usual minimal preference."

Overlay affects **generation context only** — core Founder Taste Genome™ unchanged.

---

## Multi-Company Portfolio

For founders with holding company / portfolio:

| Layer | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **Founder Taste Genome™** | Shared across all companies |
| **Company Taste Overlay** | Per-company adjustments |
| **Organization Genome™** | Per-company identity |
| **Canon™** | Per-company creative truth |

Predictive Design™ consults: `Taste Genome + Company Overlay + Organization Genome`.

---

## Privacy & Separation

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Taste does not leak between founders | Strict founder ID scope |
| Company A Canon does not auto-apply to B | Separate Canon per company |
| Taste export is founder-owned | Portable creative identity |
| Team members do not inherit founder taste | Separate profiles (future) |

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Reset taste per company | Violates transfer law |
| Copy Company A Canon to B | Companies are distinct |
| Taste tied to email domain | Founder may use multiple emails |
| Force taste on collaborative teams | Taste is founder-scoped in V1 |
