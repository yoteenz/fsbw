# Emotional Design Principle™ — System 005

**System:** Emotional Design Principle™  
**Status:** Canonical experience law  
**Scope:** Every Studio OS department · Headquarters · The Archive™

---

## Purpose

Every Studio OS department should make the founder **feel something**.

The emotion must come from the **environment itself** — not from explanatory text.

---

## Core Law

| Source of emotion | Not acceptable |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Materials · light · sound · space · motion · objects | Paragraphs · onboarding copy · feature lists |
| Architectural composition | Dashboard widgets |
| Ceremony weight | Toast notifications |

---

## Creative Direction Studio™ — Inspired

CDS establishes the **reference emotional profile**.

### Environmental levers (Room DNA™)

| Lever | Inspired expression |
|-------|---------------------|
| `luxuryLevel` | High — marble · brass · reflection depth |
| `editorialLevel` | High — drama · contrast · fashion-forward |
| `warmthLevel` | Warm key — rose-gold · inviting |
| `calmLevel` | Calm not sleepy — confident creative stillness |
| `glassLevel` | Editorial glass · pin surfaces · depth |
| `ceremonyWeight` | Approval moments feel significant |

### Anti-emotion for CDS

| Forbidden feel | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| Generic SaaS | Breaks immersive law |
| Cold corporate | Not creative headquarters |
| Cluttered startup | Not editorial luxury |
| Anxious urgency | Inspired ≠ stressed |

### Founder should feel

*"I want to create something extraordinary here."*

Not:

*"I need to complete these form fields."*

## Measurement (Qualitative)

Emotional Design is validated in **Walk the Room™** and founder review — not NPS surveys.

| Question | Pass |
|----------|------|
| Stranger enters — do they feel the target emotion in 30s? | Yes |
| Is emotion from environment not copy? | Yes |
| Does idle life sustain emotion when still? | Yes |
| Does arrival sequence set emotional tone? | Yes |
