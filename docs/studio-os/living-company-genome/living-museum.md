# The Living Museum™ — Company Story in Headquarters

**Module:** `studio.living-company-genome.v1.living-museum`  
**Status:** Visitors experience the company story

---

## Principle

> Every company should eventually contain museum wings. Visitors don't tour a headquarters — they experience the story of the company.

---

## Museum Wings (Canonical)

| Wing | Contents | Unlock Typical |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Founder Hall™** | Founder story · logo chapters · letters · vision timeline | Growing maturity |
| **Launch Gallery™** | Campaigns · product launches · go-live moments | First Major Launch™ |
| **Innovation Museum™** | Patents · prototypes · experiments · Version 1 room | Innovation DNA events |
| **Prototype Vault™** | Archived concepts · braintrust branches | Creative culture |
| **Legacy Archive™** | Documents · charters · decisions · deep history | Legacy Vault sync |
| **Wall of Milestones™** | Awards · customer tiers · funding · expansion | Milestone density |
| **Evolution Timeline™** | Interactive Multiple Timelines™ · capsule portals | Established maturity |

Wings **unlock earned** — empty museum at genesis is correct.

---

## Museum vs Operations

| Zone | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Operations wings** | Daily work · departments |
| **Museum wings** | Story · memory · ceremony |

Circulation connects both — founder walks from Story Table™ to Launch Gallery™ without "leaving Studio OS."

---

## Visitor Experience

External visitors (investor · partner · customer · hire):

```
Arrival Sequence™
    ↓
Optional: Museum tour Routine™
    ↓
Founder Hall™ → Launch Gallery™ → Innovation Museum™
    ↓
Orb narrates Legacy Anchors™
    ↓
Exit to operational HQ or department
```

Tour depth policy-gated — sensitive capsules private.

## Exhibit Standards

| Standard | Rule |
