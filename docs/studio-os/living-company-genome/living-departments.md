# Living Departments™ — Rooms Gain History

**Module:** `studio.living-company-genome.v1.living-departments`  
**Status:** Departments age — nothing meaningful disappears

---

## Principle

> Departments should age. Walls gain history. Awards appear. Past launches become exhibits.

---

## What Ages In Place

| Artifact | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| **Awards** | Appear on walls · pipeline · milestone displays |
| **Past launches** | Become Mood Wall · gallery exhibits |
| **Archived concepts** | Museum pieces — not deleted |
| **Prototype rooms** | Preserved as-is · Time Capsule linked |
| **Retired branding** | Accessible in department history wing |
| **Golden Build versions** | Prior scene stacks viewable |
| **Campaign artifacts** | Environmental storytelling props |

---

## Department Memory Layers

```
Active production layer     (current work)
         +
Historical exhibit layer    (launches · awards · campaigns)
         +
Prototype preservation      (branch explorations approved for archive)
         +
Retired identity access     (old logos · palettes · copy)
```

Founder toggles **Today™** vs **History view™** within department — not separate apps.

---

## Scene Stack™ Preservation

Each approved Scene Stack™ era may remain accessible:

```yaml
DepartmentSceneHistory:
  departmentId: creative-direction
  eras:
    - eraId: golden-build-v1-launch
      capsuleId: capsule:cds-pre-rebrand-2027
      sceneStackVersion: scene-stack-v1
      label: "Version 1 Launch Era"
    - eraId: golden-build-v2-award
      capsuleId: capsule:cds-post-award-2028
      sceneStackVersion: scene-stack-v2
      label: "Award Season Refinement"
```

Regenerating one layer **forward** does not erase prior era capsules.
