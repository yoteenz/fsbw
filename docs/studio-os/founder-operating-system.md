# Founder Operating System™ V1.0 (Milestone 118 · Studio OS Version 1 Culmination)

**Route:** `/admin/studio/founder-operating-system`

## Purpose

**Founder Operating System™** operates the founder while Studio OS operates the organization.

> Founders grow first. Organizations follow.

## Studio OS V1 final promise

**PRESERVE EXPERTISE. BUILD LEGACY. EMPOWER VISIONARIES.**

M118 completes Studio OS Version 1 — Studio OS no longer simply manages organizations; it develops the people who build them.

## Core philosophy

- Healthy organizations require healthy leaders
- Studio OS continuously supports founder effectiveness, focus, leadership, and long-term growth
- By strengthening the founder, Studio OS strengthens the entire organization

API: `buildFounderIntelligenceSnapshots()` · `summarizeFounderIntelligence()`

## Executive coaching

Leadership observations · Meeting recommendations · Focus improvements · Delegation opportunities · Communication improvements · Learning recommendations · Strategic reflection · Executive habits · Decision quality · Founder development

## Focus management

Protects deep work · creative sessions · strategic planning · personal learning · recovery time.

Reduces interruptions · batches low-priority work · recommends delegation · optimizes calendars.

API: `buildFocusProtectionActions()` · `summarizeFocusActions()`

Leadership Growth · Focus Score · Decision Load · Executive Health · Learning Progress · Delegation Opportunities · Meeting Effectiveness · Strategic Time · Burnout Risk · Creative Momentum

API: `buildPersonalDashboardMetrics()` · `summarizePersonalDashboard()`
