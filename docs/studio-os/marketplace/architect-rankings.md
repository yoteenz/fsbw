# Architect Rankings™

**Reputation growth — not sales volume alone**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical ranking architecture (docs only)  
**Parent:** [Studio Architects™](./studio-architects.md)

---

## Purpose

Define **Architect Level™** progression — how Studio Architects™ grow through reputation · quality · and community impact.

Ranking rewards **craft** — not simply sales volume.

---

## Architect Levels™

| Level | Character | Typical requirements |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Emerging Architect™** | First publications · proving craft | 1+ Golden Build™ HQ · valid packages |
| **Professional Architect™** | Consistent quality · growing installs | Multiple HQ · satisfaction threshold |
| **Master Architect™** | Recognized body of work | Portfolio depth · retention · innovation |
| **Studio Certified™** | Platform-verified excellence | Full certification gate passed |
| **Legendary Architect™** | Iconic · cultural presence | Featured history · community impact · longevity |

**Studio Certified™** is both a **quality tier** and an **architect level milestone**.

Not everyone reaches Legendary — prestige requires earned history.

---

## Ranking Signals

| Signal | Weight | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Quality** | Highest | Studio Certified™ · review scores |
| **Founder satisfaction** | Highest | Post-install experience ratings |
| **Innovation** | High | Original envelopes · new genre |
| **Retention** | High | HQ still active months later |
| **Originality** | High | Not derivative of existing HQ |
| **Community impact** | Medium | Followers · case study engagement |
| **Downloads / installs** | Medium | Popularity — not sole driver |
| **Sales volume** | Low | Commerce alone never sufficient |

---

## Level Progression (Example)

```
Emerging Architect™
    ↓  (3+ published HQ · avg satisfaction ≥ 4.2)
Professional Architect™
    ↓  (Studio Certified™ + 10k installs + retention ≥ 60%)
Master Architect™
    ↓  (Featured Collection™ + innovation score + 3yr active)
Legendary Architect™
    ↓  (community vote · platform editorial · iconic named HQ)
```

Thresholds are **tunable** — philosophy is signal weighting.

## What Ranking Is NOT

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| Top seller leaderboard | **Master Architect™** recognition |
| Pay-to-rank | Earned through certification |
| Volume-only chart | Quality-gated visibility |
| Follower count alone | Engagement + satisfaction |

## Decay & Maintenance

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Decertified HQ | Level review |
| Satisfaction drop | Visibility reduction |
| Long inactivity | Dormant status — not penalized harshly |
| New masterpiece | Accelerated progression |

Architects maintain reputation through **continued craft** — not one-hit volume.
