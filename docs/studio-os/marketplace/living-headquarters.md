# Living Headquarters™

**Complete living environments — the primary marketplace object**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical product definition (docs only)  
**Parent:** [The Economy of Studio World™](./README.md)

---

## Purpose

Define **Living Headquarters™** — the unified marketplace product that combines [Living Sets™](../world/living-sets.md) philosophy with complete, installable, evolving headquarters experiences.

Living Headquarters™ are what founders **buy · renovate · collect · and sell**.

---

## One Sentence

A **Living Headquarters™** is a complete, genome-adaptive, continuity-aware headquarters environment that transforms with the company — delivered as a single Headquarters Package™.

---

## Living Headquarters™ vs Living Set™

| Concept | Scale | Scope |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **Living Set™** | Department | Single department transforms per company |
| **Living Headquarters™** | Campus | Full HQ topology · multiple Living Sets™ · shared circulation |

Every Living Headquarters™ **contains** Living Sets™.

Marketplace can sell at either scale — Living Headquarters™ is the **premium whole**.

---

## What Makes a Headquarters "Living"

Inherited from [Living Sets™](../world/living-sets.md):

| Property | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| **Framework invariant** | Arrival · Orb · Story Table · Mood Wall · Review · Pipeline · Branch · Timeline |
| **World transforms** | Architecture · materials · lighting per Company Genome™ |
| **Set Continuity™** | Evolves Blueprint™ → Legacy™ |
| **Not skinning** | Full environmental regeneration |
| **Orb-aware** | Host explains reconfiguration |

Living Headquarters™ adds **HQ-scale** continuity:

- Wings expand
- Campus skyline evolves
- Cross-department circulation persists
- Headquarters History™ tracks versions
