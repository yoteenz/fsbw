## Mission

## Studio Architect™ vs Founder

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Founder** | Runs the company inside the headquarters |
| **Studio Architect™** | Designs the place where that company is imagined · built · operated · remembered |

Architects do not decorate software.

They design **places**.
