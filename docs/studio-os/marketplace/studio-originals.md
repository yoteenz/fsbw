## Onboarding Flow

```
1. Founder enters Studio OS
        ↓
2. Orb learns about the founder (conversation — not forms)
        ↓
3. Founder selects or accepts recommended Mode™
        ↓
4. Studio OS recommends 3–5 Studio Originals™
   based on:
   • Founder Genome™
   • Founder Taste Genome™
   • Company Genome™
        ↓
5. Founder selects Original
        ↓
6. Headquarters installs instantly
        ↓
7. Founder immediately begins building
   — no waiting · no GPU generation
```

**Sources:** [Adaptive Onboarding™](../onboarding/README.md) · [Taste Recommendations™](./taste-recommendations.md)

## Studio Originals™ vs Premium Generation™

| Path | When | GPU |
|------|------|-----|
| **Studio Original™** | Day one · always available | No |
| **Marketplace purchase** | Founder wants creator environment | No (pre-built) |
| **Renovation™** | Founder improves over time | Light (packs) |
| **Premium Generation™** | Founder wants unique commission | Full pipeline |

Original is **foundation** — not permanent ceiling.
