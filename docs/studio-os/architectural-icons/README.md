# Architectural Icons™

**The Landmarks of Studio World™**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical architectural law (docs only)  
**Phase:** Environmental identity sprint (pre-implementation)  
**Scope:** Permanent landmark identity for every Department™ and Headquarters™

---

> **Great software has features. Great worlds have landmarks.**

---

## Mission

People do not remember software interfaces.

They remember **places**.

Disney is remembered by its castle.  
Apple Park is remembered by the Ring.  
Pixar is remembered by the Atrium.  
NASA is remembered by Mission Control.

**Studio World™** should be remembered exactly the same way.

Every department contains one unforgettable **architectural landmark** — its **Signature Landmark™**.

---

## Foundational Law

```
Every Department™
        ↓
One Signature Landmark™
        ↓
Landmark = Department Identity
```

When founders think about a room — they immediately picture its landmark.

---

## One Sentence

**Architectural Icons™** is the permanent law that every Department™ and Headquarters™ in Studio World™ must possess exactly one **Signature Landmark™** — an unforgettable architectural masterpiece that is functional · recognizable · and inseparable from that place's identity.
