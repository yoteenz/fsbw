# Studio Intelligence Layer™ Architecture

**Canonical source:** `genesis/articles/STUDIO_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md`  
**Content home:** `genesis/studio-intelligence-layer/`  
**Status:** Architecture drafted; runtime implementation planned

---

## Purpose

Studio Intelligence Layer™ is the executive reasoning system that lets Studio OS
understand companies instead of merely storing information.

It connects:

- Company Genome™
- Brand DNA™
- Experience Engine™
- Institute of Knowledge™
- Experience Runtime™
- Orb™
- Mission Engine™
- Executive Headquarters™

Studio OS should reason like a senior executive team before recommending action.

---

## Intelligence systems

| System | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Company Operating Manual™** | Living operational doctrine every AI worker consults before acting |
| **Decision DNA™** | Founder decision model: risk, trade-offs, approvals, outcomes |
| **Taste Genome™** | Founder creative fingerprint: approved/rejected patterns and confidence |
| **Canon Engine™** | Governance for what becomes temporary, experiment, canon, amendment, pattern, or archive |
| **Experience Compiler™** | Intelligence-aware compiler for complete branded operating environments |
| **Audience DNA™** | Permanent audience intelligence for marketing/product/content |
| **Product DNA™** | Product identity, promise, launch, packaging, lifecycle, content, reviews |
| **Creative Genome™** | Creative knowledge graph connecting campaigns, assets, decisions, approvals |

---

## Executive recommendation frame

Every meaningful recommendation should consider:

Brand · Audience · Products · Founder preferences · Business goals · Knowledge ·
History · Creative direction · Financial impact · Operational impact · Long-term
platform implications.

---

## Canon rule

Nothing becomes canonical automatically. Founder approval is always required for
Company Canon, Brand Canon, Knowledge Canon, Genesis Amendments, Experience DNA,
and Platform Patterns.

---

## Relationship to Experience Engine / Runtime

Experience Engine™ defines the DNA inheritance model. Experience Runtime™
executes it. **Experience Compiler™** selects and weights the intelligence/DNA
inputs for a mission, role, device, audience, state, and business context before
runtime assembly.
