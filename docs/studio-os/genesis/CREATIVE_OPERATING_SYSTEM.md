## Prime rule

Creative Direction Studio™ should feel like entering the headquarters of a
world-class creative company.

Studio Production System™ coordinates individual productions.

Creative Operating System™ governs the continuous creative organization above
those productions: the board, council, department leadership, memory, learning,
asset economy, and evolution loop that make every production improve the next.

---

## Permanent organization

```text
Studio Intelligence™
  -> Creative Operating System™
    -> Executive Creative Board™
    -> Creative Council™
    -> Department Leadership™
    -> Creative Memory™
    -> Production Memory™
    -> Learning Engine™
    -> Creative Evolution Engine™
    -> Creative Economy™
      -> Studio Production System™
        -> Production Package™
          -> Production Departments™
            -> Publishing / Performance / Learning
```

---

## Executive Creative Board™

Permanent board members:

- Chief Creative Officer™
- Executive Producer™
- Creative Strategist™
- Narrative Director™
- Brand Director™
- Audience Director™
- Experience Director™
- Production Director™
- Performance Director™
- Knowledge Director™

Each executive owns one domain of intelligence and must debate recommendations
using evidence instead of opinion.

The founder always makes the final decision.

---

## Creative Council™

Before every major production, campaign, launch, curriculum, headquarters scene,
or brand-defining asset, the Creative Council™ convenes.

Every council session must collect:

- creative opportunities
- brand risks
- audience considerations
- operational impact
- resource requirements
- performance predictions
- narrative consistency
- product alignment
- educational value
- long-term strategic impact

The output is one **Unified Creative Recommendation™** with evidence, risks,
trade-offs, predictions, dissent, and founder decision options.

---

## Creative Memory™ and Production Memory™

Creative Memory™ captures:

- decisions
- reasoning
- approvals
- rejected concepts
- performance
- audience reactions
- lessons learned
- visual discoveries
- narrative discoveries
- production discoveries
- brand discoveries

Production Memory™ captures operational learning from Production Packages™:
department assignments, blockers, gates, QC issues, revisions, timing deltas,
publishing problems, and reusable production solutions.

Everything becomes searchable.

Nothing valuable is forgotten.

---

## Learning and evolution

After every production, Learning Engine™ compares predictions against outcomes:

- performance
- audience reaction
- brand risk
- resource requirements
- narrative clarity
- educational value
- conversion / engagement / retention

Creative Evolution Engine™ turns those comparisons into update proposals for:

- Narrative Intelligence™
- Production Genome™
- Brand Intelligence™
- Audience Intelligence™
- Decision DNA™
- Taste Genome™
- Creative Genome™
- creative patterns

Analytics must improve future reasoning, not merely report results.

---

## Creative Economy™

Every department can produce reusable company resources:

- visual systems
- narrative templates
- production blueprints
- motion systems
- music themes
- characters
- virtual sets
- transitions
- knowledge modules
- prompt assets
- proof frameworks
- lighting recipes
- camera patterns
- editorial rhythms
- distribution packages

These are registered as **Creative Economy Assets™** with provenance, status,
version history, performance history, reuse recommendations, and canon state.

---

## Executive Headquarters integration

Creative Operating System™ connects directly to:

- Executive Headquarters™ — active initiatives, board decisions, creative health
- Mission Control™ — status, blockers, priorities, publishing readiness
- Institute of Knowledge™ — memory, lessons, knowledge modules, playbooks
- Company Genome™ — permanent creative capabilities and operating doctrine
- Studio Foundry™ — reusable visual systems, sets, motion, prompt assets
- Orb™ — founder-facing interpreter of board debate, memory, and recommendations

Every creative initiative should become visible across Studio OS.

## Implementation note

Runtime implementation should treat Creative Operating System™ as the company
organization above Studio Production System™, not as a collection of isolated
agent personas. Its durable objects are board members, council sessions,
recommendations, memories, learning events, evolution proposals, and creative
economy assets.
