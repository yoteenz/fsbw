## Purpose

Studio OS treats brand as **living strategic intelligence**, not colors/fonts/logos.

Three permanent systems:

1. **Brand Discovery Engine™** — discovers canonical Brand DNA™ from evidence + Orb-led founder interview
2. **Brand DNA™ (Strategic)** — machine-readable identity genome influencing every brand decision
3. **Brand Intelligence Layer™** — reusable reasoning API every creative/strategic system consults before shipping

Plus **Brand Elevation Engine™** and **Brand Consistency Check™**.

---

## Strategic vs Experience Brand DNA

| Layer | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| **Strategic Brand DNA™** | Philosophy, audience, positioning, voice, packaging, luxury, rules |
| **Experience Brand DNA™** | Expression layer compiled for Experience Engine™ / Runtime™ |

Do not redesign Experience Engine or Runtime. Compile strategic DNA downward.

---

## Brand Intelligence questions

Before any brand output ships:

- Does this match Brand DNA™?
- Does this strengthen the brand?
- Does this attract the right audience?
- Does this contradict the brand?
- Does this feel premium enough?
- Does this support positioning?

---

## Brand Consistency Check™ dimensions

Visual · Voice · Audience · Luxury · Positioning · Emotional · Market Fit · Differentiation · Conversion Potential

Default pass: **80/100** with no critical contradiction.
