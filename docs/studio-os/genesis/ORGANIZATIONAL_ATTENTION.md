# Organizational Attention™

**Genesis article:** [`../../../genesis/articles/ORGANIZATIONAL_ATTENTION.md`](../../../genesis/articles/ORGANIZATIONAL_ATTENTION.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional amendment proposal for Genesis review

## Decision

Organizational Attention™ is approved as the constitutional evolution of
Notification Engine™. Attention Delivery™ becomes its delivery subsystem.

It is not a Company Genome™ capability, a second priority/risk engine, or a
notification feed.

## Prime law

```text
Truth does not automatically deserve interruption.
Urgency does not automatically deserve executive action.
Attention is allocated through context, comparison, and accountable next action.
```

## Ownership

Organizational Attention™ owns:

- attention candidate normalization and assessment
- cross-source allocation, routing, timing, batching, and suppression
- attention budgets and interruption policy
- Attention Plans™, Attention Briefs™, delivery intent, and outcomes

It consumes but does not own:

- Company Genome™ organizational context
- Manual escalation/approval doctrine
- Decision Engine priority/risk/escalation decisions
- Institute evidence
- Studio Intelligence recommendations
- Mission and workflow state
- Orb presentation and Headquarters projections

## Classes

| Class | Treatment |
|---|---|
| A0 — observe | Aggregate without delivery |
| A1 — digest | Scheduled briefing |
| A2 — route | Delegate to smallest authorized owner |
| A3 — executive briefing | Material executive decision at an appropriate time |
| A4 — interrupt | Immediate, explainable, time-sensitive material risk/authority gap |

Only A4 interrupts. Attention allocation must favor actionability, authority,
confidence, and interruption cost—not event volume or engagement.

## Consumer boundaries

- Orb receives an Attention Brief™ and applies session-local tone/focus
  composition; it does not own company-wide allocation.
- Executive Headquarters™ renders an attention projection; it is not an
  attention registry.
- Workflow Engine™ may consume approved priority/deadline constraints but
  retains execution ownership.
- Studio Intelligence™ produces evidence-backed candidates and learns from
  outcomes; it cannot self-deliver or self-authorize escalation.

department competition, AI escalation, inheritance, scale, and safety.
