# Company Genome Evolution Lifecycle™

**Genesis article:** [`../../../genesis/articles/COMPANY_GENOME_EVOLUTION.md`](../../../genesis/articles/COMPANY_GENOME_EVOLUTION.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional amendment proposal for Genesis review

## Decision

Approve a permanent lifecycle governance capability of Company Genome™.

Do not create independent peer engines named Evolution Gate™ or Projection
Engine™.

## Lifecycle law

```text
Signals may teach the Genome.
Evidence may propose a revision.
Only a governed revision may change the active Genome.
Only an approved impact plan may activate affected projections.
History is immutable.
```

## Core objects

- Genome Evidence Package™
- Genome Evolution Proposal™
- Genome Evolution Gate™
- Company Genome Revision™
- Constitutional Diff™
- Projection Impact Plan™
- Projection Record™
- Restoration Proposal™
- Contradiction Case™

## Key rules

- Gate review is proportional: observation, low-impact learning, operational,
  material organizational, and constitutional change classes.
- A single company has one active revision, with drafts/proposals and historic
  revisions pinned for audit and in-flight work.
- The Projection Planner™ computes impact; each consumer retains authority to
  compile, validate, and activate its own projection.
- Archive/index/staleness actions can be automatic; activation of authority,
  workflow behavior, public experience, automation, or workspace changes is
  approval-based.
- Restoration is forward-only: create a new revision that restores selected
  prior meaning; never mutate history.
- Conflict precedence is domain-specific. Constitution, founder authority,
  applicable law, policy, security, and privacy always constrain evolution.

See the Genesis article for projection policy per consumer, in-flight migration,
contradiction resolution, scalability, and amendment requirements.
