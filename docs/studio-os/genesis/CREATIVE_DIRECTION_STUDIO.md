# Creative Direction Studio™

**Genesis article:** [`../../../genesis/articles/CREATIVE_DIRECTION_STUDIO.md`](../../../genesis/articles/CREATIVE_DIRECTION_STUDIO.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional amendment proposal for Genesis review

## Decision

Creative Direction Studio™ evolves from a marketing-content framing into the
universal founder-facing creative direction and production orchestration
experience.

“Creative Director Studio™” may be an experience label, but not a separate
constitutional system: Creative Director™ already names a production role.

## Universal creative scope

The Studio directs coherent expression across:

- campaigns, launches, advertising, email, and social
- product imagery, video, commerce, packaging, labels, and merchandising
- websites, applications, UI direction, onboarding, and customer journeys
- films, commercials, recurring series, and presentations
- retail, environmental branding, booths, events, and installations

These are expression families—not new channel engines.

## Ownership boundary

The Studio owns direction alternatives, orchestration plans, review state, and
the founder-facing creative workspace. It does not own Brand DNA, narrative
truth, production execution, assets, publishing, experience runtime, workflow
state, or company truth.

Material work flows through:

```text
Company/Brand/Design/Narrative inputs
  -> Creative Direction Studio™
  -> Production Genome™ + Production Package™
  -> Studio Production System™
  -> Foundry / specialist production
  -> Asset Registry™
  -> Content Engine™ or Experience Engine/Runtime™
```

Every material expression pins its governing Company Genome™, Brand DNA™,
Design Canon, Narrative/Production Genome, rights, and approval provenance.

See the Genesis article for touchpoint adaptation, direct-draft exceptions,
governance, outcome learning, multi-company scale, and anti-patterns.
