# Business & Operations Constitutional Amendment™

**Genesis article:** [`../../../genesis/articles/BUSINESS_AND_OPERATIONS_CONSTITUTION.md`](../../../genesis/articles/BUSINESS_AND_OPERATIONS_CONSTITUTION.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional architecture proposal for Genesis review  
**Decision:** Do not create new peer engines named Business DNA™ or Operations DNA™.

## Constitutional decision

The platform needs business and operating comprehension before it proposes
departments, AI authority, workflows, headquarters capabilities, or experiences.

That need is real. New peer DNA engines are not the correct architecture.

| Truth class | Owner |
|---|---|
| Business facts and learned company beliefs | Company Genome™ |
| Business Architecture™ / Operating Model™ | Versioned Company Genome™ facets |
| Learned execution preference | Company Genome™ Operational DNA™ strand |
| Operating doctrine / approvals / SOPs | Company Operating Manual™ |
| Executable process | Workflow Engine™ |
| Strategic identity | Brand Discovery / Strategic Brand DNA™ |
| Experience expression | Experience Engine™ |
| Runtime environment | Experience Runtime™ |

## Correct graph

```text
Founder context
  -> Business Discovery™
  -> Company Genome™
      -> Business Architecture™ facet
      -> Operating Model™ facet
      -> Operational DNA™ preference strand
  -> Studio Intelligence Layer™
      -> Operating Manual / Decision / Audience / Product intelligence
  -> Workflow Engine™
  -> Brand Discovery™
  -> Experience Compiler™
  -> Experience Engine™
  -> Experience Runtime™
```

## Key constraints

- Company Genome™ stays the apex company abstraction below founder context.
- Strategic Brand DNA™ consumes a versioned business brief; it does not inherit
  mutable business data.
- Experience Department DNA™ inherits from Experience Brand DNA™ only.
- Department Operating Charter™ is the distinct non-visual operating model.
- All onboarding output begins as confidence-scored, founder-reviewable
  proposals—not permanent truth.
- No consumer may create a duplicate company truth store.

See the Genesis article for the full responsibility matrix, onboarding sequence,
governance, risks, and constitutional amendments.
