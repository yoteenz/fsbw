# Company Genome™ — Living Organizational Genome

**Genesis article:** [`../../../genesis/articles/COMPANY_GENOME.md`](../../../genesis/articles/COMPANY_GENOME.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional architecture proposal for Genesis review  
**Decision:** Company Genome™ becomes the canonical organizational model—not a
universal database.

## Constitutional position

Company Genome™ is the single source of **organizational meaning**:

- identity, purpose, and strategic posture
- Business Architecture™
- Operating Model™
- governance and constraint applicability
- organization graph
- confidence-scored organizational learning

It is not the authoritative store for transactions, workflow execution state,
runtime state, evidence documents, asset binaries, or recommendations.

## Ownership contract

| Concern | Owner |
|---|---|
| Organizational meaning | Company Genome™ |
| Operating doctrine and SOPs | Company Operating Manual™ |
| Executable workflows | Workflow Engine™ |
| Reasoning and recommendations | Studio Intelligence Layer™ |
| Strategic identity | Brand Discovery / Strategic Brand DNA™ |
| Experience expression | Experience Engine™ |
| Rendering and runtime state | Experience Runtime™ |
| Evidence corpus | Institute of Knowledge™ |

## Derived, not duplicated

Company Genome™ emits versioned briefs and snapshots to:

- Company Operating Manual™
- Department Operating Charters™
- AI Workforce authority envelopes
- Workflow Engine™ proposals
- Strategic Brand DNA™ constraints
- Executive Headquarters™ requirements
- Experience Compiler™ and Experience DNA™
- Orb™, Mission Engine™, Narrative Intelligence™, and Creative Operating System™

Every material projection must retain Genome version, snapshot hash, provenance,
and staleness policy.

## Implementation gate

No runtime implementation is authorized until Company Genome™ relationships,
object model, versioning, contradiction handling, and activation gates are
reviewed alongside the Business & Operations Constitutional Amendment™.

Read the Genesis article for the complete domain model, onboarding sequence,
governance, scalability, and amendments.
