# Genesis v1 Constitutional Closure Audit™

**Genesis article:** [`../../../genesis/articles/GENESIS_V1_CONSTITUTIONAL_CLOSURE.md`](../../../genesis/articles/GENESIS_V1_CONSTITUTIONAL_CLOSURE.md)  
**Status:** Constitutional amendment proposal for Genesis review

## Verdict

Genesis v1 is not yet constitutionally complete. The audit rejects feature
requests and identifies five durable responsibility gaps that must close before
a no-new-systems constitutional freeze:

1. Obligation & Entity Governance™
2. Assurance, Safety & Resilience™
3. Domain Record Stewardship Framework™
4. Data, Integration & Temporal Integrity law
5. Constitutional Due Process & Continuity

## What does not need another system

Company Genome, Manual, Workflow, Decision, Intelligence, Institute, Memory,
Attention, Identity, Permissions, Orb, Headquarters, and Experience already
have bounded constitutional ownership. Do not duplicate them.

## Closure model

Two bounded capabilities govern external obligations/entity structures and
assurance/safety/resilience. A federated framework gives finance, workforce,
customer, commitments, resource/supply, and content/asset records a declared
authoritative source without creating a universal database. Two cross-cutting
laws govern data/integration/time integrity and constitutional due process.

After ratification, future domains must attach to these responsibility slots.
New peer engines or sources of truth are forbidden unless constitutional
reopening proves Genesis v1 incomplete.

See the Genesis article for ownership boundaries, permanent record domains,
dependency graph, risks, and amendments.
