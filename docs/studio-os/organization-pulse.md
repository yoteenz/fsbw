# Organization Pulse™ V1.0 (Milestone 100)

**Route:** `/admin/studio/organization-pulse`

## Purpose

**Organization Pulse™** continuously monitors the overall well-being of an organization.

Businesses are living systems. Revenue alone cannot describe organizational health. Studio OS monitors the organization's pulse in real time.

Mission Control communicates **how the organization feels** — not just what it earned.

## Proactive alerts

Studio OS identifies changes before they become problems:

- Founder workload increasing
- Customer response time declining
- Marketing momentum slowing
- Knowledge capture decreasing
- Employee onboarding incomplete
- Automation opportunities growing

Every alert includes **recommended actions**.
