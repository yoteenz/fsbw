# Collaborative Innovation Network™

**Status:** Canonical innovation economy — July 2026  
**Philosophy:** Founders co-invent — collaboration is invention, not shared editing.

---

## Mission

When two or more founders collaborate, Studio OS recognizes that entirely new intellectual property may be created. The Collaborative Innovation Network™ treats collaboration as **invention** — with transparent attribution, joint publishing, and automatic royalty distribution.

---

## Innovation Network™

Every founder carries:

- Company Genome™
- Creative Genome™
- Experience Genome™
- Innovation Profile™
- Blueprint Library™
- Creative Portfolio™

During collaboration, Studio OS creates a temporary **Collaboration Genome™** (Shared Genome™) that combines strengths so AI understands each person's contribution.

Engine: `src/studio-os-core/collaborative-innovation-network/shared-genome.ts`

---

## Live collaboration

Multiple founders exist inside the same workspace with:

- Presence

- Attribution

Engine: `live-collaboration.ts`

## Joint Innovations™

When Studio OS detects original invention:

1. Prompt to publish (Private · Company Only · Invite Only · Marketplace · Open Source · Licensed)
2. Create Innovation ID™
3. Joint Patent Record™
4. Innovation Timeline™
5. Contribution History™

Engine: `joint-innovations.ts`

## Contribution Engine™ & Royalty Engine™

Transparent contribution percentages across domains (Architecture, Creative Direction, Automation, Programming, Systems, Brand Strategy, Animation, Lighting, Storytelling, Operations).

Engines: `contribution-engine.ts` · `royalty-engine.ts`
