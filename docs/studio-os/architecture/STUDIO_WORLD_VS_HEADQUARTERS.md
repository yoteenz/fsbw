# Studio World vs Headquarters

## Hierarchy

```
Studio World
│
├── Canonical Main Departments (global infrastructure)
│   ├── Experience Lab
│   ├── Creative Director Studio
│   ├── Command Center
│   ├── Asset Registry
│   ├── Construction Mode
│   ├── City Council
│   ├── Marketplace
│   └── … (25 total)
│
└── Industry Pack Registry (founder HQ templates)
    ├── Hair Brand Pack
    ├── Medical Practice Pack
    ├── Law Firm Pack
    └── … (18 pack options)
```

## Canonical Studio World Infrastructure

- Belongs to Studio World globally
- Admin Founder generates via Experience Lab **BUILD STUDIO WORLD**
- Published to Studio World Registry
- Normal founders **use** but do not **own** or **regenerate**

## Industry Headquarters Packs

- Founder headquarters templates
- Admin Founder generates via Experience Lab **BUILD INDUSTRY PACKS**
- Founders receive a pack and customize their HQ instance through Creative Director Studio
- Contains shared HQ templates (Reception, Lobby) and industry-unique departments

## Experience Lab programs

| Program | Label | Target |
|---------|-------|--------|
| A | STUDIO WORLD INFRASTRUCTURE | Canonical main departments |
| B | INDUSTRY HEADQUARTERS PACKS | Official industry packs |

**Do not** label canonical main departments as packs.  
**Do not** place them beneath Hair Brand, Medical, Legal, or any industry.

## Founder access

| Role | Experience Lab | Creative Director Studio |
|------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Admin Founder | ✅ Both programs | ✅ |
| Normal Founder | ❌ | ✅ Customize HQ |

## Company selectors

Experience Lab **must not** show Frontal Slayer, NDX, or other founder companies as generation targets. Deprecated company selectors are flagged server-side and removed from UI.
