# Orb Onboarding™

**The Orb leads every founder's first entry into Studio OS**

---

## Purpose

Define how the **Studio Orb™** facilitates Mode selection — the first defining moment after Headquarters appears.

The founder should feel like they're choosing a **way of working** — not purchasing different software.

---

## Sequence

### 1 — Headquarters Emergence

| Beat | Experience |
|------|------------|
| Duration | 3–8 seconds (reduced motion: instant shell) |
| Visual | Arrival Plaza™ · soft light · no UI chrome |
| Audio | Ambient welcome · optional |
| Rule | No forms · no spinners |

### 2 — Orb Welcome

> **"Welcome to Studio OS."**  
> **"This is where your company will live."**

Personalize if returning founder:

> **"Welcome back to headquarters."**

### 3 — The Question

> **"How would you like to build today?"**

**Not:**
- "What template do you want?"
- "Select your industry"
- "Complete your profile"

### 4 — Mode Presentation

Orb presents **five Initial Modes™** conversationally — not a grid of SaaS plans.

| Mode | Orb one-liner |
|------|---------------|
| 🚀 Entrepreneur | "Building a company from the ground up" |
| 🏢 Enterprise | "Running an established organization" |
| 🎨 Creator | "Growing a personal brand and audience" |
| 🤝 Agency | "Delivering work for multiple clients" |
| 🏪 Franchise | "Operating across multiple locations" |

Founder taps Mode — Orb expands with warmth.

### 5 — Reassurance (Always)

> **"You can always change Modes later."**  
> **"Your Headquarters will evolve with you."**  
> **"Nothing is permanent."**

### 6 — Mode Confirmation

Mode-specific response — see individual Mode docs.

Example Entrepreneur:

> **"Excellent. We'll build around founder-led decision making…"**

### 7 — Bridge to Blueprint™

> **"When you're ready, we'll discover who your company is."**  
> **"For now — look around. This is yours."**

**No forced immediate Blueprint** — Mode moment breathes first.

---

## Orb Behavior Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **One voice** | Specialists do not interrupt onboarding |
| **No pressure** | Skip · defer · explore allowed |
| **Conversational** | Short paragraphs — not bullet dumps |
| **Company-first** | Language builds company — not configures software |
| **Spatial** | Orb speaks from center of arriving HQ |

---

## Follow-Up Questions (Onboarding)

| Founder asks | Orb handles |
|--------------|-------------|
| "What's the difference?" | Brief compare two modes |
| "Can I switch?" | Yes — Mode Evolution™ |
| "Which should I pick?" | Reflect goals — never dictate |
| "Is this another tool?" | One Company Engine™ — Mode shapes journey |
| "Skip for now?" | Default Entrepreneur with note — discouraged not blocked |

---

## Relationship to Other Onboarding

```
Orb Onboarding™ (Mode)
        ↓
Business Discovery Blueprint™ (identity)
        ↓
Organization Inauguration™ (ceremony)
        ↓
ENTER HEADQUARTERS
```

[Arrival Sequence™](../foundational-experience-systems/arrival-sequence.md) wraps physical entry; Orb Onboarding™ wraps **choice of journey**.

---

## World Integration

| Element | Onboarding role |
|---------|-----------------|
| Lighting | Warm · welcoming · not corporate |
| HQ scale | Matches selected Mode preview |
| Mood Wall | Empty — waiting for founder story |
| Story Table | Deferred |
| Review Mode | Off |

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Multi-page signup before Mode | Delays defining moment |
| Mode buried in settings | Misses first entry |
| All specialists speak at once | Orb is sole facilitator |
| Black screen between beats | Violates World Streaming™ |
