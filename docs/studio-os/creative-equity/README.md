## Mission

**Creative Budget™** measures production capacity — what can be created **today**.

**Creative Equity™** measures accumulated creative value — everything the company has built **over time**.

Studio OS must distinguish between **spending money** and **building lasting creative assets**.

---

## One Sentence

**Creative Equity™** is the lifetime creative net worth of a company — growing through Golden Builds™, Blueprints™, reuse, certification, marketplace influence, and every investment that compounds — never resetting.
