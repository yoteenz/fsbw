# Living Architecture™

**Architecture regenerates — it is not skinned**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical architectural law (docs only)  
**Parent:** [Living Sets™](./living-sets.md)

---

## Purpose

Define how **architecture** — envelope · proportion · structure · circulation · threshold — regenerates per company while preserving department framework zones.

Living Architecture™ is the **built environment layer** of Environment DNA™.

This is not interior decorating on a fixed mesh.

---

## Core Law

```
ARCHITECTURE IS THE FIRST BRAND SIGNAL
```

Before copy · before logo · before UI — the founder reads **ceiling height · material · light · threshold**.

Someone over the founder's shoulder should name the company from **architecture alone**.

## Architectural Regeneration Domains

### Envelope Genre

| Genre | Character | Company fit |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Editorial atelier** | Double-height · skylight · gallery sightlines | Luxury · fashion · beauty |
| **Innovation lab** | Open loft · floating planes · minimal structure | Tech · media · product |
| **Strategy headquarters** | Chambers · walnut · credible gravity | Legal · finance · advisory |
| **Culinary studio** | Kitchen adjacency · warm volume · service flow | Restaurant · hospitality |
| **Production soundstage** | Truss · cyclorama · equipment honest | Studio · manufacturing |
| **Museum gallery** | Procession · reverence · artifact focus | Legacy · Archive™ adjacency |

Envelope is selected by **Company Expression System™** — refined by **Taste Genome™**.

---

### Proportion & Scale

| Parameter | Low value | High value |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Ceiling height** | Intimate 9' chamber | Double-height 24' atelier |
| **Horizontal expanse** | Focused single room | Panoramic wing |
| **Threshold compression** | Tight door · drama on entry | Open portal · gradual reveal |
| **Human scale** | Founder seat dominant | Egalitarian circle |
| **Object hero scale** | Single plinth | Full product wall |

Scale communicates **ambition** and **maturity**.

Growth™ stage may increase perceived scale without rebuilding framework.

| Zone | Architectural anchor |

| **Orb™** | Volumetric center · float plane above Story Table™ |
| **Story Table™** | Primary horizontal surface · narrative focal length |
| **Mood Wall™** | Dominant vertical plane · hero wall |

| **Reference Library™** | Flanking storage · depth · archive adjacency |
| **Production Pipeline™** | Wall-mounted board · legible from arrival |
| **Branch Gallery™** | Secondary gallery wing · discovery depth |

## Spatial Hierarchy

Living Architecture™ respects [Spatial Hierarchy™](../creative-direction-studio/spatial-hierarchy.md):

```
BACKGROUND   — Library · windows · campus · depth
MIDGROUND    — Mood Wall · Pipeline · Gallery · Timeline
FOREGROUND   — Story Table · Review · founder touch
SPATIAL CENTER — Orb™ above Story Table™
```

Company expression shifts **materials and form** within hierarchy — not hierarchy itself.

## HQ-Wide Architectural Consistency

Individual Living Sets™ express company identity — but **headquarters shares**:

| Shared architectural DNA | Per-set variation |
|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Campus beyond glass | Industry envelope |
| Floor plate logic | Wing materials |
| Circulation grammar | Threshold character |
| Time-of-day lighting | Interior drama |
| Elevator · corridor typology | Department destination |

Founder believes **one building** contains **many expressions**.

## Evolution Over Time

[Set Continuity™](./set-continuity.md) affects architecture:

| Stage | Architectural shift |
|-------|----------------------|
| Blueprint™ | Sparse · visible structure · unfinished edges |
| Golden Build™ | First built-in display · shelf millwork |
| Launch™ | Campaign fascia · temporary architectural dressing |
| Growth™ | Expanded millwork · additional display bays |
| Legacy™ | Museum casing · Archive™ portal architecture |

Architecture **accumulates** — wings do not demolish prior story without Archive™ capture.

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Violation |
|--------------|-----------|

| Remove ceiling variation | Flat SaaS box |
| Logo wall as architecture | Sticker identity |
| Ignore threshold | Arrival feels like route change |
| Inconsistent scale jumps | Breaks HQ believability |
