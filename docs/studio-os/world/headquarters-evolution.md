# Headquarters Evolution™

**The headquarters tells the story of the company**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical evolution architecture (docs only)  
**Parent:** [Living Sets™](./living-sets.md)

---

## Purpose

Define how **Studio World™ headquarters** evolves over the company's lifetime — not as a static campus snapshot, but as a **living autobiography** expressed through architectural growth, department transformation, and accumulated history.

Headquarters Evolution™ operates at **HQ scale**. [Set Continuity™](./set-continuity.md) operates at **department scale**.

Together they ensure the founder's world **grows up with the business**.

---

## Core Law

```
THE HEADQUARTERS IS THE COMPANY'S LIVING AUTOBIOGRAPHY
```

Products appear. Campaigns appear. Customer milestones appear. Awards appear. Wings expand.

The founder returns to a place that **remembers**.

---

## Evolution Scope

| Scale | System | What evolves |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| **Department** | Set Continuity™ | Single Living Set™ density · props · stage |
| **Wing / floor** | Headquarters Evolution™ | New departments · expanded circulation |
| **Campus** | World Evolution™ | Skyline · wings · earned spaces |
| **World state** | World States™ | Launch day · celebration · morning atmosphere |
| **Memory** | World Memory™ · Archive™ | Preserved snapshots · lineage |

## Department Transformation at HQ Scale

When Company Genome™ shifts (rebrand · pivot · maturity):

1. **Transformation Engine™** regenerates **all active Living Sets™**
2. **Framework zones** remain invariant across departments
3. **Expression** shifts coherently — founder recognizes one company
4. **Archive™** captures pre-regeneration HQ snapshot
5. **Orb** — *"Your headquarters now reflects the rebrand. History is preserved."*

Partial regeneration (single department) is allowed for **project moments** — full HQ regeneration for **company identity shifts**.

## Multi-Department Coherence

All departments in one headquarters share:

| Coherent | Variable |
|----------|----------|
| Time of day · weather | Industry expression per department |
| Campus beyond glass | Department function |
| Circulation grammar | Living Set™ envelope |
| Founder presence | Production stage density |
| Archive™ accessibility | Department-specific props |

Founder senses **one company** across wings — not unrelated theme parks.
