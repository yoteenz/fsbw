# Set Continuity™

**The Living Set™ evolves with the project**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical continuity architecture (docs only)  
**Parent:** [Living Sets™](./living-sets.md)

---

## Purpose

A Living Set™ is not a static snapshot.

It is a **time-aware environment** that accumulates history, celebrates milestones, and tells the company's story through physical change.

Set Continuity™ ensures the department **grows up with the business**.

---

## Core Law

```
THE ROOM REMEMBERS WHAT THE COMPANY ACHIEVED
```

Walls gain history. Awards appear. Products appear. Campaigns appear. Customer milestones appear.

The headquarters becomes a **living autobiography**.

---

## Continuity Arc

```
Blueprint™
    ↓
Golden Build™
    ↓
Launch™
    ↓
Growth™
    ↓
Legacy™
```

| Stage | Environmental character | What appears |
|-------|------------------------|--------------|
| **Blueprint™** | Potential · sparse · drafting energy | Raw materials · empty plinths · sketch surfaces · possibility lighting |
| **Golden Build™** | First proof · ceremony | Hero object installed · approval lighting · first shelf artifact |
| **Launch™** | Celebration · urgency · public-facing | Campaign walls · countdown · press energy · launch props |
| **Growth™** | Accumulation · busier life · customer proof | Milestone counters · product expansion · team presence · busier idle life |
| **Legacy™** | Patina · reverence · museum quality | Awards · Archive™ portals · historical timeline · earned wear |

Stages **accumulate** — Launch does not erase Blueprint history.

## Version Model

Every meaningful continuity shift produces a **Living Set Version™**:

```
creative-direction/frontal-slayer/
├── blueprint-v1          (sparse atelier)
├── golden-build-v1       (first hero object)
├── launch-week-v1        (campaign dressing)
├── first-million-v1      (milestone shelf)
├── rebrand-v2            (full expression regeneration)
└── legacy-v1             (museum posture)
```

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `versionId` | Unique continuity snapshot |
| `parentVersionId` | Lineage chain |
| `stage` | Primary production stage at capture |
| `artifactsAdded` | New props · shelves · wall content |
| `expressionDelta` | What changed vs full regeneration |
| `archiveEligible` | Auto-true for major milestones |

---

## Rebrand Continuity

Rebrand is a **special continuity event**:

1. **Archive™ captures** prior Living Set expression (full Environment DNA™)
2. **Transformation Engine™** regenerates from updated Company Genome™
3. **Continuity artifacts** selectively migrate (awards · Golden Builds™ · milestones)
4. **Orb explains** — *"Your headquarters reflects the rebrand. Your history is preserved in The Archive™."*

Rebrand ≠ amnesia.
