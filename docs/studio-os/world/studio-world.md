## Mission

**Studio World™** becomes the umbrella system that unifies every immersive experience in Studio OS.

Studio OS is no longer a collection of pages.  
Studio OS is **one persistent world**.

Everything else belongs inside Studio World™.

---

## One Sentence

**Studio World™** is the persistent, living headquarters universe where founders inhabit their companies — and every department, Set™, transition, AI, project, and memory exists somewhere inside it.

## What Studio World™ Is

| Studio World™ **is** | Studio World™ **is not** |

## Final Philosophy

Studio OS is not software.

Every Headquarters tells the story of a business.

Every Legacy™ preserves what mattered.

**Studio World™ unifies them all into one persistent, immersive universe.**
