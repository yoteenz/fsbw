# Adaptive Onboarding™

**The First Conversation**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical onboarding evolution  
**Phase:** Philosophy & onboarding (post-platform freeze)  
**Scope:** Every founder's first experience in Studio OS

---

> **Studio OS should never ask: "What software do you want to use?"**  
> **Studio OS should ask: "What are we going to build together?"**

---

## Mission

Founders should **not** begin Studio OS by filling out forms.

Founders should begin by **having a conversation**.

The **Orb™** acts like an experienced business advisor — not a setup wizard. Its job is to understand what the founder is trying to build **before** configuring Studio OS.

The onboarding experience should feel like meeting the **Executive Producer** of your future company — not completing a setup wizard.

---

## The Shift

| Before (Mode System™ V1) | After (Adaptive Onboarding™) |
|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| "How would you like to build today?" | "What are we going to build together?" |
| Founder chooses Mode immediately | Orb listens · then recommends Mode |
| Mode grid / tap selection | Natural conversation |
| Forms follow mode | Conversation seeds Genome |
| Founder feels configured | Founder feels understood |

**Adaptive Onboarding™ evolves** [Mode System™](../modes/README.md) — it does not replace the Company Engine™ or create a new platform.

**Precedes (when needed):** [Founder Discovery™](../founder-discovery/README.md) — helps founders discover *what* to build before *"What are we going to build together?"*

---

## The Experience

```
Headquarters emerges (cinematic · warm)
        ↓
Orb opens The First Conversation™
        ↓
Orb learns: who · what · why · how · team · vision
        ↓
Mode Recommendation™ (with reasoning)
        ↓
Founder: Accept · Choose Another · Tell Me Why · Compare
        ↓
Headquarters Preview™ (future Sets™ revealed)
        ↓
Company Genome™ initialization (from conversation)
        ↓
Business Discovery Blueprint™ (when ready)
        ↓
Organization Inauguration™
        ↓
ENTER HEADQUARTERS
```

## Forbidden First Questions

| Do NOT ask first | Ask instead |
|------------------|-------------|
| "What template do you want?" | "What are you building?" |
| "What industry are you in?" | "What inspired you to start this?" |
| Business name | (learn naturally in conversation) |
| Logo | (deferred — never blocking) |
| Colors | (deferred — Genome phase) |
| Domain | (deferred — Blueprint phase) |
| Website | (deferred — existing company path) |

**Curiosity first. Configuration later.**

## Success Criteria

The founder should feel:

> **"I just hired an executive team."**

Not:

> "I completed onboarding."

The Orb should understand the founder **before** making recommendations.

The founder should feel **understood** — not configured.

## Final Philosophy

The founder doesn't configure Studio OS.

Studio OS **learns** the founder, **recommends** the right path, and **begins building** the company alongside them.

Everything after *"What are we going to build together?"* should naturally unfold into a personalized **Headquarters**, **Company Genome™**, **Studio World™**, and **Company Engine™** experience.
