# Adaptive Onboarding™ — Master Specification

**"The First Conversation"**

---

## Core Law

**Understand before you configure.**

Studio OS must learn who the founder is and what they are building **before** asking them to choose software behaviors, templates, or operational modes.

---

## The Defining Moment

The defining moment is no longer:

> "How would you like to build today?"

It is:

> **"What are we going to build together?"**

That question opens a conversation — not a configuration flow.

---

## Philosophy Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Curiosity first** | Start with who · what · why — not what software |
| **Conversation not questionnaire** | Natural dialogue · follow-ups · no numbered forms |
| **Listen before recommending** | Mode is offered — never forced |
| **Explain every recommendation** | Studio OS always shows its thinking |
| **Preview before building** | Founder sees future Headquarters |
| **Seed once** | Conversation feeds Genome · Blueprint inherits |
| **Evolve continuously** | Onboarding never truly ends |
| **Company-first language** | Build company · not configure platform |

---

## The Orb's Role

The Orb is an **experienced business advisor** — the Executive Producer of the founder's future company.

| Orb **is** | Orb **is not** |
|------------|----------------|
| Curious · warm · executive | A chatbot collecting fields |
| Listening before advising | Pushing mode cards immediately |
| Explaining recommendations | Black-box AI decisions |
| One voice during onboarding | Five specialists talking at once |
| Patient · never rushed | Countdown timers · progress bars |

**Detail:** [orb-conversation.md](./orb-conversation.md)

## Mode Recommendation™

Instead of forcing immediate Mode selection:

1. Orb listens through conversation
2. Orb synthesizes signals into a recommendation
3. Orb presents recommendation **with reasoning**
4. Founder chooses: Accept · Choose Another · Tell Me Why · Compare Modes™

Nothing is locked. Modes remain reversible via [Mode Evolution™](../modes/mode-evolution.md).

**Detail:** [mode-recommendation-engine.md](./mode-recommendation-engine.md)

## Headquarters Preview™

Once a Mode™ is accepted:

> **"This is the Headquarters we'll build together."**

The founder immediately sees the world they are about to inhabit — Creative Atelier™ · Marketing War Room™ · Product Lab™ · Finance Vault™ · Customer Experience™ · Hall of Legacy™.

Preview is **spatial storytelling** — not a feature checklist.

## Company Genome™ Initialization

**Only after Mode™ is selected** does Studio OS begin constructing the Company Genome™.

The onboarding conversation automatically contributes to:

- Mission · Vision · Values
- Industry · Business Model
- Founder Personality
- Brand Direction · Creative Style
- Leadership Style · Growth Strategy
- Customer Philosophy

Nothing should need to be entered twice.

## Forbidden Onboarding Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why forbidden |
|--------------|---------------|
| Form-first entry | Violates conversation philosophy |
| Template / industry picker first | Configures before understanding |
| Logo · colors · domain upfront | Blocks emotional arrival |
| Mode grid before conversation | Forces choice without context |
| Recommendation without reasoning | Breaks trust · feels like black box |
| "Setup complete" language | Founder hired team — not finished wizard |
| Duplicate Genome + Blueprint questions | Wastes founder attention |
| Specialist pile-on | Orb is sole facilitator |
| Black screen between phases | Violates [World Streaming™](../world/transitions/world-streaming.md) |

---

## Language Law

| Forbidden | Required |
|-----------|----------|
| "Complete your profile" | "Tell me what you're building" |
| "Select a template" | "I'll recommend how we should build" |
| "Choose your plan" | "Choose your Mode™ — or let me suggest one" |
| "Setup wizard" | "The First Conversation" |
| "Onboarding form" | "Adaptive Onboarding™" |
| "Configure Studio OS" | "Build your company together" |
| "Finish" / "Done" / "Continue" | "Enter Headquarters" · "Let's begin" |

## Relationship to Mode System™ V1

[Mode System™](../modes/mode-system.md) established:

- Five Initial Modes™
- Mode DNA™ configuration
- One platform · many journeys

| Mode System™ V1 | Adaptive Onboarding™ |
