# Mode Recommendation Engine™

**Listen first. Recommend with reasoning. Never lock.**

---

## Purpose

Define how Studio OS recommends a **Mode™** after The First Conversation™ — replacing immediate Mode selection with an informed, explainable recommendation.

The founder may:

- ✓ **Accept Recommendation**
- **Choose Another Mode™**
- **Tell Me Why**
- **Compare Modes™**

Nothing is locked.

---

## Core Law

**Recommendations require reasoning.**

Every Mode Recommendation™ must include:

1. **Which Mode™** is recommended
2. **Why** — bullet reasoning tied to founder's words
3. **What it prioritizes** — departments · workflows · journey shape
4. **What remains flexible** — Mode Evolution™ · change anytime

---

## Recommendation Flow

```
Conversation signals collected
        ↓
Signal scoring across Mode dimensions
        ↓
Primary Mode candidate selected
        ↓
Secondary Mode candidates ranked
        ↓
Reasoning narrative generated
        ↓
Orb presents recommendation
        ↓
Founder decision branch
```

---

## Example Presentation

> **"Based on what you've shared, I recommend Entrepreneur Mode™."**
>
> **"You're building a founder-led company from the ground up."**
>
> **"I'll prioritize Brand Development, Product Strategy, Marketing, Launch, and Growth."**

### Reasoning Block (always visible on "Tell Me Why")

> **"I recommended Entrepreneur Mode™ because:"**
>
> - You're building your first company.
> - You currently work independently.
> - Your focus is launching products.
> - Your immediate priorities are branding, content, marketing, and operations.

## Signal → Mode Mapping

| Signal pattern | Primary recommendation | Confidence boosters |
|----------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| First company · solo · launching product | 🚀 **Entrepreneur Mode™** | Brand + launch urgency |
| Established org · team · governance · scale | 🏢 **Enterprise Mode™** | Multi-department · compliance |
| Personal brand · audience · content-first | 🎨 **Creator Mode™** | Creator economy · publishing |
| Multiple clients · deliverables · accounts | 🤝 **Agency Mode™** | Client workflows · billing |
| Multi-location · franchise · replication | 🏪 **Franchise Mode™** | Location ops · standards |

Signals **compose** — a solo creator launching a product skews Creator with Entrepreneur secondary.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Maps to |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Company stage | High | Entrepreneur · Enterprise |
| Team size | High | Entrepreneur · Enterprise |
| Revenue model | Medium | Creator · Agency · Entrepreneur |
| Customer type | Medium | Creator · Agency · Enterprise |
| Geographic scope | Medium | Franchise |
| Governance needs | Medium | Enterprise |
| Content emphasis | Medium | Creator |
| Client count | High | Agency |
| Location count | High | Franchise |
| Founder language | Low | All — tone calibration |

**Tie-breaking:** Orb presents top two modes with Compare Modes™ — never arbitrary silent pick.

## Founder Decision Branches

### Accept Recommendation

```
Orb confirms Mode warmly
        ↓
Mode DNA™ profile queued
        ↓
Headquarters Preview™ begins
        ↓
Company Genome™ initialization
```

Example confirmation:

> **"Excellent. We'll build around founder-led decision making."**  
> **"You can always evolve Modes later — your Headquarters will grow with you."**

### Choose Another Mode™

Orb presents five Initial Modes™ conversationally — not a SaaS pricing grid.

| Mode | Orb one-liner |
|------|---------------|
| 🚀 Entrepreneur | "Building a company from the ground up" |
| 🏢 Enterprise | "Running an established organization" |
| 🎨 Creator | "Growing a personal brand and audience" |
| 🤝 Agency | "Delivering work for multiple clients" |
| 🏪 Franchise | "Operating across multiple locations" |

Orb **does not guilt** the founder for overriding recommendation.

> **"Understood. Agency Mode™ it is."**  
> **"I'll shape your Headquarters for client delivery."**

Conversation signals still apply to Genome — Mode override does not discard learning.

### Tell Me Why

Orb expands reasoning block with:

- Direct quotes from conversation (paraphrased)
- Which signals weighted highest
- What would change recommendation (e.g., "If you hire a team of 10, Enterprise may fit better")
- Link to Compare Modes™

### Compare Modes™

Side-by-side conversational comparison of 2–3 Modes:

| Comparison facet | Content |
|------------------|---------|
| Journey shape | Launch vs scale vs clients vs locations |
| HQ emphasis | Which Sets™ prioritized first |
| Department focus | Top 3 departments per Mode |
| AI tone | Orb register per Mode |
| Best for | Founder profile match |
| Evolution path | Where this Mode tends to grow |

**Not** a feature matrix. **Narrative** comparison.

## Low-Confidence Recommendations

When signals are sparse:

> **"I have an early recommendation — Entrepreneur Mode™ — but I'd like to learn more as we build."**  
> **"We can refine this anytime. Nothing is permanent."**

Orb may recommend default Entrepreneur with explicit low-confidence flag — **never** blocks founder from choosing.

---

## Reassurance (Always)

After any Mode decision:

> **"You can always change Modes later."**  
> **"Your Headquarters will evolve with you."**  
> **"Nothing is permanent."**

Maps to [Mode Evolution™](../modes/mode-evolution.md).

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Recommendation without reasoning | Breaks trust |
| Single Mode shown (no override path) | Feels locked |
| Mode grid before any conversation | Skips Adaptive Onboarding™ |
| "Our AI determined…" | Corporate · not advisor |
| Hiding secondary Mode fit | Founder deserves transparency |
| Deleting conversation on override | Signals still seed Genome |
