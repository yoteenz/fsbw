# Founder Discovery™

**The Company Before The Company**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical pre-company discovery architecture (docs only)  
**Phase:** Founder Discovery sprint (pre-headquarters)  
**Scope:** How founders discover what company to build before Studio World™ construction

---

> **The first product Studio OS creates is not a company. It creates clarity.**

---

## Mission

Many people dream of owning a business.

Most do not know **what business they should build**.

Studio OS should never begin with:

> *"What business do you want to create?"*

Studio OS helps founders **discover the answer**.

---

## One Sentence

**Founder Discovery™** is the pre-company conversation where the Orb™ learns who the founder is — and together they discover the business they were most prepared to build — before Headquarters™ exists.

---

## The Journey

```
Founder arrives (no company yet)
        ↓
Orb conversation (not forms)
        ↓
Founder Genome™ builds continuously
        ↓
Business Matching™ across dimensions
        ↓
Discovery Report™ (multiple paths)
        ↓
Business Simulator™ + Cost Engine™
        ↓
Company Preview™ (future HQ · departments · roadmap)
        ↓
Founder chooses with clarity · confidence · commitment
        ↓
Studio World™ construction begins
```

## Final Philosophy

People don't start companies because they know exactly what to build.

They start because they **discover a vision worth pursuing**.

The Orb listens before it recommends.

The Founder Genome™ evolves before it judges.

The Company Preview™ inspires before it asks for commitment.
