# Business Simulator™

**Experience the Business Before Committing**

---

## Purpose

Define the **Business Simulator™** — answering *"If I choose this company… what will life actually look like?"*

---

## Core Law

Every recommendation must answer the life question — not just the spreadsheet question.

The founder **experiences** the business before committing.

---

## Simulation Dimensions

| Dimension | Simulated experience |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Typical workday** | Morning · afternoon · evening activities |
| **Weekly rhythm** | Meeting load · deep work · admin ratio |
| **Seasonality** | Busy months · slow periods |
| **Growth milestones** | First customer · hire · scale beats |
| **Customer interactions** | Volume · channel · emotional load |
| **Hiring timeline** | When first hire · role · cost |
| **Cash flow expectations** | In/out rhythm · tight months |
| **Stress level** | Honest intensity curve |
| **Travel requirements** | None · occasional · constant |
| **Scalability** | Ceiling · leverage points |

---

## Simulation Modes

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Day-in-the-life** | Narrative walkthrough · Orb-voiced |
| **Week map** | Calendar heatmap of activity types |
| **Year arc** | Milestone timeline with effort curve |
| **Stress honest** | "Hard weeks" flagged explicitly |
| **Compare paths** | Side-by-side lifestyle contrast |

---

## Diegetic Expression (Conceptual)

Simulation uses pre-headquarters **preview theater**:

- Founder "walks" a simulated day in suggested departments
- Orb narrates decisions · customer moments · admin blocks
- Founder reacts: *"That feels energizing"* / *"I'd hate that"*

Reactions update Founder Genome™ lifestyle facets.

---

## Simulator Feedback Loop

```
Founder experiences simulation
        ↓
Explicit + implicit signals
        ↓
Genome update (enjoyment · stress · lifestyle)
        ↓
Path re-rank or confirm
        ↓
Optional: try different archetype simulation
```

---

## Simulation Honesty Rules

| Rule | Law |
|------|-----|
| **No fantasy defaults** | Include admin · sales · support |
| **Seasonality shown** | Not flat "average day" |
| **Failure modes** | Slow months · churn · hiring miss |
| **Founder role clarity** | What YOU do vs team |
| **Comparison encouraged** | vs other paths in report |

---

## Integration with Cost Engine™

Simulator references financial reality:

> *"Tuesday includes two hours on bookkeeping — or $400/month for a concierge accountant."*

Life simulation and cost model **align** — no contradictions.

---

## Post-Commitment Simulation

Simulator remains useful after company creation:

- **Pivot exploration** — simulate alternate models
- **Hire simulation** — life with first employee
- **Scale simulation** — life at 10x revenue

Discovery simulator is **first use** — not only use.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Only rosy scenarios | Breaks trust |
| Generic "entrepreneur day" | Must be archetype-specific |
| Simulation as commitment trap | Always optional · revisable |
| No founder reaction capture | Wasted signal |
