# Founder Evolution™

**Discovery Never Ends™**

---

## Purpose

Define how **Founder Discovery™** continues throughout the founder's journey — not only pre-company.

---

## Core Law

**Discovery Never Ends™.**

Studio OS continuously updates recommendations as the founder · market · and business evolve.

---

## Continuous Discovery Triggers

| Trigger | Recommendation update |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Skills improve** | New paths · higher ambition fit |
| **Interests evolve** | Pivot suggestions · wild cards refresh |
| **Life changes** | Family · location · time · budget |
| **Markets shift** | Viability notes · opportunity alerts |
| **Business succeeds** | Scale · expedition suggestions |
| **Business struggles** | Pivot archetypes · support routines |
| **New opportunities emerge** | Orb proactive surfacing |
| **Founder requests** | "What else could I build?" anytime |

---

## Discovery Modes Over Lifetime

| Founder stage | Discovery emphasis |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Pre-company** | Full Discovery Report™ · paths |
| **Launch year** | Refinement · pivot guardrails |
| **Growth** | Expansion · new expeditions |
| **Portfolio** | Second company discovery |
| **Legacy** | Succession · teaching · archive |

Aligns with [Founder Journey™](../engine/founder-journey/01_JOURNEY_OVERVIEW.md).

---

## Re-Discovery Ceremonies

| Ceremony | When |
|----------|------|
| **Annual rediscovery** | Optional reflection · paths refresh |
| **Life event** | Orb invites conversation |
| **Pivot consideration** | Full report delta |
| **Second company** | New discovery cycle · genome travels |

Prior reports preserved — delta shown.

---

## Genome Evolution

Founder Genome™ never stops updating:

| Source | Update |
|--------|--------|
| **Operating behavior** | Observed leadership · taste |
| **Routine memory** | Habits · bottlenecks |
| **Expedition memory** | Strategic patterns |
| **Explicit founder edits** | "I've changed · here's how" |
| **Simulation reactions** | Lifestyle refinements |

Pre-company genome was **seed** — operating years are **depth**.

---

## Recommendation Refresh Without Alarm

| Rule | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| **No "you chose wrong"** | Evolution not judgment |
| **Suggest don't switch** | Founder agency |
| **Celebrate current path** | Even when surfacing alternatives |
| **Second company optional** | Not implied failure |

Orb: *"Your genome has evolved — want to see what else fits you now?"*

---

## Discovery + Taste + Intelligence

| System | Role in evolution |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Founder Genome™** | Who you are |
| **Founder Taste Genome™** | How you create |
| **Company Genome™** | What business is |
| **Routine Memory™** | How you operate |
| **Expedition Memory™** | How you transform |

Discovery synthesizes all for **next opportunity** matching.

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Discovery ends at signup | Never Ends™ violated |
| Push pivot on struggle | Support first |
| Ignore operating signals | Genome stale |
| Re-discovery as sales funnel | Trust break |
