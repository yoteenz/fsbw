# Founder Discovery™ — Master Specification

**The Company Before The Company**

---

## Purpose

Define how Studio OS helps founders **discover what company to build** — before Headquarters™ · Company Genome™ · or Studio World™ construction exists.

---

## Core Declaration

```
WRONG OPENING     →  "What business do you want to create?"
RIGHT OPENING     →  Orb conversation that discovers the answer
FIRST PRODUCT     →  Clarity (not a company)
```

---

## What Founder Discovery™ Produces

The founder leaves discovery understanding:

| Dimension | Clarity gained |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Who they are** | Identity · strengths · style |
| **Natural aptitude** | Skills · experience · hobbies |
| **Motivation** | Purpose · mission · values |
| **Work preference** | Leadership · creative · technical · time |
| **Industry fit** | Sectors aligned to genome |
| **Company fit** | Specific business types matched |
| **Founder potential** | What kind of founder they could become |

---

## Discovery vs Onboarding vs Building

| Phase | Question | Output |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Founder Discovery™** | *"What company should I build?"* | Discovery Report™ · Company Preview™ |
| **[Adaptive Onboarding™](../onboarding/README.md)** | *"How do we build it together?"* | Mode™ · HQ Preview · Company Genome™ seed |
| **Studio World™** | *"Let's operate and grow it"* | Headquarters™ · departments · routines |

**Law:** Discovery precedes company creation. Never skip clarity for speed.

---

## The Orb's Role

Conversation — not forms.

The Orb gradually learns through natural dialogue:

- Skills · experience · hobbies · interests · education
- Past careers · income · lifestyle goals
- Risk tolerance · leadership style · creative interests
- Technical ability · available time · budget
- Family situation · long-term vision
- Motivations · mission · purpose · values

Founder feels **interviewed** — not **surveyed**.

Discovery continues after company creation:

- Skills improve · interests evolve · life changes

Recommendations update — never frozen at onboarding.

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why rejected |
|--------------|--------------|
| Business idea dropdown | Founder must discover not pick random |
| Single recommendation | No choice · no paths |
| Form-first onboarding | Survey not interview |
| Commitment before preview | No clarity · no confidence |
| One-time genome snapshot | Discovery is continuous |
| Match without WHY | Trust requires explanation |
