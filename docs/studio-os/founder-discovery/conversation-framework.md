# Conversation Framework™

**Interviewed — Not Surveyed**

---

## Purpose

Define how the **Orb™** conducts Founder Discovery™ through conversation — not questionnaires · forms · or wizards.

---

## Core Law

The Orb begins with **conversation**.

Not forms.

---

## Conversation Principles

| Principle | Manifestation |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Natural flow** | One topic leads to next · not numbered questions |
| **Orb listens first** | Reflect before recommend |
| **No required fields** | Skip · return · depth optional |
| **Story invitation** | "Tell me about…" not "Select your…" |
| **Emotional safety** | No judgment on risk · income · gaps |
| **Progress invisible** | Genome builds silently · no progress bar anxiety |
| **Founder pace** | Pause · resume · multi-session |
| **WHY explained** | Orb shares why it asks |

---

## Opening (Canonical)

Studio OS never opens with business type selection.

| Forbidden | Required |
|-----------|----------|
| "What business do you want?" | Orb opens with presence · warmth |
| Industry dropdown | "What's been on your mind lately?" |
| Logo upload first | Listening before configuring |

**Adaptive Onboarding™** opening *"What are we going to build together?"* applies **after** discovery — or when founder already has clarity.

**Pre-company discovery** may open softer:

> *"A lot of people come here with a dream but not yet a name for it. That's exactly where we start."*

---

## Conversation Domains

Orb gradually explores — order adapts to founder responses:

| Domain | Orb learns |
|--------|------------|
| **Identity & story** | Who you are · background · turning points |
| **Skills & experience** | What you're good at · past careers |
| **Interests & hobbies** | What energizes you outside work |
| **Education & learning** | How you grow · what you enjoy studying |
| **Motivation & purpose** | Why business · what matters |
| **Values & mission** | Non-negotiables · ethical boundaries |
| **Lifestyle design** | Hours · location · family · travel |
| **Financial context** | Income goals · budget · runway (sensitive · optional depth) |
| **Risk & leadership** | Tolerance · decision style · team preference |
| **Creative & technical** | Make vs manage · tools · craft interests |
| **Time & capacity** | Side project vs full-time · season of life |
| **Vision** | 1-year · 5-year · legacy imagination |

---

## Interview Techniques

| Technique | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| **Reflective mirror** | "It sounds like you thrive when…" |
| **Contrast question** | "What would you never want in a business?" |
| **Story prompt** | "Describe a moment you felt most alive at work." |
| **Hypothetical** | "If money weren't a factor for two years…" |
| **Ranking without numbers** | "More energy from people or from building systems?" |
| **Gentle probe** | "Want to go deeper on that?" |

---

## What Orb Avoids

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Checkbox grids | Survey feeling |
| Jargon early | Accessibility |
| Premature recommendation | Insufficient genome |
| Judgment language | "That's risky" without context |
| Long monologues | Conversation is two-way |
| Fake urgency | Discovery is not sales |

---

## Multi-Session Discovery

Discovery may span days:

| Session | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| **Return visit** | Orb summarizes · continues |
| **New insight** | Genome updates · matches refresh |
| **Changed mind** | No penalty · paths regenerate |
| **Ready early** | Report when confidence threshold met |

---

## Conversation → Genome Mapping

Each exchange updates Founder Genome™ facets:

```
Orb question / founder answer
        ↓
Signal extraction (skills · risk · lifestyle · …)
        ↓
Genome facet update (confidence delta)
        ↓
Business Matching™ pool re-ranked (when threshold met)
```

No single "submit" — continuous integration.

---

## Handoff to Discovery Report™

When genome confidence sufficient:

| Orb says | Not |
|----------|-----|
| "I have enough to show you some paths worth exploring." | "Onboarding complete." |
| "These aren't final — they're starting points." | "Pick your business." |
