# Decision Ownership Matrix™

**Status:** Canonical decision governance — P0 Executive OS Sprint  
**Scope:** Who owns what decisions in Studio World™

---

## Decision Thesis

> **The founder leads. The organization executes.**

The organization never asks *"What would you like to generate?"* It asks *"What decision should we make?"* This matrix defines **who prepares · who advocates · who decides · who executes** for every decision class.

---

## Decision Authority Hierarchy

```
FOUNDER (sole approval authority)
    │
    ├── Strategic decisions (vision · canon · launch · investment)
    ├── Creative decisions (direction lock · concept selection · rejection)
    ├── Production decisions (authorization · distribution · quality override)
    └── Governance decisions (genome override · visitor access · legacy)
    
STUDIO ORB™ (route · frame · moderate · never approve)

TIER 1 SPECIALISTS (recommend · advocate · prepare · execute within approval)

TIER 2 CONCIERGES (support · annotate · retrieve · alert)

ORGANIZATIONAL INTELLIGENCE (detect · schedule · prepare · request founder decision)
```

**Binding law:** Founder remains Creative Director™ — Studio World Constitution™ Article V.

## Decision Framing Templates

The organization frames decisions — never open-ended generation:

| Decision class | Organization asks | Options presented |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Concept selection | "Which direction should we pursue?" | A · B · C boards mounted |
| Approval gate | "Should production begin?" | Readiness evidence table |
| Rejection recovery | "Would you like another iteration?" | Revision spec · new branch option |
| Arbitration | "How should we resolve this disagreement?" | Specialist positions summarized |
| Launch | "Are we ready to launch?" | Campaign readiness board |
| Defer | "Should we defer this decision?" | Reschedule options · risk of delay |

## Delegation Boundary

Founder Operating System™ may recommend delegation:

> *"You've made many executive decisions today. I'll postpone non-critical approvals until tomorrow."*

| Delegatable | Never delegatable |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Timing of non-critical reviews | Creative direction lock |
| Batch presentation of minor approvals | Launch decision |
| Research prioritization | Canon approval |
| Meeting rescheduling | Genome override |
| Prep depth for routine gates | Strategic vision shift |

Delegation adjusts **when** founder decides — never **what authority** founder has.

## Decision Fatigue Protection

| Signal (Founder OS) | Executive OS response |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| High decision count today | Defer Class C non-urgent gates |
| Low energy | Shorter meetings · fewer options |
| Deep work block | No proactive scheduling |
| High creative cycle | Prioritize Class B creative decisions |

Protection serves founder effectiveness — not avoidance of responsibility.

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Violation |
|--------------|-----------|
| AI auto-approves | Founder sovereignty |
| Open-ended "generate anything" | Decision framing |
| Founder executes production steps | Role inversion |
| No recording of decision | Stateless |
| Specialist blocks without founder | Non-blocking rule |
| Decision without preparation | Uninformed executive time |
| Unlimited options without framing | Decision fatigue |
