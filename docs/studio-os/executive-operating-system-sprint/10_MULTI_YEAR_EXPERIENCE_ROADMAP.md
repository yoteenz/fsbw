# Multi-Year Experience Roadmap™

**Status:** Long-term experience roadmap — P0 Executive OS Sprint  
**Scope:** How Executive Operating System™ matures over years

---

## Roadmap Philosophy

The Executive Operating System™ matures the way a real company matures — from **founding rhythm** to **operating cadence** to **institutional wisdom** to **legacy stewardship**.

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Rhythm before features** | Morning Briefing before advanced analytics |
| **Memory before intelligence** | Chronicle before predictive scheduling |
| **Meetings before automation** | Real reviews before auto-resolution |
| **One company feel before multi-company** | Single HQ rhythm perfected first |
| **Earned surprise** | Proactive scheduling only after trust established |

**This sprint completes:** Experience architecture (Phase 0).  
**Implementation aligns with:** [Foundation Sprint Era 1–7](../foundation-sprint/12_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md).

## Experience Maturity Model

| Maturity level | Founder feeling | Organizational behavior |
|----------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **L0 — Software** | "I prompt AI" | Stateless · empty · generates on demand |
| **L1 — Presence** | "Someone was here" | Pre-arrival life · occupied rooms |
| **L2 — Rhythm** | "I have a morning briefing" | Calendar · meetings · overnight progress |
| **L3 — Operating** | "I run this company" | Debate · handoffs · decision gates |
| **L4 — Wise** | "They know how I decide" | Chronicle · taste · historical reference |
| **L5 — Proactive** | "They know when I need to lead" | Intelligent scheduling · earned surprise |
| **L6 — Institutional** | "This company has a history" | Legacy · retreat · succession |
| **L7 — Legacy** | "I built something that endures" | Autobiography · preservation · generational |

**Year 1 target:** L2–L3  
**Year 3 target:** L4–L5  
**Year 5+ target:** L6–L7
