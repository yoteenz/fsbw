# Headquarters Engine™

**Studio OS™ — Foundational Platform Subsystem**

**Type:** Platform philosophy + architecture (not a Design Revision · not a new milestone · not implementation)

**Status:** Canonical direction — supersedes app/template/feature-pack mental models for all future Studio OS work

**Applies to:** Master Specification · Platform Vision · Product Philosophy · Experience DNA™ · Creative Direction Studio™ · Studio Production Engine™ · Expansion Center · Marketplace · Monetization · all future products

---

## Executive mandate

Studio OS must **no longer** think in terms of:

- Apps
- Templates
- Feature packs
- Page builders
- Isolated software modules

Studio OS must think in terms of **living businesses**.

Every customer owns a **Headquarters**. Their Headquarters **grows over time**. Instead of downloading templates, users **expand their Headquarters**.

**Studio OS becomes an operating system for building businesses — not software.**

---

## Long-term vision

The Studio OS vision is **not** to become the best business software.

The vision is to become the world's first **immersive Business Headquarters Operating System**.

Every entrepreneur should feel like they are **stepping inside their business** each time they log in — not opening software. **Living inside it.**

## Canonical hierarchy

Every Headquarters is organized as a **living spatial stack**:

```
Headquarters
    ↓
Buildings
    ↓
Departments
    ↓
Workspaces
    ↓
Projects
    ↓
Assets
    ↓
Tasks
```

| Layer | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Headquarters** | The customer's entire business operating environment — Mission Control home |
| **Building** | Major wing or destination on the lot (Operations · Creative · Knowledge · Financial · …) |
| **Department** | Named production or business room with concierge, tools, and exit criteria |
| **Workspace** | Focused room inside a department (editor · studio · gallery · queue) |
| **Project** | Complete creative or business initiative — single source of truth ([Project Model](./project-model.md)) |
| **Asset** | Production artifact belonging to a Project (master content, media, documents) |
| **Task** | Atomic execution unit — never the primary object users think about |

**Users direct Projects inside Departments inside Buildings inside their Headquarters.**

## Headquarters Expansions™

Businesses should **never outgrow** Studio OS.

Instead of purchasing isolated templates, they purchase **Headquarters Expansions™**.

Each Expansion **installs entirely new capabilities** into an **existing** Headquarters — not a separate app.

### What every Expansion includes

- New departments
- New workspaces
- New workflows
- New AI specialists
- New concierge teams
- New automations
- New analytics
- New documentation
- New achievements
- New progression systems

**These are not templates. They are business capabilities.**

### Example expansions

#### Photography Expansion™

Adds: Photography Studio · Equipment Room · Shot Planning · AI Photo Director · Editing Suite · Client Gallery · Delivery Workflow

#### Education Expansion™

Adds: Academy · Recording Studio · Curriculum Builder · Student Lounge · Certification Center · AI Instructor · Learning Analytics

#### Retail Expansion™

Adds: Showroom · Inventory Warehouse · Fulfillment Center · Returns Office · Product Photography · Customer Support

#### Podcast Expansion™

Adds: Recording Booth · Guest Lounge · Episode Planner · Audio Engineer AI · Distribution Center · Sponsorship Manager

#### Membership Expansion™

Adds: VIP Lounge · Rewards Center · Loyalty Program · Community Hub · Exclusive Content Library

#### Franchise Expansion™

Adds: Regional Office · Multi-location Dashboard · Operations Center · Training Academy · Performance Tracking

Marketplace creators sell **intellectual property** — operational systems they spent months designing.

Everything travels with the install:

- UI surfaces
- AI behavior models
- Workflows

- Knowledge structure

Marketplace assets become **living operational systems** — not downloadable files.

## Headquarters evolution

Every Headquarters **evolves** throughout the life of the business.

As new Expansions install:

- New **buildings** appear on the lot
- New **departments** unlock
- New **AI specialists** join the team
- New **concierge roles** become available
- New **achievements** unlock
- New **workflows** become possible
- New **automations** activate

The Headquarters should **visually and functionally grow** alongside the business.

This connects to **Campus Evolution Engine (M59)** and **Living Headquarters™** — evolution is earned through meaningful progress, not decorative badges.

## Game design philosophy

Studio OS borrows the **feeling of progression** from great games — **not their appearance**.

| Inspiration | Principle |
|-------------|-----------|
| **The Sims** | Ownership · world-building · spaces that feel alive |
| **The Movies** | Production management · departments · meaningful work |
| **Watch Dogs** | Ambient intelligence · overlays in the environment |
| **Xbox** | Progression psychology · milestones · mastery — executive tone |

**This is NOT gamification.**

No points for clicking. No fake streaks. No juvenile badges.

The goal is **meaningful progression** — users feel they are **building something alive**.
