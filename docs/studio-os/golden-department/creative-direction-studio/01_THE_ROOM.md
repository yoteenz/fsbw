# 01 — The Room

**Golden Department:** Creative Direction Studio™  
**Section:** Environment Blueprint

---

## Design Intent

Creative Direction Studio™ is a **double-height editorial atelier** — part luxury architecture studio, part Hollywood creative headquarters, part Apple Industrial Design Lab. The room communicates **creativity, luxury, intelligence, and possibility** before a single word is read.

The founder should feel they have walked into the most expensive creative space they have ever occupied — and it belongs to them.

---

## Overall Architecture

### Spatial Envelope

| Property | Specification |
|----------|---------------|
| Template | Stage (SDK 02) |
| Proportion | 3:2 width:depth — generous horizontal sweep |
| Ceiling height | Double-volume at hero zone (6.5m equivalent); standard 3.2m at work zones |
| Total footprint | ~120m² equivalent in normalized coordinates |
| Exterior | Floor-to-ceiling glass on right flank — city, landscape, or abstract horizon per Genome |
| Structure | Minimal columns — two slender supports at 35% depth, stone or steel per Genome |

### Architectural Character (Genome-Adaptive)

| Register | Expression |
|----------|------------|
| **Luxury editorial** (default) | Polished stone floor, brass accents, gallery-white hero wall, walnut work surfaces |
| **Tech minimal** (NDX) | Concrete gallery, blackened steel, frosted glass, precise shadow lines |
| **Beauty mansion** (Frontal Slayer) | Warm marble, rose-gold fixtures, velvet seating alcove, salon lighting |
| **Culinary studio** (Restaurant) | Warm wood, copper, open shelving metaphor, terracotta accents |
| **Legal strategy** (Law Firm) | Dark walnut paneling, leather-bound wall texture, brass library ladders |

Architecture **topology is fixed**. Materials and light are Genome-injected.

## Lighting Philosophy

### Three-Point Editorial Rig

| Anchor | Position | Character |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Hero Key** | Above Mood Wall, 15° off-center | Soft wash — imagery is the star |
| **Work Key** | Above Timeline Table | Crisp but warm — clarity for review |
| **Ambient Fill** | Ceiling sky panel + window fill | Room never feels dark |

### Accent Lighting

| Feature | Light |
|---------|-------|
| Orb Pedestal | Uplight + Genome-colored glow ring |
| Creative Brief Wall | Pin spots on active brief sections |
| Genome Observatory | Subtle internal glow — "living data" |
| Sandbox zone | Dimmable — brightens on branch activation |
| Approval moment | Ceremony accent from ceiling grid |

### Genome Lighting

| Company | Character |
|---------|-----------|
| Luxury beauty | Warm amber key, soft rose fill |
| Editorial finance | Cool neutral key, precise shadows |
| Law firm | Warm desk-lamp pools, mahogany warmth |
| Restaurant | Golden hour through windows |

**Rule:** No flat overhead fluorescent. Ever.

## Ceiling

Double-volume section above Mood Wall:

- **Primary:** Diffused luminous coffer — soft daylight simulation
- **Secondary:** Recessed track for accent spots (Brief Wall pins, ceremony)
- **Tertiary:** Optional kinetic element — slow-moving light blade across ceiling (Experience DNA: declare atmosphere)

Ceiling height **communicates importance** — hero content lives under sky.

---

## Windows & Exterior Views

**Right flank:** Floor-to-ceiling glass wall (8m equivalent span).

| Genome Context | Exterior View |
|----------------|---------------|
| Urban luxury | Soft-focus city skyline at dusk |
| Editorial | Abstract horizon — no literal city |
| Beauty mansion | Garden terrace with soft greenery |
| Finance | Minimal sky — cloud movement only |
| Law | Library courtyard or abstract stone |

Exterior is **atmospheric plate** — parallax at 0.3× foreground speed. Not a video loop — slow Genome-driven still with breathe.

## Hero Focal Point

**Living Mood Wall™** dominates visual hierarchy:

- Occupies 55% of vertical field of view from entry camera
- Slow parallax on pinned references
- Color breathe synced to Project mood
- New pins animate with stick-bounce ceremony
- Rejected references fade to archive shelf below

The Mood Wall is the **emotional center** — not a metrics dashboard.

---

## Orb Location

**Orb Command Center™:**

- Elevated pedestal at Y = 0.55, right of Timeline Table
- Visible from entry within 2 seconds
- Peripheral during table work; focal during conversation
- Pedestal: stone or metal cylinder with Genome glow ring
- Never floating in corner as chat widget

| Layer | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| Particles | Gold dust motes in hero light (luxury) · minimal dust (editorial) |
| Reflections | Floor reflects Mood Wall glow |
| Depth | Three planes: foreground table · midground shelves · background hero |

## Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

| Forbidden | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Card grid layout | This is a room |
| Left sidebar navigation | Travel replaces tabs |
| Top header bar with logo | Genome expresses on Mood Wall |
| Data tables as furniture | Timeline Table shows temporal data spatially |
| Floating chat bubble Orb | Orb has physical pedestal |
| White void background | Floor, walls, ceiling required |
| Stock photo hero banner | Mood Wall uses live references only |
