## Design Intent

Emotion is **designed intentionally** — not accidental byproduct of UI choices.

---

## Emotional Arc

```
Wonder → Confidence → Curiosity → Momentum → Scrutiny → Pride → Ambition → Calm
  ↑                                                                        ↓
  └──────────────────────── Mastery loop ──────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Phase Map

### Arrival

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Wonder · possibility** | Anxiety · overwhelm | Marble ceremony · slow reveal · one question |
| **Trust** | Skepticism · "another SaaS" | Director warmth · no feature dump |
| **Ownership** | Visitor feeling | "Your headquarters" language · named campus |

**Copy tone:** "Let's build" — not "Configure your account."

---

### Creation

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Creative confidence** | Imposter syndrome | Inspiration first · Director encourages |
| **Agency** | AI replacement fear | Propose → approve · undo always |
| **Play** | Rigid form fatigue | Chips · voice · mood boards |

**Copy tone:** "What do you see?" — not "Select from dropdown."

---

### Exploration

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Curiosity** | Lost in space | Campus map · concierge at doors |
| **Ownership** | Renting software | Named wings · persistent world |
| **Calm** | FOMO from locked content | Ghost preview · no pressure |

---

### Production

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Focused momentum** | Scattered multitasking | One department · one objective |
| **Progress** | Stuck feeling | Exit criteria visible · timeline glow |
| **Collaboration** | Tool fatigue | Director as colleague |

---

### Review

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Calm scrutiny** | Alarm · shame | Theater metaphor · warm blockers |
| **Confidence** | Publish fear | Design Health™ explain · fix paths |

---

### Publishing

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Quiet pride** | Cheap celebration | Bloom · URL · no confetti |
| **Validation** | Imposter returns | Legacy plaque · Director acknowledgment |

**Copy tone:** "Your experience is live." — period. Not exclamation marks.

---

### Success

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Momentum** | Complacency | Intelligence feed · next suggestion |
| **Reflection** | Hollow win | Legacy Wing entry · milestone record |

---

### Growth

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Ambition** | Pressure to spend | Marketplace preview · fit not upsell |
| **Clarity** | Expansion confusion | Director explains what changes |

---

### Mastery

| Target | Anti-goal | Design levers |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Executive calm** | Power-user chaos | Orb fluency · keyboard paths |
| **Respect** | Platform infantilizing | No gamification · mature tone |

---

## Emotional Anti-Patterns (Never)

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Confetti · badges · streaks | Cheapens luxury |
| Red alarm errors | Breaks calm confidence |
| "Setup complete!" theater | Breaks arrival philosophy |
| Robotic AI tone | Breaks Creative Director model |
| Dense data walls on emotional peaks | Breaks immersion |
| Forced celebration | Breaks quiet pride |

---

## Emotional Measurement (Future)

| Signal | Proxy metric |
|--------|--------------|
| Wonder | Time on threshold · skip rate |
| Confidence | Interview completion · first edit < 10 min |
| Pride | Publish completion · return within 7d |
| Mastery | Orb command diversity · keyboard usage |

**Not:** NPS popup on publish screen.

---

## Sensitive Contexts (Emotional Computing™)

| Context | Adaptation |
|---------|------------|
| Grief · loss | Reduce ceremony · mute suggestions |
| Celebration | Optional bloom · user preference |
| High stress | Fewer proactive Orb suggestions |
| First-time founder | Extra concierge presence |
