## Act I — Arrival (Minutes 0–5)

Amara completes authentication. The screen does not say "Welcome back." The SaaS frame dissolves into **white marble** — soft veining, gentle light from the upper left.

The **Studio Orb** materializes at the bottom center. A slow breathe. One pulse of warm light.

> **Director (voice · text):** "Welcome, Amara. I'm Studio Intelligence™. Before we open a single tool — let's build the headquarters your business deserves."

The Orb expands a radial menu — not overwhelming. Three items: **Begin** · **Show me** · **I have questions**.

Amara taps **Begin**.

A glass panel floats center-screen. Not a form. A question:

> "What kind of world are you building?"

She speaks: "A luxury hair salon. Editorial. Calm. Expensive in the right way."

The Orb thinks — amber glow. Blueprint lines trace across the marble plaza. A wing rises — glass catching light. A name appears engraved: **Innovation Wing**.

A second structure assembles: **Creative Direction Studio**.

> **Director:** "This is not a dashboard. This is where Salon Lumière lives. Step inside when you're ready."

Amara steps in.

---

## Act II — Creation (Minutes 5–20)

The **Creative Concierge** greets her at the studio entrance.

> "First production? Let's make it count. Drop anything that inspires you — or tell me what you see."

Amara pastes an Instagram Reel. The inspiration wall absorbs it — palette extracts · rhythm notes appear as chips.

> **Director:** "I feel editorial restraint and warm luxury. Should we whisper it or declare it?"

"Declare it — but with calm. Not loud."

Five interview steps — chips · conversation · DNA preview. No configuration wall.

**Project 014 — Salon Lumière Website** is born. On the campus map, a small light activates in Creative Wing.

The canvas materializes. Hero copy appears — she edits inline. The Orb dims while she types. This is *her* creation.

She opens Design DNA™. Luxury at 70%. Margins widen. The Director explains why.

> "Done. Undo available if you want the tighter rhythm back."

---

## Act III — Production (Hours 1–4)

Amara is not scrolling a page. She is **traveling**.

**Development Department** — writers' room atmosphere. Mood board pinned. Script panel. Production Package assembles.

Concierge at the door: "Brief approved. Ready for Assembly?"

Ceremonial transition — corridor · door · arrival.

**Assembly Department** — staging floor. Assets placed. Preview builds.

**Production Department** — the main stage. Experience Studio canvas dominates. She refines sections. Remix™ previews. Accepts.

The **Master Content Asset** follows her — one object · no duplicated context.

---

## Act IV — Review & Publish (Hour 4–5)

**Review Theater** — seats face a large preview. Design Health™: 87 PASS.

> **Director:** "I've verified accessibility — one contrast adjustment applied. Ready when you are."

Amara walks to **Publishing Control Room**. Monitors glow. Three steps: Validate · Deploy · Live.

She confirms.

The screen blooms — soft light. No confetti.

> "Your experience is live. salon-lumiere.studio.os"

Quiet pride. She exhales.

---

## Act V — Success & Growth (Day 7+)

A marble plaque appears in the **Legacy Wing** — first publish. No badge popup. Just a record.

Mission Control campus map shows Creative Wing fully lit. A ghost building appears across the plaza — **Production Wing Expansion**.

The Orb pulses once — opportunity.

> "When you're ready — the Production Wing expansion adds Review Theater and Publishing Control Room as permanent destinations. Want a preview?"

Amara says: "Show me."

She walks into the ghost building. Thirty seconds of inhabitation. She decides.

This is not software she opened.

This is the business she always dreamed of running.
