## Executive Summary

Studio OS identified a **fundamental flaw**: Experience Studio could produce premium layouts that felt **generic** — disconnected from the company creating them.

**Company Genome™** is introduced as a permanent foundational subsystem — the apex living identity object. **Project Genome™** extends it per production. **Genome-First Orchestration™** mandates consultation order for all AI systems.

**No implementation.** No UI. No React. Architecture and documentation only.
