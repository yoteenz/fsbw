# Routine Transitions™

**Physical Movement Between Routine Steps**

---

## Purpose

Define how founders **move** between routine steps across departments — reinforcing that Studio World™ is one connected headquarters.

---

## Core Law

Moving between routine steps should feel **physical** — not like changing applications.

Every transition reinforces: *you are still in the same headquarters.*

---

## Transition Between Steps

When Routine Engine™ advances a step across departments:

```
Current step complete
        ↓
Orb: "Creative direction is approved. Let's head to Distribution."
        ↓
Transition™ begins (not page load)
        ↓
Physical movement (walk · elevator · bridge · …)
        ↓
Next department Arrival Scene™ (abbreviated)
        ↓
Next routine step activates
```

---

## Allowed Transition Types

| Transition™ | When used | Feeling |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| **Walk** | Adjacent departments · same floor | Natural · continuous |
| **Executive Corridor™** | Wing to wing | Professional · purposeful |
| **Elevator™** | Floor change | Vertical journey |
| **Glass bridge / Skywalk™** | Cross-campus | Open · expansive |
| **Gallery Walk™** | Past showcase · marketplace | Inspiring · curated |
| **Innovation Tunnel™** | To research · creative depth | Focused · immersive |
| **Private shuttle** | Executive · time-sensitive | Efficient · premium |
| **Floating platform** | Ceremonial · milestone | Celebratory |
| **Train** | Long campus · multiple stops | Rhythmic · productive |
| **Portal™** | Return visit · deep link | Abbreviated re-entry |

**Source:** [Transitions™](../world/transitions/transition-types.md)

---

## Transition and Routine Context

During transit, Orb maintains routine context:

| Orb says | Not |
|----------|-----|
| "We're crossing to Distribution — your launch campaign is next." | "Loading Distribution module…" |
| "Two steps remain after this." | "Step 3 of 5" in a progress bar |
| "Your Golden Build™ travels with us." | Silent asset handoff |

---

## Same-Department Step Transitions

When consecutive steps are in one department:

| Movement | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Camera dolly** | Story Table → Mood Wall |
| **Pan** | Pipeline board → Review Area |
| **Walk** | Scene to scene |

No full Transition™ required — but movement still physical, not scroll.

---

## World Streaming™

Next department **prepares during transit**:

- Scenes activate · displays update
- Relevant assets pre-loaded
- AI concierges briefed on routine context
- No freeze · no black screen · no spinner

**Source:** [World Streaming™](../world/transitions/world-streaming.md)

## Transition DNA™ and Routines

[Transition DNA™](../world/transitions/transition-dna.md) adapts to routine context:

| Routine type | Transition tone |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Launch** | Energetic · urgent |
| **Hire** | Warm · human |
| **Executive review** | Calm · authoritative |
| **Brand creation** | Editorial · ceremonial |
| **Crisis response** | Focused · supportive |

---

## Routine Start™ and End Transitions

| Moment | Transition |
|--------|------------|
| **Routine Start™** | Headquarters → first department (full arrival) |
| **Mid-routine** | Step-to-step (contextual) |
| **Routine Complete™** | Return to headquarters lobby · celebration beat |
| **Routine Paused** | Soft hold — current scene remains · no eject |

---

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Instant teleport between departments | Breaks continuity |
| Browser navigation as transition | Software not world |
| Loader between routine steps | Not Transition™ |
| Lose routine context during transit | Orb must narrate |
| Same transition for every routine | Transition DNA™ required |
