# Routines™

**The Guided Workflows of Studio World™**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical OS workflow architecture (docs only)  
**Phase:** Workflow sprint (post-Scene Architecture™)  
**Scope:** How founders experience work inside Studio World™

---

> **Work becomes a journey. Not navigation.**

---

## Mission

Studio OS should never feel like software with disconnected features.

Studio OS should understand what the founder is trying to accomplish — and intelligently guide them through the correct **departments · scenes · and workspaces**.

---

## One Sentence

A **Routine™** is a guided journey through Studio World™ that completes a **business objective** — orchestrated by the Orb™ across destinations, not navigated feature-by-feature.

---

## The Three Layers of Work

| Layer | What it is | Founder thinks |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| **[Scene Architecture™](../navigation/README.md)** | Where work happens | *"Which room do I need?"* |
| **[Routines™](./README.md)** | Repeatable operational journeys | *"What am I trying to accomplish today?"* |
| **[Expeditions™](../expeditions/README.md)** | Transformational strategic arcs | *"Where are we taking this company?"* |
| **Tasks™** | Atomic actions in place | *"Approve this stage"* |

**Law:** Routines™ and Expeditions™ orchestrate Scene Architecture™. They do not replace it.

---

