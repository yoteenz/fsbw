# Founder Workflow™

**Status:** Canonical workflow architecture — P0 Foundation Sprint  
**Scope:** How founders move through Studio World™ daily

---

## Workflow Thesis

The founder does not **use** Studio World™. The founder **runs** their creative organization.

Daily workflow is a **journey through headquarters** — not a sequence of app interactions.

---

## Founder Identity

| Attribute | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Title** | Creative Director of their company |
| **Authority** | Sole approval · direction lock · production unlock |
| **Movement** | Walk · orbit · approach · elevate |
| **Communication** | Speech · pointer · gesture · natural language to Orb |
| **Memory** | Taste Genome™ learns from every decision |
| **Presence** | Interrupts ongoing work — does not initiate empty rooms |

---

## Daily Workflow Archetypes

### Archetype 1 — Morning Arrival

```
Founder arrives at headquarters
        ↓
Grand Atrium™ / Mission Control™ (optional overview)
        ↓
Orb Daily Brief™ (holographic projection — what happened overnight)
        ↓
Choose destination:
    → Creative Direction Studio™ (if directing)
    → Studio Warehouse™ (if reviewing production)
    → Review Theater (if meeting scheduled)
        ↓
Short Arrival Sequence™ at destination
        ↓
Resume where left off (World Persistence™)
        ↓
BEGIN WORK
```

**Feeling:** Returning to a studio that kept running overnight.

### Archetype 2 — Creative Direction Session

```
Walk to Creative Direction Studio™
        ↓
Arrival Zone™ (compressed first impression — discover by walk)
        ↓
Approach Story Table™ (read pre-placed evidence)
        ↓
Orb briefing on project state
        ↓
Choose activity:
    PIN REFERENCES → Mood Wall
    EXPLORE CONCEPTS → Sandbox alcove (A/B/C)
    ATTEND REVIEW → Review Theater (specialists assemble)
    WRITE NOTES → Founder desk
    BRANCH → Parallel board spawn
        ↓
Make decision:
    APPROVE → Vault ceremony · canon update
    REJECT → Red-tag shelf
    REVISE → Point at object · specialist revises
    BRANCH → Sandbox fork
        ↓
Walk the Room™ (optional structured critique)
        ↓
Direction lock (if ready) → production signal to Warehouse
        ↓
DEPART via corridor (not browser back)
```

**Feeling:** Directing a creative review at a world-class studio.

### Archetype 3 — Review Meeting (Replaces Prompting)

```
Orb announces: "Brand Strategy Review ready"
        ↓
Walk to Brand Strategy Boardroom
        ↓
Specialists already seated · boards presented
        ↓
Brand Strategist presents positioning evidence
        ↓
Marketing Director adds channel perspective
        ↓
Debate occurs (sticky notes · vocal · board markup)
        ↓
Founder directs:
    "Go with positioning B"
    "Combine A's tone with B's audience"
    "Reject all — try more luxury"
        ↓
Outcomes physicalize on boards
        ↓
Meeting remnants persist on table
        ↓
RETURN to Creative Atelier or CONTINUE to next meeting
```

**Feeling:** Running a creative organization meeting — not typing a prompt.

### Archetype 4 — Production Check-In

```
Walk to Studio Warehouse™ (or receive Orb transit suggestion)
        ↓
Production floor — evidence of completed work on tables
        ↓
Production Manager presents status at central desk
        ↓
Review deliverables at Quality Theater
        ↓
Approve → Distribution Dock handoff
Reject → Return to Creative Direction with transit artifact
        ↓
RETURN or CONTINUE to Archives
```

**Feeling:** Walking a production floor — not checking a task list.

### Archetype 5 — Spatial World Review (Future)

```
Enter compiled brand universe (packaging gallery · retail environment)
        ↓
Walk through at human scale
        ↓
Point at objects:
    "Replace this fixture"
    "Warmer lighting here"
    "Approve this shelf"
        ↓
Specialists revise in context
        ↓
Re-walk to validate
        ↓
World compiles to next version
```

**Feeling:** Walking through a designed world — not reviewing a PDF.

### Archetype 6 — End of Day Departure

```
Founder prepares to leave (no explicit "logout")
        ↓
Work remains on tables (World Persistence™)
        ↓
Lighting shifts toward evening (World State™)
        ↓
Distant wings dim · active project Set™ remains alive
        ↓
Orb: "I'll keep research running on the lighting tests"
        ↓
FOUNDER DEPARTS — world continues
```

**Feeling:** Leaving a studio where the team keeps working.

## Founder ↔ Orb Interaction Model

Orb is the founder's **executive liaison** — not a chatbot.

| Founder says (natural language) | Orb does (spatial) |
|--------------------------------|-------------------|
| "What's happening today?" | Daily Brief projection |
| "Take me to creative" | Transition to CDS |
| "Show me three concepts" | Assembles Review Theater |
| "I liked the lighting in that reel" | Pins to Mood Wall · tags lighting |
| "This doesn't feel like us" | Brand Concierge divergence alert |
| "Start a new direction" | Sandbox branch spawn |
| "What did we decide last week?" | Walks founder to Chronicle wall |
| "Send this to production" | Transit artifact to Warehouse |

Orb **routes and explains**. Orb never approves.

## Founder Anti-Patterns (What We Prevent)

| Anti-pattern | Prevention |
|--------------|------------|
| Starting in empty dashboard | Pre-arrival life always active |
| Typing long prompts | Meeting rooms · Orb natural language |
| Losing work between sessions | World Persistence™ |
| Not knowing where to go | Orb guidance · wayfinding · Mission Control™ |
| Approving without seeing evidence | Review rooms require physical presentation |
| Feeling alone | Ambient specialists · Idle Life™ |
| Feeling lost in features | Spatial hierarchy · one room at a time |

---

## Success Criteria

The founder workflow succeeds when:

1. Founder describes their day as *"I walked to the studio"* — not *"I used the app"*
2. Founder attends meetings — not types prompts
3. Founder points at work — not scrolls through grids
4. Founder leaves work on tables — not clicks save
5. Founder returns to accumulated reality — not blank screens
6. Founder senses the organization beyond every room's walls
7. Founder identifies their company by architecture — not logo

---

## Closing

The founder workflow is not a user journey map.

It is a **day in the life of a Creative Director at the world's most advanced creative organization** — and Studio World™ is that organization.
