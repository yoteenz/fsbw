# Studio World™ Foundation Sprint — P0 Architecture

**Sprint:** COMPOSER — Studio World™ Foundation  
**Date:** July 2026  
**Status:** Architecture & experience design only — **no production code**  
**Mission:** Evolve Creative Direction Studio™ into Studio World™ — a living creative organization, not software.

---

## Objective

The objective is **not realism**. The objective is **presence**.

A founder should genuinely feel they have entered a living creative company — not opened another AI application. If successful, Studio World™ feels less like software and more like visiting the headquarters of the world's most advanced creative organization.

## Ten Foundational Principles (Sprint Charter)

1. **Every AI lives somewhere** — no floating assistants
2. **Departments are destinations** — environments, not pages
3. **The world is alive before the founder arrives** — nothing waits
4. **AI performs visible work** — evidence, not "Done."
5. **Meetings replace prompting** — reviews, not text boxes
6. **Companies generate different headquarters** — architecture identifies the company
7. **The world remembers** — history is physical, never reset
8. **Visitors become collaborators** — the environment is the presentation
9. **Creative work becomes spatial** — walk through ideas, don't read documents
10. **The final destination is world compilation** — from assets to explorable environments
