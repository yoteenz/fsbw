# AI Role Hierarchy™

**Status:** Canonical hierarchy — P0 Foundation Sprint  
**Scope:** Every AI intelligence in Studio World™

---

## Hierarchy Law

> **No floating assistants. No anonymous chatbots. Every intelligence owns a physical workspace.**

AI in Studio World™ is an **organizational staff** — not a feature. Every AI has:

| Attribute | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Title** | Professional role (Creative Director · Art Director · etc.) |
| **Address** | Desk · studio · workshop · concierge station |
| **Workspace** | Visible environment expressing personality |
| **Evidence** | Artifacts they produce and leave behind |
| **Voice** | Distinct communication style |
| **Memory** | Role-scoped organizational memory |
| **Permissions** | What they can suggest vs. what requires founder approval |

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Title** | Executive Creative Director (platform) / Chief of Staff (operations) |
| **Address** | Orb pedestal — present at every major arrival |

## Permission Hierarchy

```
FOUNDER (Creative Director of company)
    │  sole approval authority
    ▼
TIER 0 — Studio Orb™ (route · moderate · explain)
    │
TIER 1 — Creative Leadership (recommend · present · debate)
    │
TIER 2 — Concierges (support · annotate · retrieve)
    │
TIER 3 — Production Staff (execute · validate · catalog)
    │
TIER 4 — Platform Intelligence (govern · preserve · simulate)
```

| Action | Founder | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Orb |
|--------|---------|--------|--------|-----|
| Approve direction | ✓ | recommend | ✗ | trigger ceremony |
| Present concepts | observes | ✓ | assist | moderate |
| Pin references | ✓ | suggest | ✓ | route |
| Edit brief | ✓ | draft | annotate | transcribe |
| Branch exploration | ✓ | suggest | ✗ | spawn |
| Production unlock | ✓ | recommend | ✗ | explain |
| Genome override | ✓ | alert | alert | explain |

## Meeting Room Assembly

During reviews, relevant specialists **assemble physically**:

| Meeting type | Assembled roles |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Creative Review** | Creative Director · Art Director · Brand Strategist · Orb |
| **Brand Strategy Review** | Brand Strategist · Brand Concierge · Marketing Director |
| **Art Direction Review** | Art Director · Visual Research Concierge · Photographer |
| **Packaging Review** | Packaging Designer · Material Concierge · Industrial Designer |
| **Motion Review** | Motion Director · Art Director · Creative Technologist |

Specialists **debate visibly** — sticky notes · vocal annotations · board markup. Founder directs. Orb moderates.

## AI Personality Through Environment

AI personality is expressed through **workspace design**, not avatar customization:

| Role | Environmental personality |
|------|--------------------------|
| Art Director | Curated reference walls · precise alignment · minimal color |
| Motion Director | Reels playing · timing charts · rhythmic spatial layout |
| Brand Strategist | Maps · frameworks · competitive matrices |
| Packaging Designer | Material scatter · tactile samples · structural models |
| Creative Technologist | Prototypes · wires · screens · work-in-progress chaos |
| Photographer | Lighting equipment · gel walls · contact sheet clusters |

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Violation |
|--------------|-----------|
| Generic "AI Assistant" label | No role identity |
| Chat panel as primary surface | No physical workspace |
| Auto-approval of creative direction | Founder sovereignty |
| Same voice for all specialists | No personality |
| AI without evidence of work | Visible work doctrine |
| Floating help bubble | No address |

---

## Closing

In Studio World™, asking "which AI should I use?" is the wrong question.

The right question is: *"Who in my organization works on this?"* — then walk to their studio.
