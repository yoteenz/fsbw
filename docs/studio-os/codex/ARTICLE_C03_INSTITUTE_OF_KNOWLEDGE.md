## Title

The Institute of Knowledge™

---

## Category

**Volume II — Constitution™**

C03 defines the permanent institution responsible for Studio World's official knowledge.

---

## Origin

The earlier **Studio World Press™** concept correctly recognized that Studio World needs official publishing capacity, but it was too narrow.

Studio World is no longer documenting software.

It is documenting a civilization.

Civilizations do not have documentation departments.

They have institutions.

Therefore, **The Institute of Knowledge™** supersedes Studio World Press™ and becomes one of Studio World's foundational institutions.

---

## Purpose

The Institute of Knowledge™ is the constitutional authority responsible for:

- Publishing
- Research
- Knowledge Preservation
- Canon Review
- Constitutional Review
- Version History
- Historical Archives
- Profession Research
- Industry Standards
- Educational Standards
- AI Knowledge Validation
- World History
- Future Research
- Founder's Journal
- Release Records

Everything that becomes official passes through this institution.

---

## Core Philosophy

The Codex stores civilization knowledge.

The Knowledge Core makes it searchable and operational.

Profession Brains reason within professions.

But an institution must decide what becomes official.

**The Institute of Knowledge™ is Studio World's permanent intellectual institution: it preserves wisdom, validates truth, publishes official works, and governs canon for future generations.**

---

## Guiding Principles

1. **Institution over department.** Studio World does not have a documentation team; it has a knowledge institution.
2. **Canon is reviewed, never assumed.** AI-generated knowledge, research, and profession updates require validation.
3. **History is never destroyed.** Superseded knowledge remains historically accessible.
4. **Publishing is constitutional.** Official books, manuals, specs, release notes, and reports are governed artifacts.
5. **Profession truth evolves through review.** Profession Brains™ submit updates; the Institute approves, rejects, or returns them for research.
6. **The Orb cites institutional authority.** Orb answers should consult the Institute through Codex, Knowledge Core, and validated sources.
7. **Every official work becomes a World Graph node.** Publications are not files; they are civilization artifacts.
8. **Standards remain living canon.** Design, engineering, education, simulation, and brand standards are maintained as institutional knowledge.

---

## Institutional Divisions

### Publishing Bureau™

Publishes books, manuals, specifications, official editions, and canonical works.

Responsibilities:

- Publish official editions.
- Maintain publication metadata.
- Coordinate canon-ready releases.
- Ensure every official work points back to source articles.

### Research Bureau™

Conducts profession research and keeps Profession Brains™ synchronized with real-world developments.

Responsibilities:

- Conduct profession research.
- Maintain research queues.
- Propose Profession Brain™ updates.
- Track industry standards and changes.

### Constitution Office™

Maintains constitutional articles, reviews amendments, and preserves historical revisions.

Responsibilities:

- Maintain Studio World's constitutional articles.
- Review amendments.
- Preserve historical revisions.
- Gate Constitution Review™ before implementation.

### Historical Archives™

Stores every previous edition and keeps all knowledge historically accessible.

Responsibilities:

- Preserve previous editions.
- Store superseded canon.
- Maintain historical lineage.
- Never destroy knowledge.

### Knowledge Validation Bureau™

Reviews AI-generated knowledge, verifies sources, and promotes approved knowledge into canon.

Responsibilities:

- Validate AI-generated knowledge.
- Verify sources.
- Approve, reject, or return research.
- Protect canon from unverified updates.

### Standards Bureau™

Maintains design, engineering, education, simulation, and brand standards.

Responsibilities:

- Maintain design standards.
- Maintain engineering standards.
- Maintain education standards.
- Maintain simulation standards.
- Maintain brand standards.

### Publication Office™

Produces official works for different audiences and eras.

Publication types:

- Books
- Whitepapers
- SDK Documentation
- Developer Documentation
- Release Notes
- Profession Guides
- Research Papers
- Civilization Reports
- Founder Letters
- Roadmaps
- Expansion Manuals

### World Chronicle™

Maintains the living history of Studio World.

Responsibilities:

- Record every major event.
- Maintain civilization timeline.
- Connect releases, articles, and institutions.
- Preserve Founder Journal™ entries.

---

## Relationship to the Codex

The Codex becomes the official library operated by The Institute of Knowledge™.

- The Institute governs the Codex.
- The Codex stores civilization knowledge.
- Codex Articles remain the constitutional memory unit.
- Codex Collections remain the library expansion model.
- Publication editions are institutional outputs derived from Codex truth.

---

## Relationship to World Graph™

Every publication automatically becomes a World Graph node.

Institute-managed artifacts relate to:

- systems,
- professions,
- companies,
- Career Worlds,
- citizens,
- constitution,
- history,
- AI,
- publications,
- revisions.

The Institute itself is a World Graph organization node. Its divisions are department nodes. Publications are knowledge-object nodes.

---

## Relationship to the Orb™

The Orb no longer simply answers questions.

The Orb consults The Institute of Knowledge™.

The Institute becomes the authoritative source behind every answer. When the Orb explains architecture, profession truth, release history, standards, or canon, it should cite Institute-governed sources rather than improvising from memory.

---

## Relationship to Profession Brains™

Profession Brains™ continuously submit proposed updates.

The Institute reviews them:

- approved knowledge becomes canon,
- rejected knowledge remains working research,
- uncertain knowledge remains under review,
- superseded knowledge moves into Historical Archives™.

Knowledge is never blindly accepted.

---

## Architectural Decisions

1. **Institute of Knowledge™ supersedes Studio World Press™.**
2. **The Codex is the official library operated by the Institute.**
3. **Knowledge Core™ remains the operational memory/search layer, not the final constitutional authority.**
4. **Every official publication receives World Graph identity.**
5. **Profession Brain updates must pass institutional review before canon promotion.**
6. **AI-generated knowledge must pass Knowledge Validation Bureau™ review.**
7. **Historical Archives™ preserve every superseded edition.**
8. **World Chronicle™ records major events as civilization history.**
9. **The Orb should answer through Institute-governed sources.**
10. **Release Records, Founder Letters, roadmaps, and Civilization Reports are official publications.**
