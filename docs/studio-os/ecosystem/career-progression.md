# Career Progression™

**Build an entire career inside Studio OS**

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Canonical career architecture (docs only)  
**Parent:** [Studio Professionals™](./README.md)

---

## Purpose

Define how Studio Professionals™ grow from **Emerging** to **Legendary** — through reputation that rewards craft · collaboration · and founder success.

---

## Professional Level™ (Universal Ladder)

| Level | Character | Typical signals |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Emerging** | First published work · proving craft | 1+ valid marketplace package |
| **Professional** | Consistent quality · growing following | Satisfaction threshold · portfolio depth |
| **Master** | Recognized body of work | Innovation · retention · collaboration history |
| **Studio Certified™** | Platform-verified excellence | Full certification gate |
| **Legendary** | Cultural impact · mentorship legacy | Featured history · community contribution |

Levels share **names** across professions — thresholds are **profession-specific**.

---

## Reputation Signals

| Signal | Weight | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Quality** | Highest | Certified work · review scores |
| **Innovation** | High | New frameworks · genres |
| **Founder success** | Highest | Outcomes after install/engagement |
| **Collaboration** | High | Professional Graph™ teams |
| **Originality** | High | Not derivative |
| **Community contribution** | Medium | Mentorship · open frameworks |
| **Mentorship** | Medium | Emerging → professional guidance |
| **Long-term impact** | High | Work still active years later |
| **Downloads / sales** | Low | Commerce alone insufficient |

**Not** simply downloads or sales.
