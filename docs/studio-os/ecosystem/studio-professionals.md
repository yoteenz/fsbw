## The Category

Studio OS is creating an entirely new category of **digital professionals**.

These are not marketplace sellers.

They are **Studio Professionals™**.

---

## Who Belongs

| Inside Studio World™ | Role type |
|------------------------|-----------|
| Founder running a company | **Founder™** — inhabitant · decision maker |
| Designer of headquarters | **Studio Architect™** |
| Shaper of brand identity | **Studio Brand Designer™** |
| Builder of production systems | **Studio Director™** |
| Optimizer of live business | **Studio Operator™** |
| Teacher and guide | **Studio Advisor™** |
| Future disciplines | **Future Professions™** |
