# Studio Foundation Models™ & Profession Models™ V1.0 (Milestone 124)

**Route:** `/admin/studio/studio-foundation-models`

## Purpose

**Studio Foundation Models™** and **Profession Models™** define the long-term roadmap for Studio-owned intelligence. Studio OS begins with third-party AI models but should not depend on them forever.

> General models know the world. Studio Models™ know how organizations operate.

## Core philosophy

- Studio OS should not compete with general AI companies — it builds specialized intelligence for organizations
- Begin **model-agnostic** · become **model-resilient** · become **model-independent** where possible
- Third-party models are the bridge. Studio Models™ are the destination

## Studio Foundation Models™

Long-term Studio-owned reasoning models designed around:

Organizational memory · business workflows · professional expertise · department operations · executive reasoning · customer experience · knowledge preservation · legacy

API: `buildFoundationCapabilities()` · `summarizeFoundationCapabilities()`

## Profession Models™

13 specialized Profession Models™ — not generic chatbots. Specialized reasoning layers trained to work with Profession Brain™, Knowledge Fabric™, and Professional Trust Framework™:

Studio Tax™ · Studio Legal Intake™ · Studio Medical Office™ · Studio Hair™ · Studio Construction™ · Studio Contractor™ · Studio Marketing™ · Studio Finance™ · Studio Customer Experience™ · Studio Operations™ · Studio Restaurant™ · Studio Real Estate™ · Studio Education™

API: `buildProfessionModels()` · `summarizeProfessionModels()`

## Training sources

Approved Profession Brain™ data · public professional knowledge · licensed datasets · organization-approved training · Studio Institute™ materials · operating manuals · anonymized patterns (explicit consent only)

**Never train on private organization data without explicit consent.**

API: `buildTrainingSources()` · `summarizeTrainingSources()`

## Hybrid intelligence

Layered intelligence working alongside external models:

Studio Profession Model™ (workflow reasoning) · External Model (language drafting) · Knowledge Fabric™ (org context) · Professional Trust Framework™ (scope validation)

API: `buildHybridLayers()` · `buildDemoHybridRequest()` · `summarizeHybridIntelligence()`

## Enterprise + private deployment

Private Studio Models™ · industry-specific deployments · offline enterprise models · regulated-industry models · customer-owned model instances · secure local inference

API: `buildEnterpriseDeployments()` · `summarizeEnterpriseDeployment()`

## Long-term moat

Every Blueprint · Profession Brain™ · correction · workflow · lesson · simulation · decision · Legacy Vault™ entry improves Studio Intelligence™. The moat is organizational expertise structured for models — not model size.

API: `buildMoatSources()` · `summarizeMoat()`

## Roadmap phases

1. **Model-Agnostic (Now)** — third-party via Model Orchestrator™
2. **Model-Resilient** — AI Swap Engine™ + failover
3. **Model-Independent** — hybrid intelligence reduces external dependency
4. **Studio-Owned Intelligence** — long-term destination
