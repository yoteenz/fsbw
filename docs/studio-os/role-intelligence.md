# Role Intelligence™ (M162)

**Canonical milestone M162 · Shipped nav badge M166 · Volume V — Identity Infrastructure**

## Philosophy

Two people with the same title may perform completely different responsibilities. Role Intelligence™ maps actual work:

- **Responsibilities** — what the role actually does
- **Daily Workflows** — recurring operational patterns
- **Decision Authority** — autonomy, escalation, and approval scope
- **Required Skills** — linked to Skill Graph™
- **Related Profession Brains™** — domain expertise connections
- **Required Documents** — SOPs, templates, policies
- **Required Automations** — workflow instrumentation
- **Performance Metrics** — measurable outcomes
- **Learning Requirements** — academy and certification paths
- **AI Employee counterparts** — human-AI role pairing

## Role Templates

Receptionist · Dispatcher · Estimator · Permit Specialist · Project Manager · Executive Assistant · Marketing Director · Bookkeeper · Attorney · Stylist

## Role Evolution™

As organizations grow, roles evolve. Studio Intelligence™ continuously updates role definitions — tracking emerging, mature, evolving, splitting, and consolidating stages.

## Studio Intelligence™ Examples

- Title/work gap detection when job titles don't match actual responsibilities
- Evolution alerts when roles expand with organizational growth
- Automation coverage gaps across role workflows
- Skill alignment checks against Skill Graph™

## Intelligence Sources

- Identity Graph™ (people & departments)
- Professional Profile™ (career signals)
- Skill Graph™ (required capabilities)
- Profession Brain™ (domain expertise)
- Automation Registry™ (workflow automation)
- Documentation Registry™ (required documents)
- Studio Institute™ (learning requirements)
