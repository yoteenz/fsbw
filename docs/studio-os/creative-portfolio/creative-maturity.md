# Creative Maturity™

**Module:** `studio.creative-portfolio.v1.maturity`  
**Status:** How established the creative ecosystem has become

---

## Principle

> Creative Portfolios **mature** — they never reset. **Creative Maturity™** describes where the company sits on the journey from first generation to influential creative ecosystem.

---

## Maturity Stages

| Stage | Criteria (summary) |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Emerging** | First departments · few certified assets · no marketplace |
| **Developing** | Golden Build™ achieved · Blueprint library forming |
| **Established** | Multi-department consistency · strong reuse · certified systems |
| **Influential** | Marketplace adoption · external blueprint interest |
| **Legacy** | Decades of portfolio · historic HQ · community recognition |

Stages are **guidance labels** — not gamified achievements.
