## Mission

[Creative Equity™](../creative-equity/README.md) measures accumulated creative wealth — but **creative wealth is multidimensional**.

Creative Equity™ should **never** be represented as a single dollar amount alone.

**Creative Portfolio™** evaluates the overall **strength · maturity · influence · efficiency · and long-term value** of a company's creative ecosystem.

---

## One Sentence

**Creative Portfolio™** is the living multidimensional dashboard of a company's creative capital — health scores · consistency · blueprint strength · asset vitality · marketplace influence · Visual DNA™ recognition — that matures forever and never resets.

---

## Relationship to Creative Equity™
