## Founder-facing vocabulary

| Say | Don't say |
|-----|-----------|
| Build Headquarters | Sign up for SaaS |
| Expand Departments | Buy feature add-ons |
| Hire Digital Staff | Enable AI bots |
| Digital Payroll | AI subscription tier |

## Layer details

### Headquarters License
Monthly lease of Studio OS as business operating system. Includes core command surfaces and org boundary.

### Department Packs
Permanent installs — Marketing pack, Creator pack, Medical pack, etc. via Industry Architecture & Expansion Center.

### Digital Workforce
Active concierges and workers on **Digital Payroll** — monthly per role; inactive workers don't bill.

## Growth recommendations

Executive tone via Command Dock — e.g. "Your Operations wing would benefit from a Dispatch Profession Brain" — never marketplace spam.

## Relationship to Knowledge Commerce

Monetization Architecture is **platform billing**. Knowledge Commerce is **expertise products** sold through Expert Marketplace — complementary, not duplicate.
