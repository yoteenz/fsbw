## What Studio Institute is

Studio Institute is the **Learning Operating System (LOS)** for Studio World — not an LMS, not a video library, not another university.

**Studio Institute prepares learners to contribute to real organizations operating inside Studio World.**

Students do not build fictional organizations. They prepare to work with, improve, innovate within, and eventually lead **real businesses**.

## What Studio World is (corrected)

Studio World is the **digital operational world** — digital twin of the real economy. Real organizations establish digital headquarters via Studio OS.

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Studio World** | Real organizations · real operations |
| **Studio Institute** | Education · preparation |
| **Living Systems™** | Simulations **within Institute** — inspired by real orgs (**Planned**) |

## Core educational doctrine

| Doctrine | Statement |
|----------|-----------|
| **Systems before skills** | Understand why before learning how |
| **Real organizations** | Every lesson maps to a **real business** operating in Studio World |
| **Studio World Method™** | Experience first, vocabulary second |
| **Organizations as classrooms** | Real workplaces also teach — with Institute partnership |
| **Contributors to real orgs** | Learners prepare to strengthen real businesses — not "build Studio World" |

## Contribution pipeline

Learn → Practice → Simulation (Living Systems™) → Reflection → Mentorship → Real Organization Project → Internship → Professional Contribution → Leadership → Innovation → Mentorship → Teaching

Contribution means value to **real organizations** — e.g., Frontal Slayer, Bella Hair Studio — not abstract "Studio World contribution."

## Living Systems™ (scope correction)

**Living Systems™ is NOT Studio World.** It exists **within Studio Institute**.

- Governed simulations inspired by real organizations
- Isolated · replayable · attributable · rollback-safe · non-production
- Must never modify production organizational data

Full canon: `docs/studio-world/STUDIO_WORLD_LIVING_SYSTEMS_BIBLE.md` v1.1.0
