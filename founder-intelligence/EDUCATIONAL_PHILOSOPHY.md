## What Studio Institute is

Studio Institute is the **Learning Operating System (LOS)** for Studio World — not an LMS, not a video library, not another university.

**Studio OS operates businesses. Studio Institute develops the people who build, operate, and improve those businesses.**

## Core educational doctrine

| Doctrine | Statement |
|----------|-----------|
| **Systems before skills** | Understand why before learning how |
| **Persistent world learning** | The city is the campus; students travel, not enroll |
| **Studio World Method™** | Experience first, vocabulary second |
| **Organizations as classrooms** | Every workplace is also a learning environment |
| **Contributors, not customers** | Learners are future citizens of Studio World |
