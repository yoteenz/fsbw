## Three-layer model

| Layer | Capsule | Question |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Facts | AI Context | What exists in the repo today? |
| Judgment | Studio DNA | How should decisions *feel* and *look*? |
| Strategy | Founder Intelligence | Why does the company exist? Where is it going? |

## DNA Capsule owns

- Design philosophy and architecture *judgment*
- Quality standards and canon preservation *policy*
- Communication DNA for *output shape* (tone of UI copy, executive IA)
- Decision *patterns* (how founder evaluates work)

## Founder Intelligence owns

- Business model and revenue vision
- Marketplace and Studio Workers economy
- Civilization-scale world-building intent
- Decision *history* with alternatives rejected
- Long-term roadmap and future ideas

## Overlap resolution

- **Creative direction:** DNA = principles and bar; FIC = bible of learned preferences from collaboration
- **Founder profile:** Context = collaboration rules; DNA = design instincts; FIC = leadership and strategy cognition
