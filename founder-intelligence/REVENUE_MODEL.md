## Revenue flow principle

Studio commissions and marketplace take rates must preserve **trust** — experts own IP; platform facilitates discovery and transaction integrity.

## AI Workers

## Long-term financial vision

Knowledge becomes an **asset class** inside the organization — depreciate re-teaching, appreciate captured expertise.
