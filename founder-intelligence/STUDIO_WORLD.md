## What Studio World is

**Studio World is the persistent digital operational world where real organizations establish immersive headquarters powered by Studio OS.**

It is the **digital twin of the real economy** — not a simulated city of fictional businesses.

- Every organization represents a **real business**
- Every headquarters is an **operational workplace** and **customer experience**
- Connected to real customers, employees, data, and commerce through Studio OS

## Why Studio World exists

Software features without geography feel disposable. Studio World gives every **real organization** an **address** — users travel, they don't "open settings."

## Navigation philosophy

- **Atlas** and Knowledge Graph connect places to concepts  
- **Multi-company native** — `/admin/studio/companies/{slug}/...` for org-scoped rooms  
- **Global routes** — command-center, archives, expeditions, mission-control remain platform-level  
- Never hardcode `frontal-slayer` in new company-scoped components — use `useCompanyRoute()`

## Studio Institute relationship

Studio Institute prepares learners to contribute to **real organizations** operating in Studio World. Living Systems™ (within Institute) provides safe practice before production contribution.

## Frontal Slayer on the map

Build-a-Wig storefront + admin is the **host deployment** — first live **real organization** (`frontal-slayer`). Customers experience real commerce; founder experiences Headquarters behind it.

## Future expansion

New **real organizations** establish digital headquarters — infilling geography, not replacing the world map.
