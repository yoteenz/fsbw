## North star

**PRESERVE EXPERTISE. BUILD LEGACY.**

Commerce software treats businesses as accounts. Studio OS treats a business as a **place you inhabit** — headquarters that grows districts, hires digital staff, and remembers every decision.

## The problem we solve

Founder knowledge lives scattered across:

- ChatGPT threads  
- Composer sessions  
- Unwritten instincts  
- Partial documentation  

When a new AI or employee arrives, the founder re-explains everything. **That friction destroys compounding intelligence.**

## The answer

Two halves preserved equally:

1. **Repository intelligence** — AI Context Capsule  
2. **Founder intelligence** — this capsule (+ Studio DNA for judgment)

Together: complete onboarding in minutes, not weeks.

## Ten-year intent

Studio OS should still make sense in 2036 — expansion via **infill districts**, not redesign. Every capability answers: **Where does this live? Why does it matter to the founder's legacy?**

## Emotional north star

Users should feel they **arrived somewhere**, not clicked through menus. Businesses should feel **seen as institutions**, not SKU rows.
