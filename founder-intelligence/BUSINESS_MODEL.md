## Three-layer economy (Studio OS platform)

| Layer | Model | Language |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Headquarters License** | Monthly OS lease | Founders **lease headquarters** |
| **Department Packs** | Permanent expansion | Founders **expand departments** |
| **Digital Workforce** | Monthly digital payroll | Founders **hire digital staff** |

**Forbidden language:** "Subscribe to features" / generic SaaS upsell tone.

## Frontal Slayer (host org) today

- Wig unit commerce, consultations, memberships (Stripe)  
- Premium gates, rewards, booking flows  
- Admin-operated marketing and consult quotes  

## Platform future revenue

- Expert Marketplace™ consumer-facing profiles  
- Knowledge Commerce™ — courses, templates, consultations, memberships  
- Enterprise deployments with org isolation  
- Licensing Profession Brain™ surfaces (with trust tiers)  

## Strategic principle

Revenue follows **preserved expertise** — the more institutional memory compounds, the more monetizable knowledge products become without re-teaching.
